### Acceptance

**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized Hana's experience, and honored her limits (e.g., permission to stop), creating a warm, nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective summaries captured Hana's feelings and process (e.g., the 'painful loop'), deepening understanding rather than merely restating facts.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, sustained focus on preparing for and starting the exam practice with concrete planning (timing, reminders, coping strategies) and resisted drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan with Hana by inviting her input on message wording, timing, and supports, adjusting the plan to her choices and demonstrating genuine joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly emphasized choice and permission, offered options (e.g., 20 vs. 10 minutes, breathing vs. grounding), and honored Hana's preferences without pressure, reinforcing her ownership of the change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Hana's motivations and resources (linking past success to rehearsal) but could further elicit intrinsic motives, values, and richer change-talk to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed decisions as shared by repeatedly asking Hana what she wanted and implementing her suggestions, demonstrating clear power-sharing and respect for her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and amplified desire, reasons, and ability with affirmations and small-step planning, though more targeted prompts for commitment language and values linkage would strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
