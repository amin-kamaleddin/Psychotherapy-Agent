# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalizing slips and creating a safe space for honest disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, timely reflections that capture both content and feeling, validating ambivalence and deepening rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus, systematically narrowing from values to a specific, time‑bound 10‑minute morning yoga routine with actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as joint problem‑solving, soliciting and building on the patient's ideas and repeatedly offering choices to co‑create the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors the patient's right to choose by offering options, affirming decision‑making, and inviting tweaks so the plan belongs to the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits intrinsic motivation by linking concrete actions to the patient's values and asking evocative questions about meaningful outcomes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the interaction as an equal partnership, locating expertise with the patient and co‑constructing plans together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk through evocative questions and reflections, eliciting concrete plans and boosting commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it, pivoting quickly to pragmatic supports, contingency plans, and reframing slips as manageable.
