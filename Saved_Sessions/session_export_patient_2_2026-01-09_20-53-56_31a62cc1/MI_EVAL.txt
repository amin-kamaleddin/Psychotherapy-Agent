# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard by validating constraints and normalizing incremental change, avoiding shame or judgment throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring practical constraints and feelings and inviting the patient's lived experience to deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, sustained focus on evening smoking and steered questions and reflections toward a specific, measurable plan that linked to the patient's goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducted the session as a partnership, soliciting preferences and shaping the plan around the patient's ideas using invitational language that kept control with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's right to choose pace and approach, offering options and avoiding pressure while supporting ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from the patient's own values and desired outcomes through evocative questions that elicited intrinsic reasons for change and concrete next steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative interaction with co-created solutions; the clinician invited the patient's ideas and treated her as the expert on what would work in her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited, reflected, and amplified change talk, helping the patient specify concrete steps and strengthening commitment language around evening reduction.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and did not press on alcohol, but could more actively use double-sided reflections, elicit downsides of the status quo, and more explicitly validate ambivalence.
