# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating the patient's concerns and framing missed sessions as data rather than failure. Avoided blame and offered gentle suggestions throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and reflective listening by restating values and worries and acknowledging what grounds the patient. Reflections were often summary- or solution-focused; deeper, rolling reflections could further access emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on aligning behavior with the value of health via a specific, doable yoga practice. Each exchange moved the plan toward concrete steps, reminders, and a check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt co-constructed: the clinician invited and accepted the patient's edits on wording, timing, and plans and asked permission before offering materials. Decisions were made jointly rather than prescribed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the patient's choice and avoided pressuring language, offering options and affirming ownership of the plan. Encouraged treating results as information and allowed the patient to select reminders and tweaks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation by linking values to a concrete experiment and asking the patient to choose a focus, drawing on the patient's own reasons. Could use more strategic open questions to elicit desire, reasons, ability, and confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invited the patient to shape the plan and framed recommendations as experiments and choices, demonstrating genuine power-sharing. The clinician adjusted plans based on patient input and scheduled collaborative check-ins.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk with a specific, doable plan and concrete commitments (reminders, check-in). Could more actively amplify and reflect the patient's own change language to strengthen desire and confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalized lapses and reframed them as data while offering small backup strategies to reduce resistance. Using more double-sided reflections and selectively reinforcing change-favoring statements could further diminish sustain talk.
