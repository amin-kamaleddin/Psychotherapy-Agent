# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence throughout, affirming values and prior successes without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, with timely reflections that capture both content and feeling.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing drinking and progressively narrows discussion toward specific, testable steps that the patient accepts.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner by soliciting, building on, and integrating the patient's ideas into a co-created plan rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Affirms the patient's ownership of choices, offers options instead of pressure, and normalizes slips to preserve autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's motivations, strengths, and past successes (e.g., quitting smoking) to build internal motivation and link to concrete strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative, repeatedly asking what would work best and co-creating a concrete, realistic plan with the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies the patient's change language, inviting specific, realistic steps and reinforcing commitment rather than supplying reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids challenging sustain talk and normalizes slips appropriately, but could be more strategic by using double-sided reflections or selectively highlighting discrepancy to soften minimizations.
