# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, normalizing the client's feelings and using his name to validate concerns. No blaming or moralizing language; acceptance is individualized and steady throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening that captures both content and emotion and links concerns to concrete work/home consequences. Reflections match tone and ambivalence with no major missed empathic opportunities.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on managing anger and protecting time, moving logically from problem to options to a concrete, time-bound trial. Questions and suggestions consistently tie back to actionable behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is jointly constructed: the clinician offers options, elicits preferences, and co-writes the script with the patient. Inclusive language and shared decision-making show strong teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice and avoids pressure, validating skepticism while inviting voluntary trial actions. Accepts a modest, single-attempt plan and respects the patient's decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out motivations, strengths, and a concrete trial, producing meaningful change talk. Missed opportunities to deepen and consolidate motivation (e.g., scaling confidence or eliciting the patient's own summary of reasons).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative stance with shared power—clinician proposes options and the patient selects and refines them. The clinician frames the patient as the source of practical solutions and defers to his preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk and secures a behavioral commitment to try the script once. Could more strongly amplify and consolidate change talk (e.g., ask confidence scales or reflect the client's own arguments for change).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and reframes toward workable options, avoiding escalation of defensiveness. Would benefit from more explicit double-sided reflections or summaries to further reduce resistance and resolve ambivalence.
