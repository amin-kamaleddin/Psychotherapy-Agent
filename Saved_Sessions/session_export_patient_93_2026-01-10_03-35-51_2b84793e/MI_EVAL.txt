# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and avoided judgment. They respected Margaret's preferences and tailored interventions to her goals.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective statements that validated ambivalence and worries. Could use deeper, more complex reflections to capture underlying feelings and strengthen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol-related harm and returned to concrete behavioral steps throughout. Balanced exploration with specific next actions and planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative, co-creating a tailored tracker and soliciting Margaret's format and content preferences. The clinician positioned themselves and the patient as partners solving the problem.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Margaret's sense of choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and honoring her decision not to quit. Decisions about format, alcohol-free day, and limits were left to her.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited small commitments and some motivational language but relied heavily on practical instruction and tool-building. Could further draw out intrinsic motives and values with more open, evocative questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power-sharing and positioned Margaret as the expert, repeatedly asking for her input and tailoring the plan accordingly. The clinician responded to preferences and co-created the tracker iteratively.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk that produced concrete commitments (tracking, alcohol-free day, one-glass target). Could more strategically amplify reasons, abilities, and confidence with targeted questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided overtly reinforcing sustain talk but mainly pivoted to practical strategies rather than directly exploring or reframing it. Missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and eliciting the costs of continuing current drinking.
