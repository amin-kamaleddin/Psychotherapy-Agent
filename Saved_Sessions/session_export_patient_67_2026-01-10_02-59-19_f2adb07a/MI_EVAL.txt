# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—open, nonjudgmental language and normalizing statements throughout the encounter, avoiding labels or shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening with accurate reflections of behavior and some feelings, though reflections sometimes stay at the surface rather than drawing out deeper emotions (e.g., embarrassment, anxiety).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on alcohol use and links questions/summaries to Kenji’s priorities (work, daily functioning), steering to concrete next steps and experiments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partner—solicits Kenji’s solutions, offers choices, and integrates his preferences into a jointly created, practical plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering options, asking permission, emphasizing no pressure, and checking which methods feel easiest for the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation through values exploration and a confidence ruler and asks about barriers, but could more actively amplify personal reasons and past successes to strengthen change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine power-sharing—repeatedly invites input, co-creates the plan (timing, reminders, template), and respects Kenji’s choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports some change talk via value-linked questions and low-risk experiments, yet misses opportunities to reflect/amplify statements linking drinking to threats (e.g., job) and to prompt stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and reframes toward nonthreatening experiments which reduces sustain talk, but could more often use double-sided reflections to acknowledge sustain talk while nudging toward change.
