### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence without judgment across turns, creating a supportive, non‑shaming tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections that capture content and feeling (e.g., tiredness, 'least scary'), demonstrating strong perspective‑taking and keeping Kenji engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on reducing drinking with concrete, measurable goals and practical supports, maintaining forward‑moving guidance toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as joint exploration by soliciting Kenji's input and asking him to choose steps, so decisions are co‑constructed rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasizes Kenji's choice and control, offers options without pressure, and invites him to select what feels most doable.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Draws out Kenji's reasons and personally meaningful outcomes and elicits implementation ideas, but could probe deeper into values and use scaling to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and plans, but could do more selective amplification, summarizing, and commitment/scaling questions to solidify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
