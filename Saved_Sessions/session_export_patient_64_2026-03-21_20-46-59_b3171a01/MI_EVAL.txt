# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, explicitly respected the patient’s agency (e.g., “You’re in charge of the pace”) and repeatedly validated mixed feelings.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening that captured both cognitive and emotional concerns (e.g., reflections linking needle aversion and family worries) and normalized anxiety about medical conversations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on the flu vaccine and moved toward concrete next steps (asking the doctor, texting Grandma, timing, and role-play).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** True co-construction of language and plans, invited choices (text vs. role-play), responded to requests, and provided brief feedback rather than taking over.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and avoided pressure by offering options, asking permission before giving information, and reinforcing the patient’s ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations and concrete plans (scaling, reasons, intended actions) but at times moved into offering information rather than deepening the patient’s internal values and commitment; could probe meaning and confidence more.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance with frequent invitations, co-created scripts and role-plays, and allowed the patient’s preferences to shape the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk using scaling and action planning, but did not consistently amplify or follow up to deepen commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used neutral-to-redirecting reflections and permission-based info to defuse sustain talk, though more double-sided reflections and selective amplification could further soften resistance.
