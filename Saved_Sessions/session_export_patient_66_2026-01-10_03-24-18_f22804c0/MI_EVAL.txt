### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, using validating statements and warmth throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, high-quality empathic listening with reflections that capture emotion, function of smoking, and the patient's guarded but responsive stance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior and a specific behavioral experiment, tying questions and reflections to concrete planning without pressuring.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages the patient as a partner by soliciting preferences, offering options, and co-creating a practical plan (buddy, gum, reminder, scripted text).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforces choice and removes all‑or‑nothing pressure, offering alternatives and emphasizing the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Successfully elicits change talk and links to values, but could probe deeper into personal reasons and use scaling/confidence questions to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a collaborative exploration, inviting the patient's input at every step and sharing decision-making power in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces tentative change talk and a concrete experiment, though it stops short of systematically probing importance/confidence or eliciting stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
