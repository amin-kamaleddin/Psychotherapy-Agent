# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm (e.g., opener, normalizing reflections, supportive reinforcement), conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening that capture both content and affect, validating ambivalence and mirroring the patient's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with interventions (scaling, planning, supports) directly tied to the target behavior and a logical progression to action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuine two-way partnership: clinician invites co-creation, checks preferences, and builds the plan from the patient's ideas using inclusive language.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers choices, frames suggestions as optional, and avoids pressuring language, preserving the patient's sense of ownership over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own motives, resources, and plans through evocative questions about benefits, past success, and confidence, and builds on the patient's statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as the expert on their life, offering options and co-constructing decisions (time, supports, message) rather than directing or prescribing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically elicits and amplifies change talk with evocative questions, scaling, and requests for specific commitments, moving the patient toward concrete plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalizes ambivalence and redirects toward small steps and supports, though could more actively soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections or brief exploration of costs of staying the same.
