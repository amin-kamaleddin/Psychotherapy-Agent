# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validated Ahmed’s limits and normalized slips. Demonstrated clear unconditional positive regard throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries that showed understanding of Ahmed’s perspective and guarded tone. Relied more on summary-level reflections and missed deeper affective or complex reflections to explore emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on mini‑cigar use and oral health, moving the conversation toward a concrete, time-limited 2‑week experiment. The plan and follow-up were behaviorally specific and targeted.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a true collaborative exchange by soliciting preferences, incorporating Ahmed’s wording, and co‑creating the plan. Language and behavior consistently projected partnership rather than clinician-driven instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering options, asking permission, and affirming Ahmed’s ownership of choices and the plan. Emphasized that slips are informational and respected Ahmed’s decision-making.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out internal motivators (wife’s concern, evidence, simple plan) using scaling and targeted questions. Could have expanded on the personal meaning of those motivators and probed confidence to amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly conveyed partnership by inviting choices, checking wording, and incorporating Ahmed’s tweaks into the plan and check‑in message. Decision‑making and planning were genuinely co‑constructed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change language through scaling and prompts that produced explicit change talk. Could have amplified and reflected change statements more and probed ability/confidence to move toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing or confronting minimization and sought permission before offering information, responding neutrally to sustain talk. Would benefit from more double‑sided reflections to gently highlight ambivalence without taking an adversarial stance.
