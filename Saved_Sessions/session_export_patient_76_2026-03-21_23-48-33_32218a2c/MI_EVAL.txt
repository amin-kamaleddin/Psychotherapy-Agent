# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Liora's experience, removing blame and shame. Tone is nonjudgmental and supportive throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate, nuanced reflective listening including somatic language (e.g., 'heavy pressure,' 'chest tightness'), which fosters containment and understanding. Reflections closely mirror the client's expressed feelings and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, steady focus on reducing cutting and developing alternative coping, repeatedly returning to coping options and brief experiments. Uses summaries and focused questions to move from exploration to a specific behavioral plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in an equal-partner exploration, soliciting and validating the client's ideas and using them to construct the plan. Checks feasibility and co-creates stepwise options and backup plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice and frames actions as voluntary, avoiding pressure. Reinforces that the plan is the client's decision and reversible, preserving agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits motivation from Liora's own experiences and values with questions that draw out intrinsic reasons for change. Prompts lead to client-generated solutions and a realistic experiment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the interaction as collaborative, inviting the client's expertise and jointly deciding on steps. Decisions are co-constructed rather than imposed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk by asking about small, doable strategies and prior successes, and by amplifying strengths. Framing the plan as an experiment encouraged concrete change language (ice + timer).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without reinforcing it, using double-sided reflections and normalization to reduce defensiveness. Reframes ambivalence toward exploring coping options rather than justifying the behavior.
