# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalized options, and praised effort rather than condemning behavior.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reflections and summaries were accurate and validated Brandon's perspective and risks, but could use more complex/amplified reflections to deepen emotional content and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on alcohol use and related risks throughout, moving logically from assessment to concrete, measurable harm‑reduction goals with minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as genuine collaboration—clinician solicited input at each step, tailored suggestions to Brandon's responses, and co‑constructed the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Brandon's choice and control, offered options rather than prescriptions, and respected his stated readiness and preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited readiness, doable options, and triggers which brought out some internal reasons and strategies, but missed deeper evocative questions about values or discrepancy to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly partnered with the client by asking which options felt doable, deferring to his expertise, and inviting him to decide logistics and start dates.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some explicit change language and commitments (tracking, alternating with water) but did not consistently amplify or explore desire, reasons, ability, or commitment before moving to planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed toward harm‑reduction effectively, though could increase use of double‑sided reflections and gently rephrase minimizations to invite elaboration.
