# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, affirmed progress without judgment, and validated the client's values and constraints throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking about pragmatic concerns, but did not consistently probe or reflect deeper emotions like fatigue, shame, or discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on reducing in‑cab smoking with a logical progression from values to options to a specific plan, while respecting the client's pace.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem‑solving and incorporated the client's choices, though the clinician sometimes took a more active leadership by offering multiple specific solutions rather than eliciting the client's ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and agency, offered options and framed plans as experiments without pressuring, and affirmed that decisions were the client's to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked intrinsic motives and concrete intentions (no smoking in cab, backups), but could further amplify emotional salience and assess importance/confidence to strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative tone and invitations to co‑construct a plan were clear, but greater solicitation of the client's own brainstorming before offering menus of solutions would enhance equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced specific change talk and planning that produced actionable commitments, though missed opportunities to deepen desire/ability/reasons/need via scaling or elaboration.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not reinforce sustain talk; consistently acknowledged barriers and redirected toward problem‑solving and small, realistic steps.
