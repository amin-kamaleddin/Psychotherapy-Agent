# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Milan's choices early and repeatedly, with no moralizing or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and accurate reflections capture motives like belonging and defensiveness, but reflections are more content- and solution-focused than feeling-laden or ambivalence-exploring.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on alcohol use at social gatherings and consistently steers toward concrete, proximal steps (being the driver, sending a text) while remaining client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is joint problem-solving with open questions, choices, and co-creation of messages; Milan's preferences drive the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly affirms Milan's choices, avoids pressuring change, and frames plans as under his control while letting him select and refine strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out Milan's motivations and elicits commitment language for practical strategies, but could more often evoke deeper values and use scaling or targeted prompts to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Milan as the expert, solicits his input, offers small choices, and co-constructs the plan—demonstrating a strong collaborative partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits pragmatic, actionable change talk and planning (driver, texts), yet does not probe importance/confidence or longer-term reasons to broaden and deepen change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and reframes sustain talk toward protective strategies, though more double-sided reflections acknowledging sustain reasons while gently tipping toward change would strengthen this skill.
