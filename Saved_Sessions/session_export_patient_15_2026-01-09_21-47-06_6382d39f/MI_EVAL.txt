# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's experience, avoiding blame or confrontation. Frequent affirmations and respectful language support a strong sense of acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, validating ambivalence and restating concerns accurately. Could deepen by probing emotions or the personal meaning behind choices to reach a top rating.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on blood pressure monitoring and the possible alcohol–BP link with concrete next steps. Every turn returned to actionable plans and measures of success (e.g., one-week baseline).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative with frequent invitations for the patient's ideas and co-constructed plans (alarm time, app use, cuff placement). Language emphasizes working together and shared problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly supports choice and control by offering options and asking which the patient would try, without pressuring. The clinician respects the patient's preference for a small, manageable start.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient's motivations (wanting a baseline, avoiding meds) and elicits reasons for change. Could further probe values, use importance/confidence scaling, and amplify past successes to strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as a collaborator and elicits his preferences repeatedly, resulting in plans that originate from the patient. Power-sharing language and choices reflect a strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change language with realistic, small-step options and reflections that strengthen intentions. Missed opportunities remain to amplify and elaborate reasons and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and normalizes reluctance while reframing toward manageable steps. Could use more strategic moves (double-sided or amplified reflections, gentler redirection of minimization) to further soften sustain talk.
