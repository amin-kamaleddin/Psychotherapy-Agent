# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, praising steps and normalizing Amina's feelings throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking with validations and summaries, but several reflections stayed at content level rather than deeper emotional or value-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on nocturnal phone use and advanced concrete planning, measurable outcomes, and a scheduled check-in while remaining client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged Amina as a partner by soliciting preferences, asking permission before offering help, and allowing her ideas to shape message wording and plan details.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice by offering options, inviting tweaks, and affirming that the plan was Amina's decision, reinforcing her sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations, past successes, and scaling responses, but could have used more evocative, value-oriented questions to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative stance with shared decision-making, co-created plans, and regular checks for agreement on next steps and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk through scaling and past-strategy exploration and affirmed incremental gains, though missed opportunities to amplify and summarize change language more strategically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Addressed sustain talk pragmatically (e.g., telling friends, Do Not Disturb) but used few MI-specific techniques like double-sided reflections or reflective redirection to resolve ambivalence.
