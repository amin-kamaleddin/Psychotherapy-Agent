# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Lucía's feelings and choices without pressure. Avoided admonishment and normalized her struggles throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely, and specific reflections that matched Lucía's concerns (timing, stress level, triggers) and encouraged further disclosure. Empathic summaries linked her experience to ambivalence effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear and appropriate focus on smoking and related contexts, repeatedly steering toward actionable elements like evening triggers and a concrete experiment. Maintained goal-directed conversation without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with joint option-generating and mutual decision-making about the plan and follow-up. Clinician frequently checked comfort and preferences, making decisions mutual rather than clinician-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly honored Lucía's preferences and emphasized freedom of choice, accepting low confidence without disapproval. Supported her control over topics (no push for meds) and the style of check-in.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited relevant motivations and explored barriers (desire to be there for daughter, smoking as calming) and used them to generate a small experiment. Missed some opportunities to deepen intrinsic motivation by inviting Lucía to articulate her own reasons more fully.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned Lucía as an equal partner, inviting her expertise and choice in setting the agenda and selecting strategies. Co-created a concrete, feasible plan and respected her decisions throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited some change talk and helped develop a concrete short-term plan, including confidence ratings and choices. Could have more actively amplified and explored brief change statements and the meaning of her values to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used rolling-with-resistance and reflections to avoid confrontation and reduce sustain talk impact, offering choices instead of pushing. Could have more often used double-sided reflections to explicitly highlight ambivalence and further soften sustain talk.
