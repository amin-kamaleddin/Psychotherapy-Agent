# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental language and normalization that created a safe foundation for change.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong empathic listening and accurate reflections of ambivalence and constraints, though deeper affect-focused or more complex reflections could have been used to draw out emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on increasing physical activity with each question and suggestion tied to that target and concrete planning maintained momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative—options were proposed but the patient was invited to choose and refine plans, with frequent summaries and checks for understanding.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly affirmed choice, offered multiple doable options, and avoided pressuring language, supporting the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician drew motivation from the patient by eliciting values, self-generated solutions, and using scaling/amplifying questions to surface internal reasons and resources.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician treated the patient as the expert and co-created the plan, asking preference questions and inviting input on specifics and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skilled elicitation and reinforcement of change talk through targeted questions, concrete planning, confidence scaling, and celebrating incremental gains.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was generally redirected with normalization and backups, but the clinician missed some opportunities for double-sided reflections and more explicit validation before pivoting.
