# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support with affirmations and neutral safety checks. Occasional deeper normalization of ambivalence could add warmth for more complex clients.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a good empathic stance with validations and an open invitation to share, but frequently moved quickly into advice. More extended reflective paraphrasing of ambivalence and affect would strengthen higher-level empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused agenda on early alcohol use and a discrete goal (no alcohol before practice/games). Kept the session tightly linked to action planning with minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was strongly collaborative, co-constructing goals, role-plays, and text drafting while checking Lukas' preferences. Frequent checks and shared decision-making reflected an equal-partner approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by offering clear choices and repeatedly framing decisions as Lukas' to make. Honored his readiness to choose harm-reduction and reinforced ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change-oriented responses but relied heavily on clinician-provided options and scripts rather than drawing out Lukas' internal motivations. Should more consistently ask about values, importance, and confidence and then reflect and amplify those statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance and invited Lukas into decision-making with choices and rehearsal, but offered many specific suggestions that felt somewhat expert-driven. Eliciting Lukas' own ideas before proposing strategies would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Promoted and shaped change talk via goal-setting and offering pathways, which led to concrete change statements. Could more consistently elicit Lukas' own reasons, importance, and confidence language and amplify his change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward safety and harm-reduction strategies while rehearsing refusals. Could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge both pulls and guide toward change.
