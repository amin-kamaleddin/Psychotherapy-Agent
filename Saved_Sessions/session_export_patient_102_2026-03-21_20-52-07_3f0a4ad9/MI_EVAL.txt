# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental stance, offering choices and invitations rather than directives. This reduced threat and respected Milan’s perspective throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured ambivalence, motives, and context. Could deepen empathy by using more affect‑laden or value-focused reflections to explore underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on weekend drinking and concrete risk‑reduction strategies, using scaling and moving to implementation planning. Direction was purposeful without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem‑solving with frequent invitations for Milan’s input and language tailored to his style. The plan was co‑created and decisions derived from Milan’s preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Milan’s choice and control by asking permission, offering options, and framing experiments as voluntary. Avoided coercive language and reinforced ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Milan’s own reasons for change through open questions, pros/cons, and scaling, then built on his statements to develop concrete strategies. Clinician amplified and operationalized his change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician positioned themselves as a collaborator, inviting exploration and co‑creating strategies that fit Milan’s context. This fostered shared decision‑making and client expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly elicited and reinforced change talk via pros/cons discussion, importance/confidence scaling, and requests for implementation details. Followed up on commitment language to operationalize behavior change.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Skillfully avoided confronting sustain talk and redirected toward harm‑reduction and small experiments. Could improve by using more double‑sided reflections and strategic summaries to explicitly link ambivalence and elicit discrepancy.
