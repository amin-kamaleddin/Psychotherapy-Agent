# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes resistance with multiple affirmations and strengths-based statements throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that validate practical constraints, though reflections are somewhat formulaic and miss deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on morning blood sugar checks and moves the conversation toward concrete, actionable first steps throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative, routinely soliciting patient preferences and framing problem-solving as a joint process while following the patient's cues.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms the patient's freedom to choose, offers multiple options, and avoids pressuring — emphasizing short trials and patient control over continuation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits patient-generated solutions (e.g., meter-by-coffee, spouse nudge) but could do more to deepen intrinsic motivation by amplifying change talk and exploring importance/confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positions the interaction as collaborative and invites input, though the clinician often guides decisions rather than fully transferring agenda-setting to the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits tentative change talk and reinforces small steps, but does not consistently amplify desire/ability/reasons/confidence or elicit an explicit commitment or scaling of confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses empathetic reframing to redirect to small steps, though could more often use double-sided reflections or labeling to help resolve ambivalence.
