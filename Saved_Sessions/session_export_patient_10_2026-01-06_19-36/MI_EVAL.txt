# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, validated strengths and normalized difficulties; affirmed resilience and avoided judgment throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, attuned reflections that captured both content and feeling and repeatedly mirrored the client's language, building strong rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on attending the job interview by resolving childcare barriers and moved logically from eliciting motivation to practical planning without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was mutual and egalitarian, offering tools as support rather than prescriptions and frequently checking in while incorporating Amina's choices into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Amina's choice and control with open invitations and offers of help only when invited, respecting her pace and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from Amina's own reasons and language, amplified her confidence, and elicited concrete implementation intentions like calls, texts, and backups.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative, privileged Amina's expertise in choosing wording and steps, and co-created decisions throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and strengthened change talk with open questions, affirmations, probing consequences, and concrete planning to convert motivation into action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk without reinforcing it and reframed concerns into planning. Could have more explicitly used double-sided reflections and explored the cost of inaction to further shift ambivalence.
