### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes Anya's concerns, offering affirmations and avoiding blame. Compliments planning and willingness to try, demonstrating warmth and respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate reflections and summaries that capture Anya's stress, sleep concerns, and nervousness about asking for help. Validates feelings and shows strong perspective-taking throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on managing stress and grades and ties all interventions to that goal. Moves the session toward a concrete one-week plan, accountability, and a scheduled follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invites Anya's input at each step and immediately adapts the plan and wording to her requests. Co-creates the plan, timeline, and follow-up, reflecting genuine two-way interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offers multiple options, asks permission before drafting messages or plans, and defers to Anya's choices. Reinforces ownership by letting her select focus, start date, and accountability methods.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations, barriers, and boosts (confidence ruler, concrete supports), leading to practical commitments. Could further deepen intrinsic motives and link short-term tactics to longer-term values while minimizing directive advice.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as collaborative and shares power with the client by checking preferences and co-constructing the plan. Adapts to requests (casual wording, allowing calls) and supports shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces several pieces of change talk (choices, confidence ruler, concrete boosts) and secures commitment language. Could more systematically reflect and amplify spontaneous change statements to deepen internal reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** There is minimal sustain talk and the clinician avoids amplifying it by using neutral informational language and normalization. When concerns about sticking to the plan arise, the clinician supports problem-solving rather than confronting.
