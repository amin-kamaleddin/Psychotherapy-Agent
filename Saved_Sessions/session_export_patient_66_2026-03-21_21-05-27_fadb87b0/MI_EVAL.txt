# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician communicates unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental stance, honoring the patient’s preferences and boundary conditions. Options are offered in a way that preserves choice and respect throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking, with practical follow-ups that show understanding of workplace frustrations. Could deepen empathy by exploring the emotional/ritual aspects of smoking (comfort, hand‑to‑mouth, social meaning).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior with a pragmatic, time-limited goal (one‑week gum trial) and concrete follow-up steps. Balances exploration and forward movement effectively to translate ambivalence into action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is joint problem‑solving with the patient’s ideas shaping the plan, including choices about dosing, wording of reminders, and practical accommodations. Concrete offers (samples, demo, pharmacist check) reinforce equitable partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering multiple options, asking for the patient’s preferences, and agreeing to a voluntary trial rather than pushing a quit mandate. The clinician’s language and follow‑up respect the patient’s control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient’s solutions and plan (gum trial, conditions, check‑ins) and uses questions that draw out commitment to an experiment. Missed opportunities to probe deeper for personal reasons, values, and to amplify desire/reasons/ability language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—asks for input on what will work, adapts the plan to patient requests, and shares decision‑making power. The patient’s preferences consistently guide the specifics of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change‑oriented language, secures a specific plan, and asks planning questions that promote follow‑through. Could further amplify intrinsic motivations and elicit stronger confidence and commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by offering practical alternatives and preserving choice, which reduces defensiveness. Could strengthen technique with double‑sided reflections and strategic summaries to further shift ambivalence toward change.
