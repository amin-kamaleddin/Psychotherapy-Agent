# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating difficulties of quitting while parenting/working and avoiding shaming language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking by mirroring language and affect and tying reflections to the client's context.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on smoking cessation and moved logically from reasons for change to a specific, small, actionable plan without confrontation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative interaction: solicited the patient's proposals, integrated them into the plan, and checked back for acceptability throughout the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering options, asking which coping idea felt most doable, and confirming the patient's chosen plan without pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivations, values, and strengths (daughter, breathing, pride), but could more systematically elicit confidence and commitment (e.g., scaling, rehearsals).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt co‑constructed with genuine power-sharing: early invitations for the patient's ideas, incorporation of those ideas, and avoidance of an expert stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited and amplified change talk and concrete steps, but missed chances to deepen and sequence it toward firmer commitment (no scaling or commitment language).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and did not reinforce sustain talk and offered backup options, but relied mainly on reflections rather than double‑sided reflections or reframing to shift momentum.
