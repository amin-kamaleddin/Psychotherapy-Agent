### Empathy

### Direction

### Partnership

#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits concrete commitments (e.g., two alcohol-free nights) and reinforces steps, yet misses opportunities to deepen change talk by asking more about reasons, values, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
