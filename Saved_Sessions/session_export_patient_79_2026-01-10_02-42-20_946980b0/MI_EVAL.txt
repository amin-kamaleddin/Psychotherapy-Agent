# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating the patient’s experience and normalizing slips while avoiding blame or lectures.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used strong reflective listening and matched the patient’s tone, accurately summarizing burden and preferences, but could have probed one level deeper into underlying emotions and feelings of being misunderstood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on daily controller inhaler use and moved from problem identification to a specific, measurable plan tied to the patient’s goals of simplicity.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as an equal partner by iteratively co-creating alarm labels, backup plans, and a tracker with the patient, shaping decisions around the patient’s preferences and constraints.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering options, asking permission before drafting labels/scripts, and respecting the patient’s boundaries and choices.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Oriented the session to elicit the patient’s own motivations and capabilities, using open invitations, confidence ratings, and a two-week commitment to draw out intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong shared decision-making with frequent invitations to choose and co-creation of concrete steps, treating the patient as the expert on their life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk by having the patient choose a specific small step, developing concrete plans, and affirming commitment language throughout.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk to practical solutions, but missed opportunities for explicit double-sided reflections to more actively soften resistance and highlight ambivalence.
