### Empathy

**Comments:** Used good reflective listening and validated practical worries and ambivalence, but missed opportunities to explore deeper affect and identity-related concerns behind tentative language.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited specific change talk through options, confidence scaling, and action prompts that produced concrete commitments, though reflections could more strongly amplify and heighten discrepancy to build commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
