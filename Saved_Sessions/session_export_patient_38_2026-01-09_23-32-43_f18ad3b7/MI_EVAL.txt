# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized Elena’s feelings, and maintained a warm, nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking but reflections were often brief and repetitive, with missed opportunities to explore deeper emotional nuance (fear, embarrassment, avoidance).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on contraception and immediate risk reduction, efficiently moving from assessment to concrete action planning without getting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly co-constructed—clinician invited preferences, provided practical tools (script, role-play), and made decisions jointly with Elena.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored Elena’s choices, offered multiple options, and refrained from pressuring a decision while supporting optional immediate steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation through planning and action steps but underused techniques to draw out Elena’s internal reasons, values, or confidence (e.g., evocative questions, scaling).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as collaborative, asked permission before offering tools, co-created the phone script, and repeatedly checked Elena’s comfort with next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supported preparatory actions and practical commitments (calling clinic, emergency contraception) but did not systematically deepen Elena’s internal motivations or elicit stronger change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and gently redirected sustain talk toward risk-reduction steps, though could have used more double-sided or amplified reflections to resolve ambivalence more actively.
