### Acceptance

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Elena's right to choose and avoided pressuring language, offering assistance only when she wanted it and honoring her readiness.

### Evocation

### Partnership

**Comments:** Actively invited Elena's input, asked permission before offering information, and shared power in planning, fostering a genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3

### Softening Sustain Talk
