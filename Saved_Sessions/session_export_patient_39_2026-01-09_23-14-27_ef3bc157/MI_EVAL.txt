# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing setbacks and affirming efforts, which conveyed strong unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician used accurate reflections and validations that showed understanding of Elias’s feelings, though some opportunities to deepen empathic reflection of emotional meaning (e.g., the 'very, very beautiful' experience) were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conversation stayed clearly focused on increasing gentle physical activity with concrete action planning, scheduling, and follow-up, keeping momentum on the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session was framed as joint problem-solving with choices invited, but the clinician then offered many clinician-led strategies which slightly reduced parity in co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly emphasized Elias’s choice and control, offered options and permissions (e.g., stop if pain), and reinforced his agency in deciding how to proceed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some motivation was elicited (specific commitments and noticing change), but the clinician relied more on suggestions than on drawing out Elias’s intrinsic motives, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician invited input on activities, timing, and reminders fostering partnership, yet provided detailed plans and solutions that shifted some expertise toward the clinician rather than co-creating from Elias’s ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician elicited concrete commitments and reinforced positives, but did not consistently probe for Elias’s own reasons, values, or deeper motivations to strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician acknowledged and normalized barriers, reframed goals toward gentleness, and pivoted to tiny steps which reduced sustain talk, though stronger double-sided reflections could further resolve ambivalence.
