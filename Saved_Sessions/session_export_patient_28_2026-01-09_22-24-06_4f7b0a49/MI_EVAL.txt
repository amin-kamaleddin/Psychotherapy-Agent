# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support through validation and affirmations, with no blaming language. Maintained a warm, respectful tone and thanked the client for clarity while affirming effort and past success.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, normalizing the client's struggles. Reflections were sometimes surface-level and the clinician moved quickly to solutions, missing opportunities for deeper emotional exploration of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on returning to regular exercise and translated ambivalence into concrete, actionable steps. Used little topic drift and tied empathic statements back to planning and follow-through.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—solicited preferences at multiple decision points, provided options, and integrated the client's selections into the plan. Treated the client as an equal partner whose input shaped the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the client's sense of choice and control by offering multiple options and preserving ownership of decisions. Avoided pressure and emphasized voluntary small steps and fallbacks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made several attempts to draw out motivation (asking about routines and barriers) but leaned toward offering solutions and structured advice. Missed openings to elicit deeper personal reasons, values, or confidence (e.g., importance/confidence scaling).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invited the client's input and checked preferences before offering resources, co-creating the plan based on those choices. Maintained a collaborative tone that kept decision-making shared.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with concrete evoking questions and by leveraging past success, prompting specific commitments. Could have more frequently amplified the client's change language and explored personal reasons or confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively softened sustain talk by normalizing barriers and offering acceptable fallbacks rather than confronting or arguing. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections that explicitly acknowledge both sides of ambivalence before pivoting to change talk.
