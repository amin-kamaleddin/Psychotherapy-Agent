# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed nonjudgmental acceptance and normalized Malik's feelings, validating his frustration and removing blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Reflections and summaries accurately captured Malik's ambivalence and affect, helping him feel heard and keeping him engaged throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session stayed practically focused on a written agreement to reduce conflict, but it drifted from the clinical target of cannabis use and lacked inquiry into frequency, contexts, and risks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The psychiatrist framed the process as joint problem-solving, consistently solicited Malik's input, and co-created a plan that Malik largely drove.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supported Malik's decision-making, invited him to propose and word the plan, and avoided coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some intrinsic motivation about reducing family conflict was elicited, but the clinician did not sufficiently evoke Malik's own motivations or values regarding his cannabis use.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** A collaborative stance was evident with frequent invitations to co-construct the plan, though the clinician could have more explicitly acknowledged Malik's expertise and negotiated the agenda.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician elicited limited change talk (small strategies like texts and chores) but did not broaden or amplify change language about reducing cannabis use or explore importance/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and reflected sustain talk, but missed opportunities to strategically soften it using double-sided reflections or contrasts highlighting costs and discrepancy.
