# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician was respectful and nonjudgmental, using normalization and validation that supported Alexei. Could strengthen by offering more individualized affirmations that explicitly name strengths and sustain warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and some reflective listening tied to stakes like soccer and family. Reflections were fairly surface-level and could be deepened to capture feelings, meaning, and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on underage drinking and refusal strategies, leading to a concrete phone plan and role-play. Occasionally drifted into extensive advising rather than eliciting the client's own plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session featured practical teamwork—role-play and co-created practice—helping Alexei build confidence. The clinician still supplied most content (lines, exit options) instead of prompting Alexei to generate them first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offered some choices and affirmed Alexei’s control, but frequently provided direct advice and scripts without asking permission or eliciting preferences. More invitation for client-generated solutions would enhance autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Linked drinking to Alexei’s values (soccer, scholarships) and elicited some change talk. Relied heavily on offering strategies rather than using open questions and reflective summaries to deepen and amplify internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Included collaborative moves like role-play and invitations, but the clinician often took an expert/instructor stance with lists and prescriptions. Stronger partnership would involve co-constructing responses from Alexei’s own language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and linked change talk to goals which produced useful statements from Alexei, and role-play strengthened resolve. However, the clinician moved quickly to scripts and missed opportunities to draw out, amplify, and summarize Alexei’s own reasons and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided shaming and offered some normalization, which reduced escalation of sustain talk. The clinician did not consistently use MI strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explore ambivalence and convert sustain talk into change talk.
