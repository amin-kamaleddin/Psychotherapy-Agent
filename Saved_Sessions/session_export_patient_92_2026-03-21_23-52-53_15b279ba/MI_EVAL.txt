# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing ambivalence and affirming Marek's values to create a safe, respectful space.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured ambivalence and practical concerns. Reflections tended toward summary-level and could probe underlying feelings (surprise, embarrassment, worry) more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on weekend alcohol use and moved logically from ambivalence to a concrete, time-limited plan (one-month tracking) tied to outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session felt like a joint enterprise with options offered and Marek's input shaping the plan; clinician summarized and checked understanding to share decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy by offering choices, emphasizing low-pressure testing, and deferring to Marek's judgment, which reinforced his ownership of any changes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Marek's own motivations and what would matter (numbers, next-day feeling), drawing motivation from within. Could be strengthened by asking more evocative 'why' questions and exploring values, importance, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative with repeated solicitations of preference and co-construction of the plan; decisions clearly reflected Marek's priorities and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited concrete change language and translated it into actionable experiments (tracking, NA beers, pacing). Missed opportunities to amplify expressed reasons and probe importance/confidence to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with sustain talk but did not regularly use strategies (double-sided reflections, exploring costs of status quo) to soften sustain talk, leaving some minimization unchallenged.
