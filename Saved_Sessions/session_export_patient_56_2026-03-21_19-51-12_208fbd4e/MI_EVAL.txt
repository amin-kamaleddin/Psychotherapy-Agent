# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supportive and nonjudgmental, normalizing exploration and affirming the patient's values and choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good listening and reflective summaries about behaviors and barriers, though could deepen affect-focused and complex reflections to capture emotions like frustration or pride.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on aligning daily choices with the top value and uses each intervention to move toward a concrete, behavior-specific goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is highly collaborative with co‑creation of messages, role‑play, and frequent plan adjustments based on the patient's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports autonomy by asking permission, offering options, and accepting patient adjustments, reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's core motivation and links values to behavior, but could probe deeper into intrinsic meaning and use stronger amplifying reflections to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a genuine partnership by inviting co‑decision making, rehearsing together, and sharing power in selecting and tweaking strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk and concrete commitments, but missed opportunities to amplify statements and probe importance/confidence to further strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles the one expression of doubt well by accepting revisions and offering contingencies rather than arguing, though could use more double‑sided reflections and explicit reframing of hesitations.
