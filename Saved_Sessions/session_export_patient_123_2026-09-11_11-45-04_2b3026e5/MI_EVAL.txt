# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes the patient’s stance, and avoids judgmental language to create a safe, affirming atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses frequent, accurate reflections and perspective-taking but misses opportunities to deepen empathy by explicitly naming and exploring the patient’s awkwardness and nervousness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on establishing a study routine and safer social choices, moving logically from brainstorming to specific, actionable implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative, inviting the patient’s input at each step and co-constructing the plan while offering help only if desired.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms the patient’s choice and control, offers options, and asks permission before scripting or role‑playing to reinforce ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation out of the patient by asking her to generate strategies and implementation details, eliciting concrete change language and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a genuine partnership by sharing power, asking preferences, and deferring to the patient’s judgment throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with open questions, reflections, and invitations to commit to concrete steps, producing clear planning and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Normalizes ambivalence and avoids reinforcing sustain talk, accepting hesitancy and redirecting toward small, manageable problem‑solving steps.
