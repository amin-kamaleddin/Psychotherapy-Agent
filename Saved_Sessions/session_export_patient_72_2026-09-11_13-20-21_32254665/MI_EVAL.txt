# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, offering options and affirmations (e.g., praising commitment and avoiding blame). Tone and language validated the client's choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated frequent, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing both emotional and practical aspects of the client's experience. Summaries and reflections showed a clear understanding of goals and barriers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with concrete implementation steps. Questions and reflections consistently tied back to the agreed experiment and contingency plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled genuine collaboration by co-constructing the plan, using inclusive language, and incorporating the client's ideas into decisions. The plan was clearly shaped by the client's preferences and input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the client's sense of choice and control by offering multiple options and invitations to choose, avoiding directives. Language reinforced that the client owned and could adapt the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew out internal motivation by asking what change would feel like and why it matters, linking actions to personally meaningful outcomes. Questions helped the client articulate intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the client as the expert on her life, offering choices and asking preferences while co-creating the plan. Power was shared and the client's practical solutions guided the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and amplified desire and intention multiple times, with clear client statements of wanting change and plans to act. Could strengthen practice by using more strategic reflections, importance scaling, or invitations to voice explicit commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledged barriers without reinforcing them and quickly redirected to problem-solving and backups, avoiding argument. This approach minimized sustain talk and helped resolve ambivalence toward action.
