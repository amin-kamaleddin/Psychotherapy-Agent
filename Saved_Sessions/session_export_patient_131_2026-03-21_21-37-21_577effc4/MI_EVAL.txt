# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician is consistently nonjudgmental and affirming, explicitly validating Wiremu’s values and normalizing his steps. No shaming occurs and the clinician treats Wiremu as capable and sincere throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening that validates ambivalence and shame, with frequent summaries. Could deepen with more complex, emotion‑focused reflections to explore shame, fear of losing mana, and the emotional meaning of fatherhood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on target behaviors (reducing alcohol, attending Stopping Violence, safety planning, reunification). Questions and tasks repeatedly orient to these goals without overriding Wiremu’s agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative—Wiremu is invited into decisions and the plan is co‑constructed (script, timing, backup). The clinician offers options rather than directives and treats Wiremu as a full partner in problem‑solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports Wiremu’s choice and control by asking what he wants to rehearse, checking barriers, and offering options. Language avoids pressure and reinforces Wiremu’s ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Wiremu’s own motivations and links actions to personal values (tamariki, mana) using scaling and prompts for commitment. The clinician draws out and strengthens change talk rather than imposing reasons to change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fosters client expertise and invites choices, but the clinician sometimes shifts into a more directive or enacted role (playing Mum/Tristan, offering many suggestions). These moments slightly reduce shared power though overall partnership is strong.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with scaling, targeted questions, follow‑ups, and invitations to specify concrete steps and commitments. Reinforces increases in confidence and highlights change language throughout the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward practical strategies and supports, reframing temptation as manageable. Could more systematically use MI techniques like double‑sided reflections and strategic summaries to name ambivalence and soften sustain talk further.
