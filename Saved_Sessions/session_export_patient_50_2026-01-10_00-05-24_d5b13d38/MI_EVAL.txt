### Acceptance

**Comments:** The psychiatrist consistently showed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing Hana's experience and affirming her strengths (e.g., the recorded presentation).

### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing avoidance with concrete, structured steps (breathing, Pomodoro, timed practice) and little drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk and small commitments (breathing, Pomodoro, goal-setting), though more open questions could deepen desire, reasons, and commitment language.
