# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized ambivalence, and directly responded to the patient's shame language, creating a nonjudgmental climate throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated highly accurate, sustained empathy through reflective listening and mirroring of the patient's emotional language and metaphors, capturing both content and affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and moved the conversation toward concrete, achievable steps (no-smoking-in-car, delayed first cigarette, patch, backups) without getting sidetracked.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction read as a true collaborative process: the clinician invited input at every turn, incorporated the patient's solutions into the plan, and offered support rather than directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice by offering options, asking permission before assisting, honoring the request for gentle support, and reinforcing that the patient leads the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited intrinsic motivation and concrete, patient-generated solutions (daughter as core value, specific coping strategies and backups), building the plan from the patient's own ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently fostered a partnership stance—co-constructing the plan, deferring to the patient's expertise, and sharing power in decision-making throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with concrete steps, but missed opportunities to amplify commitment and probe confidence/importance (e.g., scaling or eliciting stronger commitment language).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward planning with backups and check-ins, but could have more actively softened sustain language using double-sided reflections and strategic reframing.
