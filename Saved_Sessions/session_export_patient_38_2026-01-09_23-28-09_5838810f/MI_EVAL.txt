### Empathy

#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on contraception/testing and guided toward concrete, actionable next steps (same-day serum hCG and a no-pressure counseling visit) while honoring her readiness.

### Collaboration

### Evocation

**Comments:** Effectively elicited values and practical motivations (reliability, privacy, low maintenance) and linked them to action, but could have further deepened intrinsic motivation and commitment language (e.g., scaling, eliciting personal reasons).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint effort—asked for preferences, offered options, and repeatedly invited Elena’s input to shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk about reliable, low-maintenance options and testing, but missed opportunities to deepen and convert this into stronger commitment language (no confidence/importance scaling or explicit elaboration).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and gently accepted sustain talk while redirecting, though more targeted techniques (double-sided reflections, selectively amplifying change talk) could have shortened sustain talk and shifted toward change.
