# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently expressed nonjudgmental, supportive regard and validated Jamal's concerns, creating a safe space for disclosure. Tone and language avoided blame and normalized ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent accurate reflections and summaries showed understanding of Jamal's perspective and social pressures. Empathic attunement could be deeper by explicitly reflecting emotions like anxiety, shame, or fear of rejection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear, narrow focus on risky drinking in team contexts and moved steadily toward concrete action steps. The clinician repeatedly redirected conversation to realistic, actionable plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a genuine partnership with frequent solicitation of Jamal's preferences and co-construction of solutions. Clinician minimized hierarchy and tailored suggestions to Jamal's voice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported Jamal's sense of choice by offering options, asking permission, and acknowledging noncommitment. Offers of help were framed to preserve Jamal's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully drew out Jamal's own ideas and motivations (ally, one-liners, desire to keep his spot). Evocation could be strengthened by probing deeper motives, using importance/confidence questions, and employing selective summaries.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician invited input and repeatedly asked which options Jamal preferred, promoting shared decision-making. To reach top rating, clinician could cede more agenda-setting and ask Jamal to teach about team norms.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited concrete change talk and next steps (ally selection, specific lines, rehearsal) and reinforced these choices. Missed opportunities include amplifying change statements, strategic summaries, and building confidence/commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and steered toward planning but did not consistently explore or reframe Jamal's hesitations. More use of double-sided reflections and explicit validation of ambivalence would help reduce resistance.
