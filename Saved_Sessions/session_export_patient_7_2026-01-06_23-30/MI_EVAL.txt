# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect for boundaries (e.g., “I hear you — it’s okay to keep things private,” offers quiet presence) without judgment or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses frequent empathic statements and reflective listening (e.g., “I hear you,” respects request not to be pushed), but reflections are somewhat surface-level and lack deeper affective naming.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on initial engagement and safety with offers of grounding or quiet presence and avoids pushing for a premature plan or unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates an equal, collaborative stance by offering options and repeatedly inviting the client to set the pace, clearly following the client's lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy through repeated explicit messages that choice and control belong to the client (e.g., 'I won’t push,' 'It’s up to you').

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Does not attempt to elicit the client's own motivations, values, or reasons for change; no permission requests, curiosity questions, or scaling used to evoke internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly transfers authority to the client and invites shared control (offers to follow the client's lead, accepts minimal responses), reinforcing a strong partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Makes essentially no effort to elicit change talk (no scaling, curiosity, or questions about desire/reasons/ability/commitment), focusing instead on presence and safety.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing sustain talk by honoring the client's wish not to be pushed and sidestepping arguments, though it misses opportunities for double-sided reflections or gentle reframing.
