# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Tyler's experience, avoiding blame and using supportive, nonjudgmental language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There were accurate reflections and respectful questions that acknowledged ambivalence, but the clinician sometimes moved quickly to solutions without deeper affective exploration of Tyler's feelings and anxieties.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session kept a clear, appropriate focus on risky alcohol use and the upcoming student-conduct meeting, with questions and interventions tightly linked to preventing future incidents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician worked jointly with Tyler to create a practical plan and asked permission before offering guidance, though frequent scripted options occasionally shifted the interaction toward an expert-driven approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly offered choices, framed suggestions as optional, and emphasized Tyler's control over decisions, consistently reinforcing his autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some of Tyler's motivations were elicited (e.g., valuing school), but the clinician relied more on directives and lists than on open questions or strategies to draw out Tyler's own reasons, values, and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician generally adopted a collaborative stance, co-creating plans and inviting input, but providing many ready-made lists and scripts sometimes reduced the balance of partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician elicited concrete change steps and commitments, yet did not consistently use techniques (e.g., importance/confidence questions or reflective summation) to deepen or amplify Tyler's change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and acknowledged ambivalence, but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to actively help resolve ambivalence.
