# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys nonjudgmental positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and affirms the patient's priorities and practical efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with reflective summaries and empathic questions that link to the patient's values and elicit deeper elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on avoiding another DUI, consistently steering toward concrete, actionable steps (ride options, alarms, rehearsed excuses).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as a partner in joint problem-solving, checks patient preferences, and helps operationalize the patient's ideas without taking control.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supports patient choice by offering options, asking which feels doable, and affirming the patient's ownership of the plan rather than pressuring.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits internal motives and past successes, draws motivation from the patient, and converts ambivalence into concrete implementation intentions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance, consistently invites the patient's expertise, and repeatedly lets the patient choose and refine the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change language with evocative questions and by building on patient-generated plans and rehearsals.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and redirects to solutions, but misses opportunities for explicit double-sided reflections or summaries to more directly resolve minimizations.
