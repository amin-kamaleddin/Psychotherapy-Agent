# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—validates the patient, apologizes for the delay, and uses nonjudgmental language. Repeatedly accepts the patient's limits, which reduces defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and acknowledges practical constraints and frustration. Does not probe deeper emotions or personal meaning beyond pragmatic concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors and guides the session to specific, actionable steps. Elicited a concrete plan (water bottle, reminders) and arranged a low-effort follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages as an equal partner, solicits the patient's input, and co-creates feasible experiments while respecting boundaries. Shows flexibility in adapting the plan to the patient's constraints.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options, asking preferences, and normalizing adjustments. Respects the patient's right to limit scope and emphasizes voluntary changes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits pragmatic motivation and commitment (the water-bottle strategy) but misses opportunities to draw out deeper intrinsic reasons, values, or use scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction collaboratively, offers options and shares power in decision-making. Integrates the patient's limits into the plan and invites co-ownership of follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and strengthens change talk with options, affirmations, and requests for specifics. Could further explore importance, confidence, and past successes to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Gently redirects sustain talk and normalizes time barriers rather than arguing, which reduces resistance. Does not use double-sided reflections or explore costs of the status quo to resolve ambivalence.
