# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, avoiding shaming and pressure; reframes the plan as low-pressure which preserves dignity and reduces defensiveness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate reflections and normalizing; could deepen empathy by probing the underlying emotions (stress, guilt, fear) behind statements to elicit stronger change talk.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on diabetes self-management and efficiently narrows to a specific, measurable target behavior (one pre-lunch glucose check) while staying client-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as an equal partner throughout, co-creating the plan, soliciting preferences, and framing work as joint problem-solving rather than expert instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces the client's sense of choice with autonomy-affirming language and multiple options, avoiding coercion and respecting the client's right not to commit.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation by linking small actions to values, using open questions and if-then planning; could further probe internal reasons and amplify spontaneous change language to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invites the client's input at each step, offers choices rather than directives, and keeps decision-making with the client, demonstrating strong power-sharing and joint planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces small change talk through value-linking, very small experiments, and scaling; could more explicitly reflect and amplify the client's own motivations to deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk with double-sided reflections and normalization rather than arguing; could use more targeted rolling-with-resistance techniques or explore the downsides of not changing to further reduce sustain talk's influence.
