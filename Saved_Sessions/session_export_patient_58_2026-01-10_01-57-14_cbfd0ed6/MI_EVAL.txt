# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opens with an explicit nonjudgmental safety check and consistently uses affirmations and supportive, nonshaming responses that convey unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and validating reflections early on, but could deepen empathy by explicitly exploring Jamal’s frustration and emotional content more often.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on punctuality with a concrete 3-step agenda and ties rehearsals and troubleshooting directly to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a generally collaborative atmosphere by offering options and rehearsals, though the clinician often provides clinician-generated scripts rather than co-creating language with Jamal.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently asks permission and offers choices, supporting ownership, but leans toward directive recommendations and specific instructions that somewhat limit Jamal’s generation of his own solutions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete plans and verbal commitments but does not sufficiently draw out Jamal’s intrinsic motivations, values, or personal reasons for change beyond practical benefits.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites Jamal’s selections and rehearsals, but repeatedly takes the lead with ready-made plans and scripts, positioning the clinician more as an expert than an equal co-explorer.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces some change talk and specific intentions, yet misses opportunities to deepen intrinsic reasons, probe benefits for Jamal, or assess confidence and past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and provides behavioral tactics to manage pushback, but does not actively explore or reframe Jamal’s sustain talk to resolve ambivalence or surface internal motives.
