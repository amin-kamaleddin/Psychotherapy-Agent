# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard and a warm, nonjudgmental stance, validating Anya and offering practical support (drafts, role‑play). Language avoids blaming and conveys clear backing for the client's choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and reflective listening, normalizing feelings and naming stressors, but reflections are more pragmatic than deeply affective. Missed opportunities to probe and reflect underlying shame, fear, and sadness to deepen emotional connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on gambling reduction and debt management with sequential, time‑bound action steps and frequent steering back to concrete tasks. Avoids unrelated tangents and sustains momentum toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works jointly with Anya to co‑create scripts, texts, step‑by‑step instructions and practices role‑plays, consistently asking for preferences and following her lead. Session feels like shared problem‑solving with client input shaping plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly emphasizes choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and repeatedly returning decision‑making to Anya. Avoids pressuring language and supports ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Anya's own motivations, values, and implementation intentions (readiness ruler, linking travel/parenting goals to actions) and amplifies intrinsic reasons for change. Uses evocation to generate concrete, client‑driven commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative, power‑sharing relationship by inviting choices, co‑constructing materials, and asking permission for role‑plays and interventions. Communication conveys respect for Anya's expertise about her life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically elicits and reinforces change talk using the readiness ruler, follow‑up questions, reflections, and conversion of client language into specific actions and commitments. Repeatedly amplifies desire, ability, and intention for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing with sustain talk and redirects toward planning and readiness, using strategic questions to shift focus. Could more explicitly use MI techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections, targeted summaries) to soften sustain talk further.
