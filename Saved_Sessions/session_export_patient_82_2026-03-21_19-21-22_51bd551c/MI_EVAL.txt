# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive with explicit validations and normalization of help‑seeking throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated clear empathic listening and reflections about school and sport impacts and safety; occasionally shifted quickly to problem‑solving without deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol exposure, sleep, and functioning and consistently tied questions and suggestions back to Lukas’s goals (season and grades).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative in tone with invitations for input and tailored scripts, but much content was clinician‑provided rather than co‑generated with Lukas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely offered choices, asked permission, and framed options to preserve Lukas’s ownership of decisions while avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not consistently elicit or amplify Lukas’s intrinsic motivations or confidence, relying more on concrete strategies and education than open questions to draw out change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Held a collaborative stance and checked preferences, but partnership was limited by supplying ready‑made solutions instead of soliciting Lukas’s own wording and ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Missed opportunities to reflect and amplify Lukas’s change‑relevant statements; the clinician moved toward advice and scripts rather than systematically eliciting desire, reasons, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged ambivalence but did not consistently use MI strategies (e.g., double‑sided reflections or affirmations) to soften sustain talk and explicitly reflect competing pulls.
