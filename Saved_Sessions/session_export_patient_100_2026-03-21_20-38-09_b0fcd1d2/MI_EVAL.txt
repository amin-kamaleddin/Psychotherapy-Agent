# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard—normalized ambivalence, affirmed Maya's perspective, and avoided labeling or blaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and validations that captured content and some emotion, but missed chances to probe deeper emotional nuance (e.g., belonging, anxiety).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on drinking at parties and concrete goals (soda in a cup, one-liner, try once), with each intervention tied to that aim.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving—offered ideas while repeatedly asking Maya to select and shape them, with role-play showing shared responsibility.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Maya's choice by offering options, asking permission, and emphasizing that she decides what to try and whether to continue.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited client-generated solutions and used evocative questions, but at times supplied ready-made scripts rather than encouraging more client-originated language and values linkage.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Maya as the expert on her life, co-constructing a tailored, low-burden action plan and following her lead throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced initial change talk with scaling and a commitment to try once, but could more explicitly amplify and summarize Maya's reasons for change to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Named and neutralized sustain talk with reframes and doable strategies, which reduced its influence; could further use double-sided or amplified reflections to shift toward change.
