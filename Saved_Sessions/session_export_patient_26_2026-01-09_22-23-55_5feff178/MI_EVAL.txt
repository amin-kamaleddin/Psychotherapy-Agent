# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—validates feelings, thanks the patient, affirms efforts, and closes with encouragement throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate understanding of the patient's experience by reflecting emotions and practical strains and offering problem-focused empathy and validation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on resolving the childcare problem with concrete, near-term action steps and clear prioritization.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner—asks permission, elicits preferences, co-creates scripts, and invites follow-up to support ongoing collaboration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offers options rather than directives, honors the patient's choices (including deferring smoking/drinking), and leaves room for adaptation or decline.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some behavior-focused commitments but leans toward informational/directive moves and misses opportunities to draw out values, confidence, and intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: invites the patient to choose priorities, negotiates solutions, and shares power in developing practical steps and language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete change plans (neighbor trial, Head Start call) but could use more open, evocative questions and amplification of past successes to deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes worries and offers alternatives without confronting sustain talk, though double-sided reflections could better surface and resolve ambivalence.
