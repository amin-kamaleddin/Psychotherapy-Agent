# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained an explicitly nonjudgmental, supportive stance with repeated validations and normalizations, showing strong unconditional positive regard throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking captured feelings and practical concerns, though the clinician could have deepened empathic reflections on the emotional toll of repeated setbacks before shifting to problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed sharply focused on attending interviews and resolving childcare barriers, with consistent steering toward concrete next steps and action planning without drifting off-topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction read as a team effort with co-developed messages and invited tweaks, but the clinician often led with ready-made solutions rather than eliciting the client's ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Multiple options, permission-seeking, and repeated checks about preferences supported the client's sense of choice and control, avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some preferences and commitments were elicited, but the clinician relied more on clinician-provided content than on targeted open questions to draw out the client's intrinsic motivations and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician invited input and shared decision-making, yet frequently provided expert drafts which reduced opportunities for the client to generate solutions before co-creating them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and amplified change-oriented statements and translated them into action steps, but could more deliberately reflect and deepen the client's strong confidence language to elicit stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed without reinforcement, with rapid redirection to practical options; using more double-sided reflections would further validate ambivalence while guiding toward change.
