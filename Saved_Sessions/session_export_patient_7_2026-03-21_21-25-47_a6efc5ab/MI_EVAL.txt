# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing limits and honoring Amina’s choices. Warm, nonjudgmental language reduced pressure and supported engagement.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, well-timed reflections and strong perspective-taking that tracked Amina’s minimal responses. Empathic attunement validated barriers and avoided pressuring the client.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on engagement, safety, and immediate, achievable coping steps throughout the encounter. Agenda-setting and safety checks kept the session on track without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with choices offered and session content shaped by Amina’s preferences. The clinician co-created the plan and avoided an authoritarian stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice, used permission language, and respected declines, keeping initiative with Amina. Suggestions were framed as optional, which reduced resistance.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internally-driven responses (song, phrase, porch backup, importance rating) but did not consistently probe deeper reasons, values, or confidence. More targeted evocative questions could have strengthened intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated shared decision-making and deferred to Amina’s expertise about her life. Permission-seeking and joint planning reinforced a genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully drew out concrete, small-step change talk (e.g., 5-minute break, 2/10 importance) but missed opportunities to deepen reasons, values, and confidence. Further exploration of what would make the change more important or feasible was limited.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and reframed sustain talk into manageable steps, using double-focused choices to redirect resistance. There were missed chances to use more advanced double-sided reflections to explicitly name sustain talk while amplifying the other side.
