# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently shows unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance with validating reflections and no shaming or arguing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and accurate reflections that capture content and tone, lowering defensiveness and keeping the patient engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on asthma self-management and repeatedly narrows toward a specific, measurable, time-limited plan (alarm + Post-it + rescue-on-hook for two weeks).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly a two-way exploration: the clinician solicits the patient’s ideas, builds on them, and co-creates the experiment with the patient’s input central.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors patient choice and limits, frames the plan as the patient's experiment, and asks for input at each decision point to preserve ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation from the patient's values and past habits and links change to daily benefits, but could probe deeper into personally meaningful or long-term values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the work as collaborative and defers to the patient's expertise, using repeated invitations and joint problem-solving rather than an expert lecture.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits concrete change talk and criteria for success, but could more deliberately amplify and consolidate commitment (e.g., ask for a commitment statement or confidence rating).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and neutrally reflects sustain talk, effectively redirecting to feasible options, though it could use more strategic techniques (double-sided reflections) to further weaken sustain talk.
