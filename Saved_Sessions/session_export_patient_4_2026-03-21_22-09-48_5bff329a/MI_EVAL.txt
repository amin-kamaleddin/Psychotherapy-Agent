# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys respect and nonjudgmental regard, normalizing distress and acknowledging real‑life impact throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective‑taking with accurate reflections and an extended summary that captures both facts and feelings, naming emotional consequences and symptom variability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, goal‑oriented focus on obtaining medical answers and practical planning, moving the session purposefully from assessment to concrete next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Overall collaborative tone with invitations to choose and rehearsal support, though detailed clinician‑driven checklists and scripts slightly reduce full parity at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by offering options, asking permission, and framing tools as aids rather than directives, reinforcing ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation (asking what the patient wants, offering choices) but limited elicitation of internal motivations, values, or use of scaling questions to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works collaboratively—co‑creating scripts and practicing—but clinician expertise and prescriptive lists sometimes make the interaction more clinician‑led than fully equal partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opens space for change‑directed talk and coaches assertiveness, yet misses systematic elicitation and amplification of intrinsic reasons, desire, and plans for broader change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Respects biomedical concerns and avoids confrontation, but rarely uses double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries to actively soften sustain talk and explore ambivalence.
