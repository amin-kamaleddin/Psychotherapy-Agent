# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated Aisha's experience and stressors throughout the session, creating a respectful, nonjudgmental tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, though reflections were often brief and pragmatic with missed opportunities to deepen emotional content (e.g., shame/‘feeling gross’).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on restarting exercise/walking and guided the session toward a concrete, actionable plan with contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked as a joint problem-solving partner and invited preferences, but at times offered detailed suggestions early which limited equal client-generated idea generation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking what felt doable, and reinforcing that Aisha decides who and how to involve others.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation using open questions and a confidence ruler, but could more actively amplify passionate language and explore the emotional significance of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and checked preferences, yet frequently presented multiple specific options which somewhat steered the session instead of eliciting more client-generated solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and strengthened change talk with open questions, reflections, and a confidence ruler, and moved the conversation to concrete commitment and planning.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Validated concerns about bailing and fatigue without amplifying sustain talk, promptly redirecting to realistic backups and contingency planning that preserved autonomy.
