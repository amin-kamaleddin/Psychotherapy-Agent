### Empathy

**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on asthma self-management with logical progression from barriers to concrete, time-limited behavior tests. Session stayed tightly linked to reducing nighttime panic and coughing with a measurable plan and follow-up.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Functioned as an equal partner throughout, inviting Maria's input at each decision point and co-constructing the plan. Offered help only if wanted and repeatedly asked for her preferences.

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative stance with co-created decisions (trigger choice, reminder strategy) and frequent invitations for Maria's input. Tone and structure positioned the clinician and patient as working together.

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Opened and reinforced some change talk and used a confidence ruler, but did not consistently follow up to elicit reasons, importance, or elaboration. Missed opportunities to amplify Maria's statements about wanting fewer night panics to build commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated skepticism, offering alternatives and using double-sided reflections to redirect toward change. Could more explicitly reflect sustain-side concerns then summarize and amplify small change language to tip the balance further.
