# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, using normalization and validation to acknowledge ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic reflections and tone that matched suggestions to the patient's context, but reflections were often brief and quickly followed by solutions rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on evening alcohol use and concrete reduction goals, consistently linking suggestions to the patient's priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as cooperative problem-solving, repeatedly invited the patient's input, and tailored recommendations to her life and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized patient choice, offered multiple options, and avoided pressuring language, reinforcing the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change-oriented statements through questions and choices but tended to move to solutions rather than systematically drawing out the patient's motives, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invited choices, though at times the clinician offered multiple expert-generated strategies instead of co-constructing options from the patient's own language.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Prompted specific change-language and experiments, but missed opportunities to amplify reasons, probe importance/confidence, and elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed concerns into manageable experiments, though could have used more double-sided reflections to deepen ambivalence work.
