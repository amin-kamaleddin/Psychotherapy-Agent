# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and validated Daniela’s feelings without blame, normalizing ambivalence and acknowledging courage. This warm, nonjudgmental stance supported the patient throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, tracking affect and concerns and deepening the conversation. Open questions and targeted reflections showed strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on planning and rehearsing disclosure throughout the session. Skills, role-plays, and wording choices were repeatedly linked back to the goal of a small, comfortable disclosure step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was co-constructed with Daniela: the clinician offered choices, invited her input, and adapted interventions to her preferences. The clinician avoided dominating and engaged in joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options and affirmed Daniela’s preferences, normalizing hesitation and reinforcing her control over pace and decisions. This consistently supported patient autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Daniela’s reasons for change and helped produce a concrete plan, but rarely probed values or used importance/confidence scaling or summarizing to deepen intrinsic motivation. Strong evocation but could be strengthened to consolidate commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Behaved as a genuine collaborator by asking which options felt safest and co-creating text and role-plays. Power-sharing and responsiveness to Daniela’s choices demonstrated high partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively invited and shaped change talk through practice suggestions and concrete wording, and Daniela voiced clear change intentions. Missed opportunities to amplify/summarize that talk or ask commitment/confidence questions to build momentum.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk without reinforcing it and used role-play to address fears, helping neutralize reasons for non-disclosure. Could further reduce sustain talk by using more double-sided reflections and less immediate solutioning.
