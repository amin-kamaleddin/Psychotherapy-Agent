# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated respect and nonjudgmental support with multiple validating statements and normalization of the client's concerns. Avoided criticism or moralizing language, conveying unconditional positive regard throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately reflecting both content and feeling to validate ambivalence. Reflections and summaries built rapport and showed clear understanding of the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on relapse prevention and concrete behaviors, guiding the conversation toward a specific coping plan. Questions and prompts kept momentum toward actionable steps like script, alarm, and backup plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction unfolded as joint problem-solving with inclusive language and frequent solicitation of the client's preferences. The clinician honored practical constraints and co-created the plan with the client.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the client's choice and control, offering options and explicitly giving the sister an out in proposed wording. The clinician accepted the client's caveats without pressuring for promises, reinforcing agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client's solutions and reasons for change but could have probed more deeply for importance, confidence, and values linkage. More amplification of the client's change language into commitment statements would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the work as collaborative and treated the client as the expert, asking which steps felt most doable and co-creating wording and limits. Language and process demonstrated genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change-oriented language and specific steps, highlighting successes and asking for actionable wording. Missed some opportunities to amplify and convert change talk into explicit commitment or confidence talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and ambivalence without reinforcing it, redirecting quickly to practical supports and alternatives. Could increase use of strategic softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to further reduce sustain statements.
