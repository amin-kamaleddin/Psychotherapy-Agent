# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard by validating feelings, normalizing the experience, and avoiding judgment while supporting Elena's right to choose.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, consistent empathic listening with reflective statements that captured Elena's affect and concerns and normalized her nervousness about freezing.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on contraceptive safety and moved the conversation purposefully from exploration to concrete planning (condoms, emergency plan, rehearsal) without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative: the clinician invited Elena's preferences, adjusted to her comfort, and allowed Elena to shape which strategies to pursue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported choice by offering multiple paths, normalizing reluctance, and repeatedly asking which option felt easiest to preserve Elena's ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation and used role-play to generate planning, but could have probed deeper for intrinsic reasons, scaling, and stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as a joint exploration, co‑creating decisions (choice of strategies, tone, and lines) and sharing power throughout the encounter.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and built on change talk with values questions and concrete options, yet missed opportunities to probe importance/confidence and amplify change statements for commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward manageable options and reframed concerns, though used few double‑sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence.
