# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, respectful tone and validated Margaret's concerns, offering options rather than criticism and honoring her preferences throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking are evident, but the clinician missed opportunities to explore deeper feelings and the lived impact of retirement and social life to reach a higher empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on reducing alcohol-related risk with concrete, measurable goals and stepwise next actions (safety check, Gentle Cutback Plan, follow-up aids).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative interaction with frequent invitations for input and co-creation of the plan, and the clinician implemented Margaret's edits without pushback.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly supported choice and control by offering multiple options, honoring preferences (not quitting, file format), and asking permission before proceeding.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of change talk occurred, but the clinician relied more on offering practical options than drawing out Margaret's personal motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful of Margaret's input, though the clinician at times provided expert suggestions before fully eliciting the patient's own ideas for solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician effectively elicited and reinforced tentative change talk and translated it into a concrete plan, but could have amplified personal reasons and importance/confidence language more explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician avoided confrontation and allowed sustain talk to coexist, but did not use double-sided reflections or reframings to strategically soften sustain talk before moving to advice.
