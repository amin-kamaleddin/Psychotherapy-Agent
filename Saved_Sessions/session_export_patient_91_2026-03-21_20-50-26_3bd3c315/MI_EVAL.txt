# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed warmth, unconditional positive regard, and nonjudgmental validation while affirming strengths and normalizing ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and picked up specific concerns (forgetting, work awkwardness, finger pricks), but missed opportunities for deeper affective reflections to explore the emotional weight of being overwhelmed or anxious.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on blood sugar monitoring and steered the conversation toward concrete, actionable strategies and a specific implementation plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in genuine joint problem‑solving by soliciting Marcus's views at each step, adapting suggestions to his answers, and co‑creating the plan based on his lived experience.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and ownership by inviting preferences, offering options and permission to adjust or delay, and normalizing non‑perfection to reinforce Marcus's control over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Marcus's ideas, past successes, and confidence ratings, but could more strongly evoke intrinsic motivations and values about why monitoring matters personally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered authentic partnership by asking permission before offering ideas, inviting Marcus to choose among options, and ensuring decisions were client‑driven and mutually agreed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced several change statements and set commitments (alarms, start date, check‑in), yet could amplify and reflect Marcus's own change language more often and explore personally meaningful reasons to monitor.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and constructively redirected toward planning (snooze rule, spouse nudges), though could use more double‑sided reflections or explicit normalization to further diffuse ambivalence.
