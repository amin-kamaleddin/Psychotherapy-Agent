# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's feelings, avoiding blame or pressure. Could further reduce shame by explicitly normalizing common barriers (e.g., caregiving, fear of diagnosis).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly accurate, timely reflections capture both content and emotion and help the patient feel heard and safe. Occasional deeper empathic reflections (e.g., existential fears about parental role) could further deepen the alliance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on seeking medical evaluation and moves the patient toward a concrete action (ready-to-copy message). Could consolidate options earlier into one agreed plan with a specific timeline and follow-up to strengthen momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is a genuine two-way collaboration: clinician invites input, accepts the patient's wording, and co-creates the final message and coping steps. Offers concrete shared actions (staying, counting breaths) that reinforce equal partnership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's choice and control by offering options and asking which feels most doable, avoiding directive language. Help is offered only as requested, reinforcing autonomy throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations and converts them into actionable steps, using open questions and preference prompts. Missed opportunities to deepen intrinsic motivators by linking actions to values or asking for commitment/confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician communicates a collaborative stance and shares power by inviting choices and deferring to patient preferences. The session's plan clearly reflects the patient's decisions, fostering true partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change-oriented statements and prompts selection of a doable small step, affirming the choice and wording. Could have asked more to deepen desire/reasons and to elicit confidence or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing feelings and offering pivot options and small coping steps. Could more explicitly use double-sided reflections or summaries to highlight ambivalence and further reduce the pull of sustain talk.
