# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through validating language, normalizing slips, and affirmations that acknowledged the client's struggle and strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Sustained, accurate reflections captured ambivalence, emotions, and barriers (fear, shame, routine), demonstrating strong empathic attunement and perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking cessation and moved the session toward concrete, actionable steps (NRT choice, coping skills, start date) while balancing client-centered pacing with goal-oriented guidance.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving, regularly solicited the client's input, and co-developed the plan and relapse strategies consistent with equal-partner engagement.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported client choice by offering multiple options, validating preferences (e.g., timing of telling her daughter), and inviting the client to select start times and strategies.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out intrinsic motivators (health, daughter) and used the client's language to build momentum, but could have probed deeper values and confidence (e.g., scaling questions) to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the client as an active collaborator by asking permission to share information, inviting choices, and following the client's lead in shaping the plan and rehearsals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced multiple strands of change talk and helped set a realistic test (patch+gum, routine swap), though could have amplified specific change statements or used scaling to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and skillfully redirected sustain talk by validating worries, normalizing slips, and providing a nonjudgmental relapse plan and practical coping strategies.
