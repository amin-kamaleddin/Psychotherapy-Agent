# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Jason's stress from the opening onward. Never blamed or dismissed him and offered practical, supportive help.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate reflections and invited Jason to tell more. Could deepen empathy with more complex reflections probing underlying emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on preparing Jason for the conduct meeting and the immediate behavioral target. Interventions (scripts, role-play, tips) were tightly tied to concrete next steps with virtually no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session read as a partnership: clinician invited input, offered choices, and jointly refined wording. At times the clinician shifted into an expert role by providing detailed procedural advice, which reduced client-led exploration.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported Jason's choices and offered options while acknowledging his preferences. The clinician sometimes gave strong recommendations that slightly limited pure non-directiveness.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some client material (preferences, what would help) but relied heavily on clinician-supplied language and solutions. Missed opportunities to probe values or discrepancies to draw out Jason's intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction felt cooperative and team-like, with shared drafting and role-play options. The clinician's provision of expert procedural guidance occasionally shifted power back toward the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some attempts to elicit planning language and offered concrete steps, but largely supplied scripts rather than eliciting Jason's own reasons or commitment. Missed chances to ask open questions that would amplify tentative change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing minimizations and redirected toward accountability and practical steps without confrontation. Could have used more double-sided reflections to acknowledge social values while highlighting discrepancies.
