# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing slips and validating ambivalence throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and reflective listening at key moments, but missed opportunities to explore deeper underlying feelings like guilt, FOMO, or anxious/tired affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, steady focus on reducing problematic phone use with concrete strategies and a co-created weekly plan tied to sleep and grades.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was strongly collaborative with multiple invitations for patient input and tailoring of suggestions, co-constructing alarms, phrases, and fallback actions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering multiple options, asking which felt doable, permitting reasonable flexibility, and normalizing slips to reduce pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effort to draw out commitments and doable choices, but the clinician leaned toward solution-provision rather than systematically eliciting intrinsic reasons, values, or using scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership through choices and invitations to decide wording and plans, though at times the interaction moved into clinician-driven solutioning rather than deeper joint exploration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited a few concrete change statements and choices, but often shifted quickly to offering solutions instead of reflecting, amplifying, and deepening the patient's own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and avoided argument, but used few double-sided reflections or reframes to honor reasons for phone use (connection/stress relief) while guiding toward change.
