# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently communicated unconditional positive regard, normalized ambivalence, affirmed realistic steps, and avoided moralizing or judgment throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking reduced defensiveness, but the clinician could use more complex, emotion-focused reflections to capture Marco's weariness and resignation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on a concrete proximal goal (delay first cigarette 30 minutes) with operationalized steps and a planned follow-up, maintaining purposeful momentum without overriding autonomy.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a genuine collaboration: the clinician invited input, checked preferences, and co-constructed the plan so the patient’s choices shaped the strategies and follow-up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently offered choices, emphasized patient control, framed strategies as experiments, and reinforced that slips would not be judged, matching the patient's desire to act on his own terms.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician successfully evoked motivation for the specific small change but did little to explore broader intrinsic motives, values, or past successes that could deepen internal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** A strong partnership stance was conveyed through collaborative language, offering options rather than prescriptions, and honoring the patient's ownership of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician effectively elicited and reinforced change talk by proposing a small experiment, eliciting a concrete commitment, and immediately operationalizing and amplifying that commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician rolled with resistance, normalized ambivalence, and avoided arguing, but used few double-sided reflections or reframes that might more strategically soften sustain talk and build momentum.
