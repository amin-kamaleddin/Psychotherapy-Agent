# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with repeated validations and nonjudgmental framing that honored the patient’s priorities and ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provided accurate reflections and captured both sides of ambivalence, but reflections were sometimes summary-level rather than deeper affective or evocative explorations of stress and social feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing/quitting smoking and repeatedly returned to concrete, behaviorally specific steps and testing plans without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in joint problem-solving by offering options, rehearsing language, and repeatedly deferring to the patient’s choices and preferences when building the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice and low-pressure experimentation, normalizing stopping the test and framing steps as reversible to protect patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited the patient’s own motivations with open questions and prompts, leading the patient to voice benefits, select an experiment, and define success criteria.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative and power-sharing, inviting the patient to prioritize options and co-create the plan throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk through open questions and reflective reinforcement, which resulted in specific, testable commitments from the patient.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalized and used double-sided reflections to reduce resistance, but could have been more proactive in eliciting counterarguments to sustain talk or exploring past non-smoking successes.
