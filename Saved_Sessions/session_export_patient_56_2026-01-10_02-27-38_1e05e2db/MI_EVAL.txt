# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with validating, nonjudgmental language that normalizes the client's experience and affirms her priorities.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, specific reflections capture both content and feeling, showing strong perspective-taking and emotional attunement throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior (two-night self-care trial) and consistently guides questions and reflections toward concrete planning and implementation.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as a joint exploration, listens and builds on the client's responses, and co-creates the plan rather than directing or instructing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offers choices, asks which options the client prefers, and affirms that the decision is the client's to make, avoiding pressure or directive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits values, reasons, and a concrete plan but could deepen evocation by prompting the client for more personal summaries or using scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the client as the expert on her life, asks for her input on wording and next steps, and defers to her judgment when shaping the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and supports change-oriented language and planning, but missed opportunities to more actively amplify and sequence change talk into explicit commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** When sustain talk arises the clinician normalizes and reframes it, avoids reinforcing resistance, and pivots to practical coping strategies and small trials.
