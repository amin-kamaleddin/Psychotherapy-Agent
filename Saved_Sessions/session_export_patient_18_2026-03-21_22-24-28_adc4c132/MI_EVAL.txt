# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, validating the client's perspective and choices without shaming or directive language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, specific reflective listening captures ambivalence and feeling states, mirroring details like text-first preference, soda, and exit plan to validate and deepen the client's expression.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stays focused on alcohol-risk reduction and concrete safety plans, moving pragmatically from brainstorming to selecting and refining a feasible plan tied to the client's goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician works as a partner—inviting choices, offering to co-write text options, and repeatedly checking the client's preferences so the plan is co-created rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are offered throughout and the clinician emphasizes the client's control over timing and approach, supporting agency and avoiding pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicits practical motivations and steps (protecting school, buddy plan, soda) and checks confidence, but could deepen intrinsic motivation and elicit stronger personal meaning or commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interactions clearly reflect shared decision-making with the client leading next steps; the clinician repeatedly defers to the client's expertise about her relationships and style.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicits and amplifies change talk (intent to try buddy plan, send text, bring soda) and asks a confidence rating, but could further summarize and amplify to elicit firmer commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is minimized through reflective normalizing and pragmatic reframes (soda, exit plan), though additional moves (e.g., double-sided reflections linking concerns to goals) could more actively pivot toward change.
