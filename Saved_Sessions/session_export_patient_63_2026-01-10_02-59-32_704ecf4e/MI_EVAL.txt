# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician conveys respect and nonjudgmental tone, normalizes difficulty, and reduces blame. Could add more individualized affirmations and explicitly normalize ambivalence to reach a top score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that accurately summarizes Jordan's experience and hesitations. Could use more complex reflections to deepen the emotional content (e.g., shame or fatigue).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays tightly focused on shoplifting and concrete coping strategies, with repeated returns to actionable steps and rehearsal. The clinician maintains clear direction toward behavior change throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly a joint effort with negotiation of role‑play intensity and selection of scenarios, soliciting Jordan's input. Language and procedures create an egalitarian, co‑constructed process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently asks permission, offers options, and acknowledges Jordan's control over choices, supporting his autonomy. This stance reinforces that any change is Jordan's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits what matters about avoiding re‑offending, explores triggers and small successes, and translates motivation into practice. Could more often prompt Jordan to articulate his own reasons/values and use scaling to build commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician adopts a collaborative stance, invites Jordan to choose options and role‑play scenarios, and respects his expertise. To improve, invite Jordan to generate more of his own solutions before offering clinician suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some change talk is elicited and small steps are reinforced, but there are missed opportunities to amplify desire, reasons, and confidence and to elicit commitment language. More follow‑ups and reflective amplification would deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician acknowledges sustain talk and avoids confrontation while offering practical strategies. However, there is limited use of double‑sided reflections or exploration of the downside of continuing behavior to resolve ambivalence more effectively.
