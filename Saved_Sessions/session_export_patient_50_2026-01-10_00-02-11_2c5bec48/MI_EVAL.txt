# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, warmth, and normalization throughout the session, avoiding blame or labeling and repeatedly validating Hana's experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and reflects feelings and somatic reactions, but could deepen empathy with more complex reflections of underlying fears and use double-sided summaries to capture ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with concrete graduated exposures, coping techniques, and repeated checks of readiness and next actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction is highly collaborative: the clinician invites Hana's input at decision points, co-constructs the plan, and responds to her reports rather than imposing a unilateral agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission before guiding, and framing advice as options so Hana retains ownership of timing and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation by drawing on Hana's past success and offering in-session mastery experiences, but leans toward providing solutions instead of using more open, evocative questions to probe values and deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as a partnership, frequently inviting preferences and co-creating decisions (phrases, pacing, rewards), demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and supports change talk by highlighting past successes and prompting doable steps, yet misses opportunities to deepen and amplify commitment with evocative follow-ups or scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and redirects sustain talk with validation and practical strategies (naming thoughts, brief exposures), but could strengthen use of double-sided reflections and link sustain talk more systematically to the plan.
