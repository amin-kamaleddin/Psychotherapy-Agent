# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician was consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, using affirmations and normalizing Diego's concerns and desire for privacy throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate summaries showed understanding of Diego's situation, but the clinician stayed largely at the content level and missed deeper emotional probing about using to 'escape.'

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear, consistent focus on harm-reduction behaviors with concrete, actionable steps (SSP, naloxone, safer injecting) and minimal drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a team effort—clinician invited Diego to co-design steps, offered role-play and scripting, and followed his lead on which options to pursue.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly honored Diego's choices and pacing, offered multiple options, and avoided pressuring language while reinforcing confidentiality and control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited motivations and practical intentions and used open questions, but often shifted into information-giving rather than drawing out Diego's personal values or deeper reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician framed the work as client-led, deferred expertise to Diego's preferences, and consistently invited him to choose next steps, fostering genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited and reinforced specific change talk and intentions, but missed opportunities to amplify strong commitment language (e.g., exploring the '10 out of 10' readiness).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided arguing and neutrally reflected ambivalence, yet used few double-sided or evocative reflections to explicitly acknowledge the appeal of 'escape' while gently pivoting to change.
