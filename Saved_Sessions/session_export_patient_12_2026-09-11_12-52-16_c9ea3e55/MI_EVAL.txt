# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys respect and unconditional positive regard with affirmations and nonjudgmental reflections, avoiding labels or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate summaries, but could deepen empathic responses by elaborating on the depth of Amira's isolation, despair, and shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, focused direction toward reducing immediate risk and building a concrete micro-behavior (one‑minute step), consistently returning to actionable planning without topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as a joint enterprise by asking preferences at decision points, offering manageable options, and co-constructing the plan so the client’s choices drive the process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice and avoids coercion by offering multiple options, asking what feels doable, and affirming worth regardless of action, reinforcing client control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits client-generated, practical solutions and micro-behaviors, but could better evoke deeper intrinsic motives, values, and confidence about long-term change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently invites Amira’s input, shares power in planning (e.g., rehearsing, setting timers together), and positions the clinician as a collaborator rather than an authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces specific, concrete change steps and small commitments, but should more often amplify change-language and explore reasons, importance, and confidence to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids confrontation but tends to repeat its content rather than strategically soften it with double-sided reflections, exception-finding, or reframes toward agency.
