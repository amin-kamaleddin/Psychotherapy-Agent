# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and validation without judgment, praising successes and normalizing struggles. No instances of shaming or minimizing observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing both content and feeling and matching a warm, conversational tone. Reflections deepen rapport and encourage disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on target behaviors and moves from exploration to concrete, actionable strategies. Conversation stays behaviorally specific with no drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positions the clinician as a partner, co-constructing the plan and inviting the patient's input in wording and rehearsal. Decisions and strategies are shaped by the patient's preferences and experiences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by asking permission and offering multiple options, then letting the patient choose what feels most doable. Language affirms ownership and avoids coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicits internal motivations (pride in quitting, parenting values) and links them to strategies, but could more deliberately draw out intrinsic reasons and confidence/ability language to build commitment. More systematic elicitation of personal arguments for change would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys shared expertise and client-centered decision-making, offering to role-play and asking permission before interventions. Invites co-creation of phrasing and plans, reinforcing a collaborative stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and links strengths to concrete strategies, producing specific change language. Missed opportunities include explicitly evoking commitment or asking confidence ratings to solidify commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain-talk drivers and avoids confronting them, using reflections and strategic pivots to options and rehearsals. Could enhance impact by using more double-sided reflections and immediately highlighting discrepancy to soften sustain talk further.
