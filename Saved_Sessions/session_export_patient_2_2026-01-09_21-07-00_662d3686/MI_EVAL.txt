# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, with individualized affirmations (e.g., caregiving role) that created a safe, nonjudgmental atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated high-quality reflective listening and perspective-taking—accurate summaries and reflections tracked affect and mirrored the patient's language throughout the visit.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on smoking reduction, moving from values and reasons to concrete strategies and a two‑week experiment to guide behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed the plan with the patient, inviting input at each step and shaping decisions (tools, routines, contingencies) around her preferences and context.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice and framed change as the patient's experiment, avoided coercive language, and reinforced her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation from the patient's own values and reasons with open questions, but could have probed confidence/importance more directly and summarized change talk to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the patient as the expert on her life, sharing power in decision‑making and routinely inviting her to choose priorities and strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified desire, reasons, and ability with praise for trial behavior, though could have more explicitly summarized and strengthened commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and respectfully redirected around drinking while normalizing ambivalence, but missed opportunities for a double‑sided reflection or brief exploration to further diminish sustain talk.
