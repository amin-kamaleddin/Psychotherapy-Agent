# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and warm—reframed self-labeling, normalized ambivalence, and validated slips to reduce shame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with timely reflections, but could use more complex/emotion-focused reflections and summary of ambivalence to deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, behaviorally specific focus on reducing alcohol in social situations with concrete goals (three-drink limit, refusal lines, support person, tactics).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—invited role-play and checked in frequently—but sometimes led with specific tactics rather than eliciting the patient's ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and avoided coercion by offering options, asking permission, normalizing pace, and framing advice as suggestions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation and concrete commitments (limit, rehearsal), but relied on clinician-suggested options rather than more open questions to draw out self-generated reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed collaboration and invited input, yet frequent expert-direction with unprompted tactics slightly reduced shared ownership of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk leading to a concrete plan, but could better amplify and reflect change statements and ask importance/confidence follow-ups.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed social concerns to make cutting back compatible with socializing, though missed opportunities for double-sided or sustain-focused reflections.
