# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, using validating summaries and normalizing slips to affirm the client's worth and encourage self-compassion.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and accurate summaries that mirrored the client's language and affect, fostering rapport and a deep understanding of ambivalence and values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on aligning daily routine with values via a concrete tiny habit, with questions and reflections that maintained momentum toward a pragmatic experiment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained an equal-partner stance throughout, using open questions and inviting the client's preferences to co-create the plan rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced the client's choice and control by offering options, permission for slips, and language that emphasized her decision-making and ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited values, past successes, and practical strategies, but could more deliberately probe for strength, confidence, and explicit commitment language to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative interaction that deferred to the client's expertise and incorporated her solutions (alarm, mat, buddy) into a jointly developed plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively encouraged and amplified change talk with focused questions and reinforcement of the chosen plan, though more strategic elicitation of ability/confidence and clear commitment language would strengthen it.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk and reframed concerns toward doable experiments and self-compassion, but could more often use double-sided reflections to further soften resistance.
