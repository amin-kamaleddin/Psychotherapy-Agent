### Acceptance

**Comments:** Immediate, consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental tone; validates Ethan's perspective and repeatedly affirms his autonomy throughout the session.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, succinct reflective listening and perspective-taking, but relies more on straightforward paraphrase than deeper, affect-focused or complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, steady focus on reducing drinking with concrete, time-limited goals and sequential steps from intent to plan to rehearsal, resisting drift to other topics.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Genuine teamwork approach: invites Ethan's input, offers options rather than prescriptions, and integrates his choices into a jointly constructed plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and concrete supports, producing change talk and a plan, but could probe more for personal meaning, values, and amplify change language into commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions Ethan as the expert on his life, offers choices and invites him to select supports, fostering real power-sharing in planning and decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asks targeted readiness/confidence questions and invites doable alternatives that generated change talk, though reflections could have more often amplified Ethan's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
