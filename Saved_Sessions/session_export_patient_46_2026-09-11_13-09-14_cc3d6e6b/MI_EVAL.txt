# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates nonjudgmental, respectful regard and affirms the patient's perspective, avoiding labels or lectures throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provides strong reflective listening and accurate paraphrase that validates belonging and concerns, though could deepen by explicitly naming underlying ambivalence or emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on risky drinking and guides the session from eliciting ideas to selecting a concrete, measurable trial with implementation details.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as collaborative problem‑solving with the clinician and patient co‑constructing the plan and the clinician checking phrasing and offering help only as requested.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports the patient's sense of choice by offering options, asking what feels doable, and avoiding pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Relies on open questions and reflections to elicit the patient's own reasons and strategies for change, sequencing them into a feasible plan and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the patient as expert and shares power in decision‑making, supporting the patient's selection and shaping of strategies rather than prescribing solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk through open, evocative questions and reflections, converting contemplation into a specific, time‑bound trial (sipping a soda).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to practical risk‑reduction, though could more explicitly acknowledge the pull of social enjoyment using double‑sided reflections.
