# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shows consistent unconditional positive regard through normalizing language, affirmations, and validation while avoiding judgment or shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening with accurate content and affect reflections, though some reflections are concise and could probe deeper emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reclaiming morning routines and repeatedly steers the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps without being coercive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the interaction as an equal partnership, solicits the client's preferences, and co-constructs a practical plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client choice and agency by offering options, avoiding prescriptive language, and affirming the client's control over pacing and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively links behavior to core values and elicits motivation with evocative questions, but could further deepen evocation by drawing out confidence, barriers, and firmer commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicits the client's expertise and preferences at each decision point and frames clinician and client as teammates in designing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk with concrete steps and signs of success, though could more consistently amplify commitment and assess confidence (e.g., scaling or verbal commitment).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Does not amplify sustain talk, briefly normalizes ambivalence, and redirects toward strengths and practical planning to avoid dwelling on reasons for inaction.
