# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with warm, validating language and affirmations that normalized Farid’s experience and reduced self-blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate empathic listening with several reflective statements, though many reflections stayed at content level rather than exploring deeper emotional nuance like discouragement or self-doubt.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on increasing brief physical activity and progressed from general goals to specific, actionable plans with timing, cues, and contingency options.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly joint problem-solving: options were offered, strategies were adapted to Farid’s responses, and plans were co-constructed rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and avoided pressuring language, asking permission before offering solutions and respecting Farid’s decisions about supports like colleague check-ins.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Farid’s motivations, past successes, and implementation details effectively, but could have probed deeper into personal values and used summaries to amplify intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance—clinician solicited Farid’s preferences, invited his suggestions, and tailored strategies to his choices, demonstrating shared power and respect.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and shaped change talk with concrete options and a confidence ruler, though sometimes moved quickly to offering solutions instead of amplifying Farid’s own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and reframed sustain talk to reduce blame and redirect to planning, but did not consistently explore what staying the same provides and occasionally moved too quickly into problem-solving.
