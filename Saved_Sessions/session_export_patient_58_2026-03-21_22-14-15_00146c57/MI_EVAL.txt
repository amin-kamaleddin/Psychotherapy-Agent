**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior (waking/getting to school) and repeatedly brings discussion back to practical, behavior-specific steps. Questions and summaries remain tied to the specific goal throughout.

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Actively solicits Jamal's input at every decision point and builds the plan from his choices, keeping the relationship equal and cooperative. Frames reminders and follow-up as support rather than control.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequently elicits Jamal's own motivations, reasons, and confidence, using open questions and confidence scaling to draw out change talk. Avoids supplying reasons and reinforces Jamal's language and commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys a collaborative stance and shared decision-making, asking what Jamal wants and tailoring the plan to his preferences. Jamal's plan and preferences drive the concrete decisions made.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits, reinforces, and strengthens change talk with targeted questions, reflections, and follow-up on confidence and barriers. Secures a specific, concrete plan and commitment to check in.

**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirects toward change by reframing external motivations toward personal reasons. Could increase use of explicit double-sided reflections and probe barriers indicated by sustain talk to further reduce resistance.
