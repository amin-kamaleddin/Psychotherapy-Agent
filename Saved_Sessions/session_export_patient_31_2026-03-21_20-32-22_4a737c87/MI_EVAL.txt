# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive; opens warmly, invites the patient's perspective, affirms choices, and normalizes ambivalence without labeling or pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures practical and emotional ambivalence; could deepen empathic responses by reflecting stress/fatigue and using more complex reflections about cravings and soreness.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking reduction with practical, stepwise planning (NRT dosing, timing, coping strategies) and returns to the plan when barriers arise.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as joint problem-solving—invites the patient to choose barriers to address and co-designs solutions that fit his constraints and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's sense of choice by offering multiple options, asking preferences, and reinforcing that change is the patient's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation from within the patient, using scaling and eliciting concrete internal reasons to create a patient-owned, specific plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance—asks permission before providing information, offers choices, and treats the patient as the expert in co-constructing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly elicits and amplifies change talk and specific plans (e.g., 4 mg gum, one-week experiment), and elicits concrete commitment language and steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without confrontation and shifts to pragmatic harm-reduction; could more strategically use double-sided reflections earlier to explicitly validate both sustain and change perspectives.
