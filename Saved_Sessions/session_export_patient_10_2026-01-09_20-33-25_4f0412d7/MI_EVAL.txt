# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the client's experience, validating both disappointment and confidence in a warm, nonjudgmental way.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing feelings and ambivalence throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior (attending the interview) and moves the conversation logically from barrier identification to concrete action planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in cooperative problem-solving, inviting preferences and co-constructing a plan while alternating between offering tools and seeking the client's choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice by offering multiple options, asking permission before interventions, and leaving final decisions (time, format, role‑play) to the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits practical motivation and invites role‑play and planning, but could do more to draw out the client's internal reasons and personal meaning for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fosters a collaborative stance and invites input, though at times the clinician supplies scripts and solutions rather than eliciting the client's own wording first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk through doable options and planning, but misses some opportunities to directly amplify the client's own reasons and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to solutions effectively, though responses are sometimes more prescriptive than reflective (e.g., lists and scripts).
