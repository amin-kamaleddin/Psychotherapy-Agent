# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently shows unconditional positive regard with safety checks, nonjudgmental language, normalization, and affirmations that convey respect and support throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and validation of Kenji's experience, but missed some opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections of underlying emotions and specific moments.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on alcohol use with targeted assessment and moves efficiently to a pragmatic, concrete plan (tracking, limits, coping strategies, PCP contact).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—invites Kenji's input, co-creates tools (simple tracking sheet, tailored PCP script), and frames the session as joint problem-solving rather than directive advice.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, honoring reluctance to see a doctor, and framing decisions as Kenji's to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation via open questions and eliciting a small goal, but the clinician often shifts to offering information and strategies rather than drawing out Kenji's internal motives, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong partnership evident—asks permission, offers options, incorporates Kenji's preferences into the plan, and treats him as the expert on his life.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and small concrete goals, but could more strategically amplify desire/ability/reasons/commitment language to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by validating reluctance, offering acceptable alternatives, and reframing slips nonjudgmentally; could add more double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
