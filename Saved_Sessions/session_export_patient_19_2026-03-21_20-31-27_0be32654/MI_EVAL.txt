# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and warmth, normalizes ambivalence, and affirms the patient's efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and accurate reflections of ambivalence and triggers, though at times moves quickly to solutions and misses opportunities to deepen emotional reflection (shame/anxiety).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on gambling and debt-related goals with repeated scaling, targeted problem-solving, and concrete action planning tied to the patient's priorities.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as a joint problem-solver, offers options, invites patient choice, and co-designs texts and contingency plans rather than adopting an expert stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports autonomy by offering options, asking permission, and validating hesitancy, reinforcing patient ownership of decisions and plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits reasons for change and links behavior to values (travel, finances) using scaling, but could more systematically amplify intrinsic motives and past successes to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a strong partnership by asking collaborative questions, inviting the patient to choose and shape plans, and co-creating realistic next steps.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and strengthens change talk through scaling, concrete planning, affirmations, and shaping client language into actionable steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change, though could use more double-sided reflections and elicitation of downsides to further reduce sustain statements' pull.
