# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (early framing, validating both sides, explicit acceptance statement) and offered options rather than admonishing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections that allowed expansion, though reflections stayed pragmatic and missed deeper exploration of affect and emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session focused on avoiding repeat DUIs with clear, behaviorally specific strategies and moved logically from options to selection to implementation planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint endeavor using inclusive language, soliciting input at every decision point, and honoring the client's preferences rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the client's choice and control by offering options, affirming his right to keep friendships, checking readiness, and reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical motivations and concrete solutions (license importance, cab plan, readiness rating) but could further evoke deeper personal values to anchor and amplify commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear collaborative stance, invited the client to choose among options and co‑construct solutions, and avoided defaulting to an expert prescription.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and shaped change talk with open questions and a commitment request, generating concrete plans, though could have amplified and summarized key change statements more strategically.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it but relied on a neutral, pragmatic approach and missed opportunities for double‑sided reflections or stronger linkage of minimizations to consequences.
