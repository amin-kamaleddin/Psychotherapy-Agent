# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental tone (accurate framing, normalizing language, compassionate backup for slips). These behaviors communicated warmth and acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking that validated Anisa’s experience and built rapport. Missed an opportunity to deepen exploration of intense shame (e.g., 'like a leper') with a more complex reflection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and progressively narrowed to a concrete, immediate target (evening routine and a nightly plan). The sequence was purposeful and action-oriented.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction unfolded as a clear partnership with frequent invitations to choose and co-construction of plans and messages. The clinician adjusted recommendations to Anisa’s concerns and treated her as an expert on her life.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and reinforcing that decisions were Anisa’s to make. Language avoided pressure and honored her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intrinsic motivation through open questions and the readiness scale, and repeatedly tied suggestions to Anisa’s values and past successes. Client-generated reasons and resources were used to shape the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance with joint decision-making and co-created wording, but the clinician supplied many concrete suggestions rather than eliciting Anisa’s own brainstormed options first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk (daughter, past quit, readiness scale) and translated it into doable steps. Could further amplify change statements and prompt elaboration to strengthen commitment and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and strategically redirected to problem-solving with nonjudgmental reframes and a recovery plan. Could enhance this by using double-sided reflections and exploring the function of sustain talk more deeply.
