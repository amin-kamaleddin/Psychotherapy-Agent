# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with repeated validation and offers of practical help that convey unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses good empathic statements and brief reflections, but often shifts quickly to advice rather than exploring emotional nuance or deeper feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on preparing the patient to ask the flatmate to move out, with interventions tightly aligned to that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works jointly on phrasing and role-play and solicits reactions, but sometimes supplies ready-made solutions instead of inviting the patient to generate options first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers choices, asks permission, and frames actions as optional, though early suggestions of coercive consequences slightly undermine autonomy support.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete commitment behaviors and role-play but relies heavily on clinician-provided scripts rather than eliciting the patient's own motivations, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance by co-creating language and offering role-play, but could better emphasize the patient's expertise and invite his wording first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces change intentions through scripting and rehearsal and refines language to match comfort, but misses opportunities to ask questions that deepen and amplify the patient's own motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges guilt and softens wording later in the session, but early recommendations for consequential actions risk escalating sustain talk and could be tempered with double-sided reflections.
