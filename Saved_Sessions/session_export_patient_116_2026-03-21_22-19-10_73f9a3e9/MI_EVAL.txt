# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (validation, normalizing barriers, affirming expertise). Avoided blaming and respected Samir’s cautions and humor throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed clear empathic listening and accurate reflections of Samir’s content and values. Some reflections stayed surface-level and could probe deeper affect (fear of dependence, shame) to access richer motivation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a focused, outcome-oriented agenda centered on medication adherence and practical strategies. Kept the session goal-directed with concrete plans (4-week trial, measurable goals, log, check-ins) without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine teamwork by co-creating the trial, log, and check-in schedule and repeatedly soliciting Samir’s input. Responsively adapted materials (mood line, ‘Med helped?’, phone-friendly format).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices, emphasizing Samir’s control, and respecting his limits and cautious language. Avoided coercion and reinforced ownership of the plan (start date, phone number, stop rules).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited Samir’s values and reasons (staying able to work, not being a burden) which guided goal-setting. Missed some opportunities to draw out more internal motivation and confidence (targeted DARN-C elicitation or rulers).

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operated clearly as a partnership by ceding expertise to Samir and inviting him to choose goals and options. Shared decision-making meaningfully shaped the plan and demonstrated power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change talk and commitments (4-week trial, goals, log) through options and goal prompts. Could have amplified and selectively reflected spontaneous change statements more to build momentum and stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolled with resistance, validated concerns, offered alternatives, and proposed a time-limited trial to reduce sustain talk. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to more actively resolve ambivalence.
