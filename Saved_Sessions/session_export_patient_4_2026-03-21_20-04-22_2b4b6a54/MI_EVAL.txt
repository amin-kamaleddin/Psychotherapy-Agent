# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, validating the patient’s distress and acknowledging fears without blaming or minimising.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate paraphrase and perspective-taking that likely helped the patient feel heard, though could have probed more deeply into emotional impact and isolation.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on obtaining appropriate investigations and communicating with the GP, offering concrete actions and frequent summarizing to maintain momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Engages in collaborative problem-solving with options, scripts, and role‑play, but collaboration is somewhat asymmetrical because the clinician supplies much of the concrete wording.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports choice by presenting options, repeatedly asking what the patient wants next, and seeking permission before role‑playing, reinforcing patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits the patient’s desire for investigations but does not consistently draw out intrinsic motivations, values, confidence, or amplify change language beyond requesting tests.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and invites patient decisions, but occasionally defaults to expert-driven recommendations rather than first eliciting the patient’s own wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Creates space for a decision focus and expresses options, but mostly supplies scripts and advice instead of eliciting, reflecting, and amplifying the patient’s own change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and acknowledges the patient’s resistance to a purely psychological explanation, yet uses only modest strategies (few double‑sided reflections) to shift sustain talk toward change.
