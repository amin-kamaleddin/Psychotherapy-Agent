### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, normalizing concerns and offering supportive closure; adding more individualized affirmations (e.g., naming ambivalence) could further strengthen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with reflective listening that captured content and some affect, but many reflections were brief and missed deeper emotion or ambivalence; more complex, affect-focused reflections would improve empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on the vaccination decision and moved logically from concerns to information, problem-solving, rehearsal, and a concrete plan (Friday after shift), effectively guiding the patient to action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by inviting input, co-creating scripts and reminders, rehearsing responses, and accepting the patient’s choices; session felt like joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice by offering options, asking permission before providing information, and avoiding pressure, while shaping plans to fit the patient's preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited values and produced change talk that led to commitment, but relied more on information and practical tips than on deeper elicitation of the patient's intrinsic reasons; more open questions to draw out personal motivations would help.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited the patient's input, asked permission, and co-created a workable plan, but could more explicitly acknowledge the patient's expertise about his schedule and build the plan more from his suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and amplified change talk around protecting grandparents and concrete planning, yet leaned on providing information rather than consistently using strategic elicitation and amplifying language to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
