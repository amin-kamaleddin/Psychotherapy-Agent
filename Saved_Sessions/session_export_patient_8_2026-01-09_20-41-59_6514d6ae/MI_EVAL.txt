# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently displayed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, using validating reflections and normalizing ambivalence throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and frequent reflective listening, paraphrasing the patient's ambivalence and matching emotional tone effectively.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing late-night scrolling and study phone use, returning to the agenda and supplying concrete behavioral strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by co-creating tools and plans, checking preferences, and adapting suggestions to the patient's constraints and style.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported client choice by offering options, asking permission before advice, and emphasizing that the patient decides what to try and how to adapt it.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicited the patient's motivations and supports (scaling, specific supports), but could probe deeper into values and amplify change statements to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as collaborative, inviting choices and privileging the patient's expertise while jointly developing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with scaling and a concrete small step, though more deliberate amplification and summarizing of change language would deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk and offered compromise strategies, but could more systematically use double-sided reflections or strategic reframing to soften sustain talk further.
