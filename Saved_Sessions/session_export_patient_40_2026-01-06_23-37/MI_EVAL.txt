### Empathy

**Comments:** Excellent perspective-taking and reflective listening—matched tone/affect, acknowledged shame and pride, and repeatedly checked in about urges and feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on substance-use goals and relapse prevention, repeatedly returning to concrete, time-bound steps and translating values into specific actions.

### Collaboration

### Evocation
