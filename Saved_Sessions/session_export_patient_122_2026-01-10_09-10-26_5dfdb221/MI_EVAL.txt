### Empathy

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

**Comments:** Used evocative prompts (confidence ruler, doable options) and elicited contingency plans, drawing out some internal motivation. Could deepen evocation by asking more values‑ and consequence‑focused questions to build stronger commitment language.

### Partnership

**Comments:** Actively fostered client expertise and shared power—Sofia generated key elements while the clinician structured and supported them. The interaction consistently reflected genuine partnership.
