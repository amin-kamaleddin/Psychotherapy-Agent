# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with frequent affirmations and warm, validating language. Normalises difficulty and avoids blame, supporting the client's dignity and progress.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately paraphrasing strengths and barriers. Could deepen emotional exploration (e.g., the feelings behind 'pride' and nervousness) using more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on anger management tied to alcohol use and moves logically from past success to concrete planning. Produces specific action steps and implementation details that advance behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—repeatedly invites choices, role-plays partner replies, and co-designs the plan with the client. Treats the client as an equal partner whose preferences shape the session.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and ownership by offering options, asking preferences, and respecting the client's pace. Avoids pressuring language and reinforces that the client controls decisions and timing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation from within the client through past-success exploration, scaling, and rehearsal of implementation intentions. Repeatedly draws out and strengthens internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys that expertise about change resides with the client, asks permission, and invites the client's direction. Co-constructs concrete plans and consistently checks the client's preferences and confidence.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reinforces, and amplifies change talk using targeted questions, reflections, scaling, and rehearsal. Prompts concrete planning and commitment, effectively deepening the client's change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects toward workable change with alternative strategies, naming sustaining forces without reinforcing them. Could more often use double-sided reflections and explore reasons for maintaining current drinking to tip ambivalence further toward change.
