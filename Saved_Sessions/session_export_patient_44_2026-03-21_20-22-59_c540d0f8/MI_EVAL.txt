# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation from the outset, making the patient feel heard and safe. Explicitly acknowledges successes and struggles without blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking that track the patient's shifting emotions and ambivalence. Empathy deepens trust and elicits further disclosure and planning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, appropriate focus on exercise routines and implementation intentions throughout the session. Conversation progresses logically from review to goal-setting, troubleshooting, and a specific follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co-constructed with frequent invitations for the patient's input and integration of his choices into the plan. Alternates between offering options and following the patient's lead, supporting shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly preserves the patient's sense of choice by offering options and phrasing suggestions as invitations rather than commands. Respects hesitancy and frames strategies as testable supports.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own motivations, benefits, and concrete reasons for change (energy, less pain, looser pants). Prompts the patient to generate supports and realistic plans, strengthening internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters an explicit partnership by asking preferences, co-creating implementation details, and offering mutually agreed accountability options. Positions the patient as the expert on his life and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully evokes and amplifies change talk through choice questions, confidence scaling, and requests for specific implementation intentions and success indicators. Reinforces and builds commitment language toward the agreed plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handles sustain talk respectfully and redirects it toward practical problem-solving and compassionate fallbacks, preventing it from dominating the session. Could increase use of double-sided reflections and deeper exploration of comparison/shame and dietary urges to further transform ambivalence.
