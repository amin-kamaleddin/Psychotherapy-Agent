# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect, validated frustration and normalized constraints without blame. No stigmatizing or minimizing language was used.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking focused on pragmatic constraints and frustration. Could deepen empathy with more complex, feeling-focused reflections to explore underlying emotions driving ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate direction toward diet/lunch behaviors with agenda-setting and iterative concrete planning. Kept the conversation focused and tied plans to the client's stated constraints and health goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative: co-designed a feasible plan, negotiated logistics, and adjusted content to the patient's preferences. Tone and language positioned the clinician as a supportive partner rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, asking permission, and honoring boundaries. Reinforced ownership of the plan and avoided pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively evoked the patient's own motivations and solutions through open questions, scaling, and requests for specifics. Drew strategies and reasons for change from the patient rather than prescribing them.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a strong partnership stance by inviting priorities, offering choices, and co-creating the plan. Repeatedly sought and honored the patient's input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk, asking for concrete details, confidence ratings, and operational steps. Reinforced and strengthened patient-generated intentions and practical commitments.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk nonconfrontationally and shifted to realistic strategies that respected constraints. Could improve by occasionally using double-sided reflections or explicitly naming ambivalence to further soften sustain talk and bolster commitment.
