# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard, used validating language, and avoided judgment throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflective listening and perspective-taking, including checking safety and adapting tone to the client's cues.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on resolving the group project issue and guided the session to specific, time-bound action steps and sequencing.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Structured the interaction as shared problem-solving by asking for the client's input at each decision point and tailoring materials only with consent.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, respecting the client's refusal to practice aloud, and phrasing suggestions tentatively.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some client preferences and commitments but relied more on clinician-provided solutions than exploring deeper personal motives, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered power-sharing by checking preferences, offering choices, and implementing the client's decisions, showing genuine co-construction of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited desire and behavioral commitments and provided concrete steps, but missed opportunities to amplify intrinsic motivations and explore importance/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing avoidance and offered gentler alternatives which reduced sustain talk, though more double-sided reflections could have further softened reluctance.
