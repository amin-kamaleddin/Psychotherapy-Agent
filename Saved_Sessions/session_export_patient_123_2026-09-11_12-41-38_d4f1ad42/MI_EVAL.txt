# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental warmth, normalizing ambivalence and validating the patient's priorities without moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and perspective-taking capture both sides of ambivalence and subtle hesitancy, with responses that reassure rather than challenge.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on protecting school/time outdoors and reducing evening alcohol exposure, guiding the conversation toward specific, actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly a partnership: the clinician solicits input, mirrors the patient's words, and offers options that the patient selects, co-creating the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors the patient's choice and control by offering options and letting her decide supports and reminder format, reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own motivations and practical reasons for change, leading to self-endorsed strategies and concrete plans generated by the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the session as a joint process, invites the patient's ideas and choices, and avoids a prescriptive stance while co-deciding plan details.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk with open questions and reflections, resulting in multiple statements of intent and specific commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently acknowledges barriers, but relies mostly on neutral reflections rather than targeted strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections) to deliberately soften sustain talk.
