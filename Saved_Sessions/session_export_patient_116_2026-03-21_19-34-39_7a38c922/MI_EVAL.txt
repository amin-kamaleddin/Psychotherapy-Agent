# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating and normalizing Samir's concerns without criticism. This warmth supported engagement and safety throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and named concrete experiences (fog, hassle), showing understanding of Samir's perspective. Reflections remained somewhat surface-level and could have probed emotional consequences or ambivalence more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on medication nonadherence and the fog, guiding toward specific, measurable actions (script, tracking, 25/5 timer). The clinician kept the session tightly linked to practical next steps and the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Modeled a collaborative stance by inviting preferences, co-creating the phone note and tracking template, and respecting Samir's choices. Decisions were made jointly and Samir was positioned as an active partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirmed Samir's choice and avoided pressure, offering a menu of options and honoring his pace and refusals. Supported partial engagement and client ownership of any changes.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation and micro-commitments (saving the note, agreeing to track and try a timer) with personally relevant options. Missed opportunities to probe values, ask scaling questions, or amplify intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shared power and solicited Samir's input on focus and next steps, allowing him to shape the plan. At times the clinician offered many suggestions rather than eliciting Samir's own solutions, which limited full partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Asked useful questions and elicited tentative change-oriented language and small experiments, but did not consistently deepen or amplify emergent change talk. Lacked follow-ups, scaling, and strategic evocation to strengthen desire, ability, or reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing and rolled with resistance, reframing and offering low-effort options to reduce sustain talk. Could have used more strategic double-sided reflections or contrast to more actively tip the balance toward change.
