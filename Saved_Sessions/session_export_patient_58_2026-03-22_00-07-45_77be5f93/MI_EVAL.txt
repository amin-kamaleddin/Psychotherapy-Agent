### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, avoiding blame or shame throughout the interaction.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Used accurate, timely reflections that captured both content and feeling, including empathic reflection of sustain talk to show Jamal was understood.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on school attendance/punctuality and built toward a concrete, measurable one-week trial without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan with Jamal, consistently invited his input and preferences, and framed the conversation as joint problem-solving rather than instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered meaningful choices, emphasized change at Jamal's pace, and reinforced his ownership of the decision to foster internal control and motivation.

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated Jamal as the expert on his life, solicited his preferences, and shared power in decision-making with repeated checks for his view.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk through questions about past success, doable steps, and concrete planning, amplifying Jamal's owned change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk with double-sided reflections but at times accepted resistance and shifted to planning instead of more strategically reframing it into stronger change language.
