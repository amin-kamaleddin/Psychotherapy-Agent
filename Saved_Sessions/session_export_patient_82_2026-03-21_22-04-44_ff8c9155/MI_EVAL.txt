# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Lukas’s experience throughout the session. Avoided blaming or shaming and validated normal nervousness during role-play.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and summaries and validated feelings, helping Lukas articulate worries about teammates and performance. Could deepen empathic depth by exploring underlying emotions (e.g., shame, fear of rejection) with more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on early alcohol use and boundary-setting and moved the session from exploration to concrete planning and rehearsal. Questions and interventions stayed tightly linked to protecting grades, practice, and social connection.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicited Lukas’s input at every step and co-created refusal lines, backup plans, and role-play practice. The interaction reflected genuine back-and-forth and shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Lukas’s right to choose and offered options rather than directives, asking permission for role-play and inviting his preferences. Language reinforced ownership of decisions without coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Oriented toward Lukas’s own motivations with open invitations and prompts to generate his own lines and plans. Could strengthen evocation by asking more targeted 'what'/'why' questions and using scaling to draw out importance and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Established a strong collaborative stance, asking agenda-setting questions and inviting choices about practice and strategy. Consistently positioned Lukas as an active agent in shaping the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk (leaning toward not drinking, protecting practice/grades) and asked preference questions. Missed opportunities to amplify motivations and elicit confidence or commitment language with scaling or summary amplification.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not push Lukas away for ambivalence, but sometimes offered middle-ground options that left sustain talk unchallenged. Could use double-sided reflections and selectively highlight change talk to more actively tip the balance away from sustain language.
