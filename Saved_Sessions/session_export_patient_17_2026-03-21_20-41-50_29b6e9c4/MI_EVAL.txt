# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently communicated respect and nonjudgmental support, with repeated affirmations and normalization of NRT that reduced blame. Reflections validated mixed feelings and strengths effectively.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic understanding by naming emotions and triggers and using accurate reflections. Missed a few opportunities for deeper affective reflections on intense shame and the emotional burden of being a single mother.

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on quitting—identifying triggers, choosing a single manageable target, and developing an implementation plan. Questions stayed focused on behavior change and coping with slips.

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership

**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance through repeated solicitations of the patient's preferences and co‑creation of the plan. Avoided an expert‑only approach and emphasized shared decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk by offering choices, affirming steps, using confidence scaling, and encouraging concrete planning. Clinician reflected and reinforced language in favor of change effectively.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk and used normalizing/reframing strategies and planning for slips. Relied more on neutral reflections and pragmatic problem‑solving rather than strategic double‑sided reflections to further diminish sustain talk.
