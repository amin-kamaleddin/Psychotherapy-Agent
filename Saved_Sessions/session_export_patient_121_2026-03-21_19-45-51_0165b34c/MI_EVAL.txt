# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed respect and nonjudgmental support with validations and a safety check, but some language was practical and procedural rather than deeply personalized.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Solid empathic statements and brief reflections helped rapport, though there were missed opportunities for more feeling-focused or layered reflections of Sofia's guarded vulnerability.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on rumors/harassment and safety with concrete, goal-linked outputs (paste-ready email, incident log) and follow-up steps, avoiding drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction felt like a genuine joint effort—clinician adapted wording, formatted documents per Sofia's requests, and checked back frequently to implement her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Sofia's sense of choice by offering options, asking which she preferred, and avoiding pressuring language, reinforcing her control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete commitments and actions (sending email, compiling log) but focused mainly on practical supports rather than drawing out Sofia's internal motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance and followed Sofia's preferences, but occasionally moved quickly into giving instructions instead of co-creating strategies from Sofia's ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced concrete change steps and commitment, yet provided limited exploration of personal reasons, values, or confidence scaling to amplify change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validated and normalized trying but did not consistently use strategies like double-sided reflections or reframing to soften Sofia's repeated sustain talk, so redirection was uneven.
