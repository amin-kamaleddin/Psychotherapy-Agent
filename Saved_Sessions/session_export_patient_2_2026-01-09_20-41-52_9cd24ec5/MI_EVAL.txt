### Acceptance

### Empathy

### Direction

**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on evening smoking reduction and guided the conversation toward concrete, actionable steps (gum, 10‑minute delay, reminders, nicotine gum backup).

### Collaboration

**Comments:** Worked as co‑partners with inclusive language and frequent solicitation of Aisha’s input, negotiating decisions (reminder wording, start date, dosing) rather than imposing them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
