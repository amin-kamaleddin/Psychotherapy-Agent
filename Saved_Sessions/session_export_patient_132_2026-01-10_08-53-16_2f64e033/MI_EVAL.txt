# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows respectful, nonjudgmental support and normalizes Yasmin's experience while validating her goals; sometimes becomes directive with detailed meal plans, reducing warm, individualized acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and reflective listening (normalizing the binge/restrict cycle and addressing concerns); missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections of emotions and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session clearly focused on moving from crash dieting to sustainable eating and activity with concrete, stepwise plans; consistently steers toward actionable behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Often invites preferences, tailors resources, and co-constructs safety/support scripts, fostering joint effort; lengthy clinician-led lists and teaching moments sometimes make the encounter feel more expert-driven than co-created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Affirms choice, offers options, and asks what feels doable, supporting Yasmin's control; occasional directive phrasing and prescriptive 'quick plans' limit stronger autonomy promotion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses open prompts that elicit some client-generated language and a doable plan, but often supplies solutions instead of consistently drawing out intrinsic motivations, values, and past strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes collaborative moments and checks for preferences, yet much of the session is clinician-driven with prescriptive instructions; greater shared decision-making would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits initial change talk (a small doable plan) but misses opportunities to amplify and explore desire, reasons, ability, and commitment, moving quickly to advice and tools.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Redirects sustain talk at times toward healthier practices but sometimes legitimizes weight‑loss goals with concrete targets and calorie tweaks, and rarely uses MI-specific softening techniques like double-sided reflection.
