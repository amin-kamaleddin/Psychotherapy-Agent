# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes difficulty, and validates progress without shaming; praise is used to affirm effort and honesty.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, capturing shame and ambivalence; some reflections are brief/formulaic and the clinician moves quickly to action rather than deepening emotional content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on concrete target behaviors (medication safety, meetings, coping) and ties choices back to Elias’s values and goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a genuine team effort with permission-seeking and rehearsal; occasional clinician-led solutions or specific instructions slightly shift power but are usually offered as options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers options, checks readiness, and respects the client’s preferences, emphasizing that decisions belong to Elias without using pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and resources using open questions and the confidence ruler, and eliciting concrete strategies; could further explore deeper values and amplify spontaneous change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session is largely collaborative with co‑constructed plans and frequent invitations for input, though offering specific wording and steps at times shifts some influence back to the clinician.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk well (direct question about one small change, affirmations, confidence ruler, planning); could more often amplify change language and elicit firmer commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and avoids challenging it, but uses few strategic techniques (double‑sided reflections, reframing) to gently shift sustain talk toward change and tends to move quickly into action planning.
