# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's experience, avoiding blame or pressure throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate empathic reflections that capture both content and feeling and link empathic statements to practical barriers to move the conversation forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the target behavior (making a medical contact) with questions and options tied to actionable next steps without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative, inviting the patient's input at each decision point and co‑creating solutions like scripts and role‑play while checking comfort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the patient's choice by offering multiple options, asking which feels most manageable, and honoring the patient's preference about who should call.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation and reasons for change through open questions that generate the patient's own statements about why and how to act.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Partnership is explicit and steady, with language that shares power and expertise and positions the clinician as working with the patient rather than directing them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and strengthens several statements favoring change and offers concrete options, though there were missed opportunities to amplify commitment and elicit explicit timing or confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reflects sustain talk without confrontation and effectively pivots to problem‑solving, but could use more double‑sided reflections or reframing to further soften resistance.
