# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Isabella's avoidance, affirming small steps and avoiding admonishment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening and perspective-taking with timely reflections and normalizations, but could use more complex reflections and summaries to capture ambivalence and deeper feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on increasing community engagement and used goal-directed, coherent interventions tied to concrete, achievable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was collaborative with co‑constructed planning and role‑play, though frequent provision of scripts and rules slightly reduced opportunities for Isabella to generate content herself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized choice and preserved control by offering multiple options, normalizing non‑sending, and avoiding pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out small motivations and willingness to try with practical prompts, but relied more on planning than eliciting deeper personal reasons, values, or confidence ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative stance and invited preferences, yet frequent clinician-provided examples and rules shifted some of the work toward clinician-led solutions rather than fully client-generated ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced tentative change talk and provided manageable next steps, but could amplify and deepen change language by selectively reflecting and asking for importance/confidence and reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and skillfully reframed it into neutral or forward-moving options, though more double-sided reflections could further highlight the change side of ambivalence.
