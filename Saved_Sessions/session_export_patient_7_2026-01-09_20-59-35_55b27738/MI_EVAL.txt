# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard with timely, nonjudgmental statements and validation. Could occasionally pair acceptance with explicit affirmations of strengths to deepen positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, layered reflections and empathic summaries that matched the patient's content and emotional tone, and paced to the patient's responses. Tolerated silence and demonstrated strong empathic engagement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on a concrete target (privacy/interruptions) and consistently steered toward small, achievable actions like a door note. Effectively narrowed from broader conflict to a specific, doable step.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited the patient's input at multiple decision points and co-created solutions while deferring to her expertise. Avoided an expert-driven stance and used inclusive language to support joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported the patient's choice and control, honored refusals, and framed options rather than commands. Respected pace and limits, reinforcing patient autonomy throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited internal reasons for change and prior successes with evocative questions that drew out intrinsic motivation. Could probe values more deeply and link the small step to broader goals to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly positioned the work as collaborative and repeatedly checked what felt least risky or doable for the patient. Honored her preferences and allowed them to shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced tentative change talk and small steps (e.g., 'I might do it') and highlighted past successes. Could more often amplify and explore change language, ask about confidence/importance, and elicit stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and pivoted toward low-risk options to not reinforce sustain talk, validating reluctance while offering alternatives. Missed opportunities to use double-sided reflections and more strategic reframing to further soften sustain talk.
