# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized Hana’s experience, affirming effort and strengths without blame or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and sensitive responses (offered to coach and breathe together), but reflections were often concise and shifted quickly to problem-solving rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on starting study sprints and exam prep with concrete, actionable steps and a logical progression from planning to follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as a partnership: the clinician co-created the sprint, offered choices (alarm wording, coaching), and consistently solicited Hana’s preferences instead of directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported autonomy by offering options, emphasizing Hana’s control (pause, finish any amount = win), and clarifying clinician limits on initiating contact.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Built on Hana’s past success and elicited small, plan-focused motivations, but did not sufficiently explore core values, emotional meaning, or elicit stronger confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative stance—asked permission, gave choices, and shared power in decision-making so Hana’s preferences shaped the plan throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk through concrete planning and highlighting small wins, yet missed opportunities to deepen desire/reasons/importance or draw out stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing fears and offering manageable steps, but could have used more double-sided reflections and explicit acknowledgment of ambivalence to further soften sustain talk.
