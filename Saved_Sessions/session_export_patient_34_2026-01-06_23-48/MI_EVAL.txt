# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing Dylan's goals and offering options rather than shaming. The clinician maintains respectful, positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking that reduces defensiveness. Could deepen empathic responses by reflecting underlying emotions (fear, embarrassment) rather than focusing mainly on logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is tightly goal-directed, consistently focused on the conduct incident and preparing Dylan for the meeting. Every intervention is practical and tied to the desired outcomes.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction feels like a joint effort with role‑play and tailored lines that address Dylan's concerns. At times the many ready-made scripts slightly reduce the sense of equal co-creation.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offers clear choices and invites Dylan to choose approaches, supporting his sense of control. The clinician avoids pressuring and foregrounds Dylan's decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of Dylan's perspective and willingness to remediate is present, but the clinician moves quickly into advising and scripting. More systematic exploration of values, motives, and discrepancy would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong collaborative stance—asks what would help and co-constructs wording during role-play. Occasional unsolicited scripted lines slightly reduce full ownership by Dylan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts to elicit change talk (offers education/service) are present but largely clinician‑scripted rather than spontaneously generated. Missed opportunities to amplify Dylan's own reasons for change or explore what staying in residence means to him.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Appropriately avoids arguing and redirects sustain talk with alternative phrasing and normalization. However, the clinician rarely uses MI techniques like double-sided reflections or pros/cons exploration to actively soften sustain talk.
