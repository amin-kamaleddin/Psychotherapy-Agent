# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys respect and a nonjudgmental stance, validates feelings and praises small steps; could add more individualized normalization and more frequent affirmations of strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and useful summaries that show understanding of feelings and patterns; could use more complex reflections to capture ambivalence and deeper emotional drivers.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing alcohol-related risk with concrete, behaviorally specific goals and safety advice, consistently linking goals to relevant concerns like the fall.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session feels joint and co-creative (pub-plan, tracking choices, tactic negotiation); could more consistently invite the patient's solutions first and explicitly summarize the agreed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering choices, asking what feels doable, and reassuring about control over decisions and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient's own reasons and plans and highlighted discrepancy to generate change talk; could increase evocation by exploring values, importance, and confidence with scaling or deeper questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats the patient as an equal partner through shared decision-making and co-creation of plans; could further shift power by eliciting the agenda and ideas for change earlier and confirming ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change statements and offered small goals, but only intermittently amplified or reflected them and missed targeted follow-ups about importance/confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed patterns toward change, but missed opportunities to validate reasons for sustaining and to use double-sided reflections to reduce resistance.
