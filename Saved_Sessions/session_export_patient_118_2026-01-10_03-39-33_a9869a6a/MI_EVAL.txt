# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, repeatedly honoring the patient's wish not to discuss quitting while offering practical help.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening with accurate content reflections and validation, though reflections were mostly pragmatic and could deepen to capture underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session clearly focused on the patient's prioritized goal (dental care) and concrete next steps, while deliberately de-emphasizing smoking to respect the patient's agenda.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong equal partnership throughout: asked for preferences, co-produced documents and scripts, and consistently followed the patient's directions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered options and emphasized the patient's control and choice, avoiding coercive language and affirming the right to decline discussion of quitting.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did little to elicit internal motivations for change, missing low-risk opportunities to ask brief evoking questions about oral health or hesitations about smoking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction clearly positioned patient as an active partner with shared decision-making, co-creating materials and asking about format and content preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to elicit or amplify change talk about smoking; respected autonomy but missed simple, low-pressure follow-ups on potential openings.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided pushing back or confronting resistance and shifted focus to patient-requested tasks, though could use double-sided reflections to further acknowledge ambivalence.
