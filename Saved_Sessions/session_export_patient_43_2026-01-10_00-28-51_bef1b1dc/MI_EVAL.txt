# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used consistently nonjudgmental, validating language and normalized ambivalence, acknowledging Eric's priorities and affirming his choice to test a small change rather than pushing abstinence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening and perspective-taking with accurate, specific paraphrases and summaries that captured Eric's feelings and concerns without confronting him.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear behavioral focus on reducing indoor smoking, guiding the conversation from exploration to a concrete, time-limited plan that matched the client's readiness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative interaction that co‑constructed the plan using Eric's own ideas, invited input, and used inclusive language to work alongside him rather than prescribe solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasized Eric's choice and control (e.g., 'testing, not committing to quit'), avoided pressure, and supported incremental change the client selected himself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical reasons for change (less nagging, less cough, save money) and invited a low‑burden experiment, but could have probed deeper values and asked for importance/confidence ratings to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as a joint exploration, invited Eric to lead decisions, and co‑created actionable supports while respecting his limits and preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out and amplified specific change talk and past success (porch rule during pregnancy) and operational supports, but missed opportunities to elicit intrinsic reasons and explicit commitment/confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and reframed the goal as a short test to steer away from sustain talk, though many reflections echoed sustain arguments instead of using double‑sided reflections or highlighting discrepancy more explicitly.
