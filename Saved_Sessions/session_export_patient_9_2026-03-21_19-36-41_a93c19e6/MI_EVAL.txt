# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician establishes and sustains unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, validating Amina's concerns and priorities throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Multiple accurate empathic reflections show understanding of Amina's anxiety and overwhelm, but the clinician moves quickly to problem-solving without deeply exploring the emotional texture of her fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on protecting the child and managing co-parent conflict, with practical, behavior-focused strategies consistently linked to Amina's stated values.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician invites drafting scripts and rehearsal and adapts to requests, creating a team-like dynamic, but much content is clinician-directed rather than co-constructed with Amina.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Offers options and asks permission at points, supporting choice, but recommendations often come before eliciting Amina's preferences and many scripts are clinician-supplied instead of client-generated.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Aligns interventions with Amina's values and invites rehearsal, but rarely elicits her internal motivations, past successes, or confidence; the session relies on clinician-provided solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—asking what to focus on and offering options—but interaction remains somewhat expert-driven with limited invitation for Amina to craft her own wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change-oriented language by connecting suggestions to child safety and inviting practice, but misses opportunities to ask open questions or reflect and amplify Amina's own reasons and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates sustain-talk and provides concrete counter-responses and scripts that defuse it effectively, though more double-sided reflections could better acknowledge ambivalence.
