# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician conveyed unconditional positive regard and used consistently respectful, nonjudgmental language while acknowledging Milan's perspective and limits.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate reflections and summaries captured both content and emotion (embarrassment, concern about trust), demonstrating strong perspective-taking and attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed focused on alcohol use at social gatherings with concrete, behavior-linked goals and clear movement toward planning and skills practice.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—clinician invited Milan to co-create strategies, used inclusive language, and adapted suggestions to his stated comfort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician reinforced choice by offering options, asking permission for advice/role-play, normalizing ambivalence, and avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited personally relevant reasons and value-based reflections that produced change language, but could probe deeper into meaning and use scaling to amplify motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently shared power—inviting ideas, asking which options felt doable, and framing suggestions as choices rather than directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited multiple instances of change talk and practical strategies, but stopped short of systematically amplifying or converting them into firmer commitment language or scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged and reframed with double-sided reflections without confrontation, though more strategic selective reflections and summaries could further soften it.
