# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and validating of the patient's constraints, normalizing his position. Tone was efficient and solution-directed rather than warmer or explicitly naming emotions, which limited unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and accurate reflections that helped the patient disclose concrete plans. Could deepen empathic exploration by naming emotions like frustration or fatigue rather than focusing mainly on logistics.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on weight-management behaviors tied to the health concern and steadily moved toward a concrete, time-limited plan. Every clinician turn returned to practical, goal-directed strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with the patient's preferences shaping decisions and the clinician inviting input. The clinician did not assume unilateral authority and offered tasks contingent on patient direction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, respected the patient's decisions, and sought instruction about next steps, affirming ownership of the plan. Avoided pressuring and honored cost and communication preferences.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change language and practical commitments that moved the patient from ambivalence to a specific trial. Could have further evoked intrinsic motivation by exploring values, importance, and confidence more deeply.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Collaborative stance with frequent preference-checking and deference to patient choices. At times the clinician led with pre-structured options rather than co-creating alternatives from the patient's spontaneous ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk through choice questions, affirmations, and summaries that produced concrete planning. To improve, more targeted probes about desire, reasons, ability, and confidence would deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by acknowledging barriers and shifting to problem-solving and low-burden options. Relied more on logistical redirection than explicit softening techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) that would more directly reduce sustain talk.
