### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows clear respect and nonjudgmental regard with affirmations and normalization that reduce blame. Could add more explicit unconditional acceptance and explore Amina’s emotions (e.g., embarrassment or worry) to reach a top score.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately mirroring ambivalence and concerns. To improve, deepen emotional reflections and use more complex reflections that capture the emotional nuance of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on phone use, sleep, and study and moves logically from ambivalence to concrete action planning. This strong task focus effectively supports behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a generally equal, collaborative interaction by co-creating messages and inviting choices. Collaboration is slightly weakened when detailed technical instructions are offered without first checking the client's preference for that level of help.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports the client's sense of choice by offering multiple options and inviting preferences at each decision point. Validates hesitations and avoids pressuring, reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits Amina’s own motivations and operational solutions, drawing planning from within the client. Could strengthen by linking changes to deeper personal values and eliciting stronger commitment/confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters client expertise and shared decision-making, asking permission and offering options rather than dictating steps. Co-creation is evident in selecting Study Focus and tailoring technical guidance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk with scaling questions and requests for specific steps, amplifying client solutions. Missed opportunities include more explicit amplification of change statements and asking confidence/commitment questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without arguing and offers compromise strategies that respect reasons for staying the same. Could more often use double-sided reflections and strategic summaries to more softly shift momentum toward change.
