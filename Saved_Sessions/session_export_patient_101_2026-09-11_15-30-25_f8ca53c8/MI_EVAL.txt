# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, avoids judgment, and normalizes the client's worries, creating a welcoming, shame‑reducing atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening and accurate reflections, including an early summary that captures both emotion and content and repeatedly checks understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing risky drinking and improving contraceptive reliability, moving conversation from values to concrete, time‑specific plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in mutual problem‑solving by inviting the client's ideas, alternating suggestions with the client's proposals, and refining the client's chosen plan together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports client autonomy by offering choices, asking which option feels doable, and avoiding pressuring language while normalizing potential setbacks.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and resources, building on internal reasons (e.g., protecting reproductive health) to generate practical steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the client as the expert and co‑decision maker, using repeated open invitations to place choice and plan construction with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Routinely elicits and amplifies change talk, narrowing options into concrete, actionable commitment language (e.g., the 11:00 pm text and safeguards).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk but misses opportunities to strategically soften it—tends to pivot to planning rather than using double‑sided reflections or exploring ambivalence to enhance discrepancy.
