# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes feelings, and uses respectful, nonshaming language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate empathic reflections match the patient's concerns (e.g., loss of control, work/family fatigue) and guide practical suggestions rather than being perfunctory.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stays focused on reducing alcohol use with concrete next steps (tracking method, alarms, check‑in) and a clear, scheduled follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician works as an equal partner—offering expertise while co‑creating the plan and soliciting the patient’s input on feasibility and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices are repeatedly offered (tracking methods, telling spouse or not, check‑in format) and the clinician avoids pressuring language, supporting the patient’s sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good elicitation of the patient's ideas and implementation intentions, but most elicitation centers on logistics rather than deeper values or intrinsic motives for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician frames the work as collaborative, honors the patient's decisions, and jointly develops practical strategies (alarms, photos, reminders).

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete, action‑focused change talk and commitments, though could more actively amplify intrinsic reasons and desire/need language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk is met with validation and reflective redirection to small steps rather than confrontation, but using more double‑sided reflections or summaries could better resolve ambivalence.
