# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Warm, nonjudgmental, and supportive throughout; affirmed efforts and normalized setbacks, conveying clear respect for the client’s experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate empathic statements and validation but moved quickly to practical suggestions and offered few deeper reflective statements of ambivalence or emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on increasing physical activity with concrete, measurable steps (5–10 min walk, shoes by door, Sunday follow-up) and safety checks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invited the patient's input and co-constructed the plan, but multiple unsolicited tips and specific tactics reduced the sense of fully equal two-way problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offered choices, asked preferences for follow-up, and reinforced client control while encouraging self-compassion and respecting practical limits.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited a behavioral commitment and a doable choice, but relied more on expert strategies than on drawing out the patient’s own motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance and invited decision-making, yet occasional prescriptive guidance shifted some control toward the clinician’s expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk by asking which option felt doable and affirming the plan, though could have further amplified personal reasons and values for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged low energy and inconsistency and offered tiny-step options and normalization to reduce sustain talk, but could use more double-sided reflections and fewer directive solutions.
