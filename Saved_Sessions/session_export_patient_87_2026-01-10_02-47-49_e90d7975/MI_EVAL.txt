# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed respect and nonjudgmental support with normalizing language, safety checks, and reassurance. Validated Marco’s struggle and avoided blaming language.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic reflections and attunement (e.g., normalizing triggers), but reflections were brief and solution-focused. Missed opportunities to name deeper emotions like discouragement or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on evening snacking with concrete, pragmatic goals (tea+brush, timer, check-in). Repeatedly returned to the target behavior and immediate coping strategies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was largely collaborative with co-created scripts and preference checks, and the patient chose options. At times the clinician led with ready-made solutions rather than eliciting Marco’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported autonomy by offering choices, asking permission, and emphasizing the patient’s control over plans. Reinforced that the plan was Marco’s decision and adaptable to family life.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out motivation via scaling questions and asking what would increase confidence, and elicited specific doable actions. Could deepen evocation by exploring values, personal reasons for change, and past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative tone and co-created messages and contingency plans, inviting preferences. The balance shifted at times toward expert advice with multiple specific strategies rather than fully privileging Marco’s expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk using confidence scaling and planning, and prompted small, concrete actions. Could amplify and reflect Marco’s own motivational statements more to elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it and redirected to family-friendly solutions, avoiding debate. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections that validate pull of comfort eating while contrasting it with Marco’s goals.
