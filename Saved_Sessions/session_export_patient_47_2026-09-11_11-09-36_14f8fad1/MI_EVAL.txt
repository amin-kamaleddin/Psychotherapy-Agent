# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing ambivalence and honoring the patient's limits throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates clear understanding with accurate reflections and summaries that validate ambivalence and practical concerns, but could probe more into deeper emotions (e.g., feelings about the son, the fall, or shame).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on alcohol reduction with each question and reflection tied to concrete behavior-change strategies and an actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner throughout, soliciting the patient's input at every decision point and integrating her preferences into the co-constructed plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supports autonomy by offering choices, avoiding pressure, and inviting the patient to set wording, placement, and how to handle missed nights, reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits preparatory change talk and implementation intentions, but could better strengthen commitment by asking importance/confidence questions and amplifying change-talk toward commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance with repeated invitations for the patient to choose among options, demonstrating genuine power-sharing and respect for her expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk through open questions that produce concrete patient commitments and specific implementation steps.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and skillfully redirects toward small, doable steps, though could more explicitly reflect and contrast sustain-talk content to further soften resistance.
