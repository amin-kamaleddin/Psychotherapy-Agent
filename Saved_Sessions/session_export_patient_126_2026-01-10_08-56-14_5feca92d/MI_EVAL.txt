# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validated the patient's perspective throughout, explicitly framing the visit as supportive and avoiding blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and perspective-taking with safety checks and reflections, though could deepen affective reflections to explore underlying emotions more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept the session practical and goal-oriented with small achievable steps, but did not explicitly link chosen actions to smoking/alcohol health targets from the referral.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative—offered options, asked which felt useful, co-created scripts and follow-ups, and adjusted to the patient's preferences rather than directing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice and low commitment by offering options, asking permission, and explicitly leaving decisions to the patient without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some small change-related choices but relied heavily on offering solutions and scripts rather than drawing out the patient's own reasons, values, or motivations for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated clear partnership and power-sharing by inviting input, letting the patient choose options, and co-constructing the plan together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some evocation of doable steps and a behavioral commitment (softer text), but did not systematically elicit the patient's own reasons, importance, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with minimizations, which is appropriate, but did not use strategic techniques like double-sided reflections or eliciting costs of the status quo to soften sustain talk.
