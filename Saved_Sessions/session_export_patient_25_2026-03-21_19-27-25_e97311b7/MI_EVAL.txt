# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and affirming (safety check, praised quitting, normalized pressure) with a strengths-focused tone that conveyed positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used validating statements and some reflections (normalizing, acknowledging awkwardness) but provided few deeper, complex reflections of the patient's emotions, values, or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol-related goals and steered the session toward concrete, actionable strategies and role-play without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—invited co-creation of goals and rehearsals, alternated roles in role-play, and adjusted based on the patient's responses rather than dictating steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored patient choice by offering options, asking permission, and avoiding prescriptive language, supporting patient ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation (pride in quitting, triggers, choosing to rehearse) but missed opportunities to draw out deeper personal reasons, values, and scaling questions to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently invited input and co-created exercises, but at times supplied multiple scripts and tactics before eliciting the patient's own phrasing, leaning slightly toward expert delivery.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk through praise, questions about triggers, and rehearsal, yet did not sufficiently probe reasons, values, or importance/confidence to deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided amplifying sustain talk and redirected to coping options, but used few double-sided reflections to actively acknowledge ambivalence and soften sustain talk.
