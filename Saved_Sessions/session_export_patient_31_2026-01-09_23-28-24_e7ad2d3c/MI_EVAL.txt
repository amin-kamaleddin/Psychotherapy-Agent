# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed respect and nonjudgmental acceptance through affirmations and normalizing language, showing clear unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening that captured concrete practical and emotional concerns, though reflections stayed mainly behavioral and could deepen by exploring feeling- or identity-level meaning of smoking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent behavioral focus on smoking reduction and NRT use with a coherent, actionable week‑one plan and follow‑up steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem‑solving—clinician solicited preferences, asked permission, avoided lecturing, and shared ownership of the plan and follow‑up.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice by offering options, accepting noncommitment to quitting, and framing slips as information rather than failure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons for change, goals, and a start date, but could use more open, evocative questions to draw out intrinsic motivations beyond practical benefits.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated genuine partnership by repeatedly inviting input, asking permission, and leaving key decisions to the patient based on his life context.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk and translated goals into a concrete plan, but would benefit from amplifying and following up on patient statements to deepen commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used harm‑reduction/double‑sided framing to accept sustain talk, though could more strategically reflect both sides and pivot more tightly to change talk.
