# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, validating both sides without labeling or moralizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, sustained reflective listening mirrored the patient's experience and emotions, helping him feel understood and prompting further disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a clear focus on weekend drinking and progressively narrowed the conversation toward a specific, measurable test (alternating water, alarm, backups).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session modeled equal partnership with frequent invitations for the patient’s input and co‑construction of a concrete, patient‑driven plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choices were framed as the patient’s, the clinician emphasized that this was a test not a forever promise, and multiple options were offered rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong elicitation of personal reasons for change and client‑generated strategies, but could further link values to behavior and elicit stronger commitment/confidence language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly solicited the patient’s ideas, adopted his suggestions, and facilitated co‑planning, demonstrating genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and developed meaningful change talk leading to a concrete plan, but missed opportunities to amplify desire/ability/need and probe confidence to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk using neutral and double‑sided reflections, though could more actively reframe rationalizations and emphasize discrepancy to further soften sustain talk.
