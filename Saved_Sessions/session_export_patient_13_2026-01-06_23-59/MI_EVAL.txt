# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows consistent unconditional positive regard, normalizing and validating the patient's experience with a nonjudgmental, supportive tone.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and perspective-taking, naming emotions and practical concerns which fosters safety for disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on seeking medical assessment throughout, keeping questions and reflections tied to making care-seeking doable.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is a true joint endeavor with inclusive language and frequent solicitation of the patient's input, co-creating scripts and options based on her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and framing steps as voluntary and small to preserve the patient's autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic reasons for change (care for child, desire for peace of mind) and manageable steps, but stops short of consolidating motivation into explicit commitment or confidence measures.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong power-sharing and client influence—clinician invites choices, checks permission, and positions the patient as the expert on feasibility.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change talk and supports a manageable first step, but misses opportunities to more systematically deepen and convert change language into a specific commitment or timeline.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reflects sustain talk with double-sided reflections without arguing, yet does not consistently reframe or more actively pivot sustain talk toward change and problem-solving.
