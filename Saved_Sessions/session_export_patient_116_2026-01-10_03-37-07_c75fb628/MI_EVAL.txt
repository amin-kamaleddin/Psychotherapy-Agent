# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalizing the patient's experience and offering options without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and perspective-taking with reflective statements and validation, though deeper emotional reflections and more complex reflections were occasionally missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on medication concerns and concrete next steps, repeatedly steering the session toward manageable actions related to meds.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative—asks permission and preferences, co-constructs scripts and questions, and treats the patient as a partner rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice and control by offering multiple options, respecting refusals, and explicitly affirming it's okay to wait until ready.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Attempts some elicitation with open questions about dislikes and worries, but mainly pivoted to pragmatic resources instead of exploring internal motivators, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fosters a collaborative relationship by offering options and supports, though could more explicitly highlight the patient's expertise and use co-created summaries linking values to change.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change-oriented language and small steps but does not consistently amplify or deepen reasons, commitment, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects with pragmatic, low-effort strategies and normalization, though could use more double-sided or complex reflections to pivot toward change talk.
