# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally respectful and nonjudgmental with validating reflections and practical supports, but warmth and individualized normalization are more pragmatic and task-focused than consistently deepening acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid perspective-taking and reflective listening and tailors interventions to Dylan's concerns, but reflections are brief and the clinician moves quickly to problem-solving rather than exploring underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, task-focused direction throughout the session, concentrating tightly on drafting statements, coaching delivery, and managing immediate student-conduct/housing issues.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers cooperative elements like role-play and options, but frequently defaults to prescribing exact wording and coaching details, which shifts power toward the clinician rather than co-constructing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Affirms choice by offering options and permission to practice, yet exerts some directional pressure by advising specific non-apologetic stances and instructing what not to add.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Little effort to elicit Dylan's own motivation or explore ambivalence; the session centers on damage-control rather than drawing out values, goals, or discrepancy to build intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows collaborative intent through questions and offers to help, but becomes more expert-driven during scripting and coaching instead of fully co-creating language with Dylan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal elicitation of change talk; the clinician focuses on practical tactics and misses invitations to explore values, consequences, or future-oriented reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation but often operationalizes Dylan's minimization and coaches denial, thereby reinforcing sustain talk rather than using double-sided reflections or reframing to soften resistance.
