PSYCHIATRIST: Common side effects of citalopram: nausea, dry mouth, sleep changes (sleepy or wired), headache, sweating, tremor, and sexual side effects. It can also prolong the heart’s QT interval (lower doses for older adults).

Important: citalopram and phenelzine (an MAOI) should not be combined — that raises the risk of life‑threatening serotonin syndrome. A washout period (usually about 14 days) is needed when switching. Don’t start or stop either medication without your prescriber. If you have high fever, severe agitation, fast heartbeat, or muscle stiffness, seek emergency care.
