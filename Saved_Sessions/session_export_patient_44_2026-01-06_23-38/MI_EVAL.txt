# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, praising progress and normalizing frustration without criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good reflective listening and perspective-taking, acknowledging worries and wins; could probe deeper emotional content and use more complex reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on exercise and diet goals and consistently moved the session toward specific, actionable implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited client choices, rehearsed plans, and co-developed accountability text, showing two-way interaction; however, frequent prescriptive strategy lists made the interaction somewhat expert-driven.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited preferences and offered options that respect the client's control, though some directive phrasing and immediate instructions could be experienced as pushing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk and choices but relied more on delivering solutions than drawing out deeper personal motivations or values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally fostered a collaborative stance by asking which options felt doable and involving the client in planning; at times reverted to an expert/solution-giving posture.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforced and elicited concrete commitments and highlighted wins, but sometimes moved quickly to prescribe tactics instead of amplifying the client's own reasons and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and reframed setbacks without shaming, offering tactical ways to lower barriers; used few double-sided reflections to explicitly summarize ambivalence.
