# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and validation using strengths‑focused and normalizing language, avoiding judgment across multiple turns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective‑taking and timely reflective listening, paraphrasing emotions and ambivalence to deepen rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on reducing reproductive risk and risky drinking and steadily moves the session from ambivalence to a concrete IUD/implant consult and implementation plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as cooperative, repeatedly soliciting the patient’s preferences and co‑creating the plan (script, role‑play, supports) rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, asks permission, and affirms the patient’s choice and control, reinforcing autonomy throughout planning and decision points.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient’s own motivations and past strengths, pulling motivation from the patient and translating it into concrete, self‑generated steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shares power and expertise with the patient by offering options and inviting choice, positioning the clinician as a collaborator supporting the patient’s decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk, asks for a specific small step, and scaffolds commitment into actionable supports (script, role‑play, reminders).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change, but could more actively use double‑sided reflections or strategic summaries to explicitly name and soften the pull toward staying the same.
