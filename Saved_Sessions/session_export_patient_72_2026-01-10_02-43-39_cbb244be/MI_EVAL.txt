# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental support with repeated affirmations and normalization of barriers; tone demonstrates strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows solid perspective-taking and validation with supportive coaching, but uses relatively few reflective or affect-focused statements and often moves quickly to problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, sustained focus on restarting a sustainable exercise routine with concrete, actionable steps and implementation details; no drift from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently a joint enterprise: elicits Leila's preferences, co-constructs scripts and plans, and checks in frequently to follow her lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly asks permission, offers options rather than mandates, and reinforces that Leila is making the choices, avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits commitments and reinforces motivation through role-play and planning, but leans toward practical coaching rather than deeper open questions that draw out personal values and meanings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and invites input, though at times provides many concrete suggestions in a somewhat directive way that could be more explicitly framed as options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively supports and amplifies existing change talk and elicits concrete commitments, but could more deliberately evoke desire, reasons, and commitment with open-ended, change-focused questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects to practical solutions and backups, though it seldom uses MI-specific techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections) to explicitly integrate and reframe ambivalence.
