# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive language, normalized ambivalence, and affirmed the patient’s efforts, conveying clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections and perspective-taking that matched the patient’s affect and pacing, and the clinician asked permission before sensitive probes.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on alcohol-related harm and steered to a small, concrete harm-reduction plan with explicit follow-up and no drift from the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, solicited preferences, and co-created a practical plan that reflected the patient’s choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice, offered options rather than pressure, and left decisions and pace with the patient to support ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivation around recent consequences and helped select a personally acceptable step, but could more systematically draw out reasons, values, and confidence/commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and respectful of the patient’s expertise, though frequent specific clinician suggestions occasionally shifted more expertise into the clinician role rather than inviting the patient to generate tactics.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced several change statements and a concrete first step, but missed opportunities to deepen and sequence change talk (e.g., scaling importance/confidence or exploring long-term values).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and validated the benefits of drinking while pivoting to harm-reduction, though could have used more explicit double-sided or complex reflections to further soften sustain talk.
