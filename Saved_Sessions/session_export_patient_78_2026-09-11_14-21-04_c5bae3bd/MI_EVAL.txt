### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and respect, normalized frustration, and explicitly honored Lucía’s boundaries (e.g., no extra appointments or preachy contacts).

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, matching both content and affect of Lucía’s terse, defensive style. Reflections were warm and validating throughout the exchange.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction with a concrete first step (30–60 minute delay) and repeatedly returned to implementation details to maintain momentum. Conversation stayed goal-directed and action-focused.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as joint problem-solving, soliciting Lucía’s preferences at every step and shaping the plan around her choices. The clinician acted as a partner rather than an expert delivering directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced choice and control by offering options, accepting a modest trial, and honoring requests for minimal contact and no extra appointments. Language supported Lucía’s sense of agency throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical, small-scale motivations and doable change talk, but did not substantially explore deeper values, broader reasons for change, or scale importance/confidence to deepen commitment. More targeted evocation of intrinsic motives would strengthen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly ceded control and co-created decisions (plan, timing, text wording), ensuring Lucía’s preferences shaped the intervention. Demonstrated strong power-sharing and client-centered collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and amplified change language, inviting specific options and converting tentative desire into a concrete, testable plan (delay, tally, gum backup). Reinforced and clarified Lucía’s expressed intentions throughout.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and rolled with resistance, using neutral reflections and action-focused pivots, but used fewer strategic techniques like double-sided reflections or explicit softening statements. Competent at redirecting but could more directly name both sustain and change sides.
