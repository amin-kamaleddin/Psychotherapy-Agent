# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, honored limits, and avoided judgment throughout the session, normalizing Camila’s experience. Warmth and explicit affirmations supported a nonshaming atmosphere.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used good reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately paraphrasing content and validating emotions. Reflections were mostly content-focused and would benefit from deeper emotional or value-level reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing problematic drinking with concrete strategies tied to triggers and contexts. The session remained purposeful and action-oriented without drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the session as a joint exploration, co-creating plans and inviting Camila to pick and tweak scripts. Frequent brainstorming, role-play, and incorporation of Camila’s ideas demonstrated strong teamwork.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Camila’s choice to seek balance rather than total abstinence and repeatedly asked permission before offering ideas. Emphasized options and asked what would raise her confidence to reinforce ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited motivations, past successes, and used evocative questions to draw out goals and confidence levels. At times shifted into offering many concrete suggestions rather than continuously prompting Camila to articulate her own reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated power-sharing by asking permission, presenting options, and co-constructing the plan with Camila. The clinician valued Camila’s expertise and invited her to choose and refine strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced several instances of change talk and used a confidence ruler to explore readiness. Missed opportunities to amplify strong change statements and to probe value-oriented reasons behind the high importance rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without arguing and redirected toward achievable strategies, respecting boundaries. Could more strategically use double-sided reflections and elicit pros/cons to better resolve ambivalence.
