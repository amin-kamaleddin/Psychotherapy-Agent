### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes Marcus’s experience with repeated validations and no blame. Acceptance is maintained throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and accurate paraphrasing of concerns and context. Reflections are sometimes surface-level and could probe deeper emotions like fear or shame.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on blood-glucose self-monitoring and moves toward concrete, measurable steps. Conversation is progressively narrowed to an actionable plan without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly solicits Marcus’s input and co-creates the plan, offering to coordinate with the PCP only as desired. The clinician maintains an egalitarian stance and lets Marcus shape decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by asking which supports Marcus prefers and how he wants them arranged. Language affirms ownership and avoids pressure or directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Marcus’s practical reasons and intentions (e.g., morning checks, two-week trial) and asks evocative questions. Could deepen intrinsic motivation by using scaling/confidence questions and amplifying values-based reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and defers to Marcus’s preferences, asking him to choose among options and tailoring follow-up accordingly. Demonstrates strong power-sharing and respect for Marcus’s expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and behavioral intentions through targeted questions and reflections. Missed opportunities to amplify values-based reasons and use scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validates worries and redirects to practical supports, which reduces focus on sustain talk. Relies mainly on reflection and planning rather than strategic softening techniques like double-sided reflections or exploring exceptions.
