# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed nonjudgmental respect and positive regard, validating the parent's ambivalence and avoiding confrontation throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening with concise reflections and summaries that captured both content and underlying emotion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on deciding about vaccination and what information/process would help that decision, keeping questions and reflections tightly on target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Invited the parent's input at every step, checked back with summaries, and adjusted the plan based on her preferences, creating a genuinely joint process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering choices rather than directives and repeatedly framing the decision as the parent's to make, confirming her preferences for format and content.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited procedural preferences and criteria for deciding (formats, numbers, sources) but did not probe underlying motivations, values, or confidence in the decision.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the parent as an expert on her preferences and co-created the plan through repeated invitations to choose and shape the materials, demonstrating clear power-sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete steps and planning that move toward action (one-page then call) but did not explicitly draw out or amplify intrinsic reasons, values, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and reframed concerns into practical next steps, though could have used more explicit double-sided reflections and amplification of emerging change talk to further weaken sustain language.
