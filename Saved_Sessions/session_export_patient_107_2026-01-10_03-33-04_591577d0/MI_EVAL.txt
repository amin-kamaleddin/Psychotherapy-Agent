# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician is consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating the patient's perspective and normalizing ambivalence while offering options rather than blame.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking with brief reflections and tailored responses to concerns, but reflections are more informational than deep emotional exploration of the ritual's meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on coffee consumption and blood pressure risk, with all questions and plans tied directly to that target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine two-way engagement by asking preferences, inviting readings, and tailoring plans to the patient's work and logistics concerns.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors patient choice and frames recommendations as options, using language that preserves control and allows the patient to decide the next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits practical information and commitments but rarely draws out intrinsic motivations, values, or uses scaling/evocative questions to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making and invites input, but leans toward expert directives with detailed medical instructions rather than co-creating all plan elements.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provides choices and doable options that open space for change talk, but does not consistently amplify or deepen the patient's own reasons, desire, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and validates sustain talk while redirecting to monitoring and pragmatic mitigations, though could use more double-sided reflections to highlight ambivalence.
