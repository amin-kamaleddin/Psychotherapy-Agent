# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard—validates feelings, normalizes difficulty, offers options and honors the client’s choice. No criticism or minimization was observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong reflective listening and perspective-taking that captures core concerns. Could deepen empathy with more complex reflections about underlying emotions and values (e.g., what self-respect feels like or fears about disappointing others).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, continuous focus on protecting self-respect through concrete behavior change. Each clinician turn steers toward actionable steps and avoids tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner—proposes ideas then immediately invites the client to select, modify, and practice them. Uses collective language and serves as a rehearsal partner rather than director.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice and elicits the client’s preferences, offering multiple wording and support options. Checks what the client wants to try first, supporting ownership of the decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the client’s reasons and plans and draws them out for implementation, especially logistics. To improve, more systematic probing and amplification of intrinsic motives and scaling of importance/confidence would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the work as collaborative—co-creates messages, role-plays, and invites the client to choose and modify strategies. Power-sharing and joint planning are consistently evident.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change-oriented language and helps translate intentions into concrete actions. Could more strategically amplify and summarize the client’s own desire, ability, and reasons to deepen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids strengthening sustain talk and acknowledges concerns while offering practical protections (DND, auto-reply). Could further soften remaining sustain talk using double-sided reflections to highlight both functions and costs of staying available.
