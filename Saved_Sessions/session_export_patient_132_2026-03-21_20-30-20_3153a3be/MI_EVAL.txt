# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing difficulty and avoiding labeling throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening, mirroring the patient's language and summarizing feelings to deepen rapport.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on shifting from dieting to sustainable habits and gym-based activity, tying questions and plans back to that goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly collaborative and framed as joint problem-solving, with frequent invitations to choose options and co-creation of concrete steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirms choice and avoids pressure by offering options, asking permission, and letting the patient select reminders, wording, and plan details.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations and uses readiness scaling, but could more often amplify intrinsic reasons, link to values, and draw out stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently fosters power-sharing and centers the patient's expertise by asking how to support and incorporating her preferences into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk with open questions and planning, yet sometimes moves quickly into logistics without pausing to reflect and amplify desire/ability/reasons/need.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges ambivalence and offers alternatives, but uses few double-sided reflections or explicit exploration of costs/benefits to help resolve sustain talk.
