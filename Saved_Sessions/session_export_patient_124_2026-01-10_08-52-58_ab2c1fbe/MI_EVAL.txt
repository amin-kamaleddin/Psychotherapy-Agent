# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence with warm, nonjudgmental language and validation throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and paraphrase to show understanding, though reflections were often summary-level and could probe deeper into underlying emotions like anxiety or fear of exclusion.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on marijuana use and moved purposefully from assessment to collaborative planning and role-play to support behavior change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—invited Sofia's input at each step, co-created concrete plans, and used inclusive language to shape decisions together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, accepting Sofia's readiness level, and framing decisions as hers to make, reinforcing ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited pros/cons and specific plans that produced some change talk, but could use more evocative, open questions about values, long-term goals, and confidence to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership by offering options and checking preferences, though providing many specific scripts risked feeling somewhat directive without explicit permission or more client-generated wording.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively invited and strengthened tentative change talk and choices, but could amplify and follow up on change statements (e.g., importance/confidence) to build toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and used double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence, but could more actively emphasize client doubts and use strategic summaries to tip ambivalence toward change.
