# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with warm, nonjudgmental, strengths-based language. Could occasionally add explicit normalization of common setbacks to deepen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, consistent reflective listening and perspective-taking, mirroring the client's language and deepening reflections. Reflections validate emotional experience and the demands the client juggles.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, task-focused direction toward a concrete behavioral target (short outing in a nicer top) and supports actionable planning. Keeps the conversation on relevant steps without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly co-constructs the plan with the client (text content, timing, coping phrase) and offers role-play and choices. Positions the clinician as helper and partner rather than prescriber.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports client choice and control by offering options and validating the client's right to bail or change plans. Language preserves ownership and avoids pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own motivations and meaningful indicators of change, deepening intrinsic reasons to act. Asks what would make steps feel safer and more doable, strengthening client-generated motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters power-sharing by repeatedly asking for the client's preferences and inviting collaboration on wording and approach. Integrates client choices directly into the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk, converting general desires into specific, testable steps and co-creating an implementation plan. Could further strengthen by selectively emphasizing client-expressed motivators to elicit clearer commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes worries while reframing concerns into a safety plan, effectively avoiding strengthening sustain talk. Missed opportunities include using double-sided reflections or summaries to more actively juxtapose costs of staying the same with reasons for change.
