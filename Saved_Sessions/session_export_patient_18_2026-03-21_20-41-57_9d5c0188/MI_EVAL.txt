# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently used warm, nonjudgmental language and validated the teen's perspective, avoiding moralizing and conveying strong unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provided accurate, frequent reflections and paraphrases showing understanding, but reflections were sometimes brief or formulaic with missed opportunities to explore deeper emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on party-related alcohol safety throughout and guided the session toward concrete harm-reduction steps and next actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a two-way effort by inviting choices, adapting suggestions to the client's preferences, and offering to rehearse together, reinforcing mutual problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the client's choice, offered options rather than directives, and used language that reduced pressure to support client ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the client's own ideas and plans (refusal lines, buddies, rehearsals) but missed opportunities to probe motivations, importance, or confidence to strengthen internal commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positioned the session as collaborative, invited the client to set the agenda, and co-created practical plans while deferring to the client's judgment.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited specific intentions and steps toward change, but could have amplified and summarized change talk and asked importance/confidence questions to build stronger commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing harm-reduction, redirecting to safety options, and not confronting or debating reasons to continue risky behavior.
