# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing ambivalence and avoiding blame throughout the exchange.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that acknowledges emotions and context, which lowers resistance and invites further disclosure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with questions and reflections that steer toward actionable steps and commitment checks.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as a joint problem‑solving effort, inviting preferences and co-creating the plan rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supports the client's sense of choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and phrasing suggestions as invitations rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the client's own reasons and resources through exploring past successes, values, and confidence scaling, privileging client-generated solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and client-directed: the clinician repeatedly asks preferences, offers help only when invited, and co-constructs the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly elicits and amplifies change talk and past successes, using scaling and specific planning to build toward concrete steps and commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and rolls with resistance, validating sustain talk and redirecting to pragmatic planning, though could more often use double-sided reflections to deepen discrepancy.
