# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and respect, validated Richard’s perspective, and avoided shaming while honoring his limits and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed good empathic reflections and validations and acknowledged relational tension, but reflections were more pragmatic than deep emotional or value-focused exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on smoking and the relationship throughout, moving logically from exploration to concrete action planning without sidetracking.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—invited Richard’s input on strategies, co-created scripts and check-ins, role‑played at his request, and adjusted pace and content to his preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering options, asking which approach he preferred, honoring his limits, and emphasizing that any change was his choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of perspective (what smoking does for him) and use of relational motivation, but limited probing of intrinsic motives or deeper values to strengthen internal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated true partnership by treating Richard as the expert on his life, sharing decision‑making, and co-creating practical plans and rehearsals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited brief change talk tied to his wife but did not consistently evoke or amplify that language into stronger desire, reasons, or commitment for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided direct confrontation and argument, but largely accommodated sustain talk by offering strategies that allow continued smoking rather than deliberately softening or redirecting it toward change.
