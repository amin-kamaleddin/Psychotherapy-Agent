### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expresses unconditional positive regard and normalizes Elena’s feelings with warm, nonjudgmental language that validates her priorities.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, summarizing Elena’s concerns and asking empathic, open questions that follow her lead.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on the vaccination decision throughout, tying questions and summaries to information needs and concrete next steps without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is largely collaborative with frequent queries about preferences and format, though it could more often invite Elena to formulate her own plan or restate next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivations, concerns, and decision rules, but could do more to draw out intrinsic values and amplify Elena’s own pro-change language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treats Elena as decision-maker and seeks her preferences repeatedly, though occasional expert‑action phrasing slightly reduces the sense of equal knowledge exchange.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Asks targeted questions that produce explicit change talk and a plan, but misses opportunities to reflect, amplify emerging pro-change statements, and elicit commitment/confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes sustain talk and redirects to information needs, but could strengthen impact with double-sided or strategic reflections that immediately juxtapose change talk.
