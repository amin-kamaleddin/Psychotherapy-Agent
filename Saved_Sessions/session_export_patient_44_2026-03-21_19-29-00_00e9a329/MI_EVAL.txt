# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes Erik’s feelings, and validates progress without judgment. Tone is warm and supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and reflective statements that capture Erik’s experience. Responses often shift quickly to problem-solving rather than deeper, affect-focused reflections that would deepen empathy.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on concrete target behaviors and goals with practical next steps. The session does not drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative: plans are co-created, homework is negotiated, and the clinician repeatedly asks for Erik’s preferences. Recommendations are adapted to Erik’s comfort and input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors Erik’s choice and agency by offering options and framing suggestions rather than mandates. Avoids pressuring language and reinforces ownership of the change process.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits some internal motivation by highlighting gains and asking which area to address, but the session is fairly solution-focused. More open-ended prompts to draw out Erik’s own reasons and confidence would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats Erik as a collaborator by offering choices, inviting role-play, and adapting suggestions based on his feedback. Patient contributions clearly shape the session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforces and amplifies change talk by highlighting wins and supporting concrete plans and rehearsal. Missed opportunities include more systematic elicitation and reflection of spontaneous change language (desire, ability, reasons, need).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and reframes sustain talk and offers strategies to avoid spirals, reducing reinforcement of reasons not to change. Relies more on offering strategies and scripts than on double-sided or strategic reflections that explicitly honor ambivalence.
