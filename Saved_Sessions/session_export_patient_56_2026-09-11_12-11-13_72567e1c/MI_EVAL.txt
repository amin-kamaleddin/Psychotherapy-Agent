# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard, normalizes ambivalence, and affirms the client's family‑centered values and sensible solutions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good perspective‑taking with accurate reflections of content and tension, but could deepen empathy by using occasional complex reflections that name underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, tight focus on a single, actionable target (two 20‑minute walks, two‑week trial, timer/check‑in) and narrows options to produce a measurable plan without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames decisions as joint, invites spouse and client input, and uses inclusive language to make the session a mutual exploration rather than an expert lecture.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offers choices, solicits the client's preferences, emphasizes trialing, and preserves the client's sense of control and ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the client by eliciting their reasons and wording, amplifying change talk, and inviting practice to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys a partnering stance throughout, defers to the client's expertise about family logistics, and makes the client's input determinative in planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently elicits and amplifies client change talk, turning a vague idea into a concrete, client‑generated commitment and practicing the proposed script.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Appropriately avoids reinforcing reluctance and reframes worries toward solutions, but relies more on problem‑solving than explicit MI softening techniques like double‑sided reflections.
