# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently uses respectful, nonjudgmental language and affirms the patient’s perspective, normalizing experiences and offering options rather than directives.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong empathic listening with accurate reflections of frustration and ambivalence, though some responses move quickly to solutions rather than deeper feeling-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a clear focus on sustainable habits and body image, returning to concrete targets (belief experiment, snack rule, SMART gym goal) and practical timelines.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is consistently joint and co-constructed, with negotiated experiments, checklists, and shared problem-solving rather than an expert-driven plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Autonomy is explicitly supported through offered choices, framing suggestions as experiments, and repeated emphasis on the patient’s control over decisions and pace.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicits the client’s goals and invites experiments to generate intrinsic data, but could strengthen evocation with more values-linked questions and strategic summaries of change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative stance with frequent invitations for preference and co-creation, positioning the patient as decision-maker throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change language and practical goals, yet would benefit from amplifying spontaneous change talk and eliciting more confidence and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and neutrally reframes ambivalence while redirecting toward experiments; could further use double-sided reflections to explicitly name both sides.
