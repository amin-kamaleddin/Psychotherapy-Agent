# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (opening line, validations) and avoided shaming or labeling while normalizing ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening and perspective-taking (reflections of relief/fear, offers to role-play and coach breathing), though reflections were mostly surface-level and could be deeper to capture underlying emotions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on contraception and harm-reduction and moved to actionable steps, but relied on information-giving and could more explicitly negotiate a short-term measurable goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—asked for Elena’s input on agenda, offered choices about role-play and clinics, and co-created a harm-reduction checklist tailored to her needs.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honored Elena’s right to choose, offered multiple options, respected deferral, and provided information (e.g., confidential billing) to support informed decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to explore motivation (asking about hesitancy, offering to explore reasons) but the session largely provided solutions rather than eliciting and deepening Elena’s internal reasons and values.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear partnership and shared decision-making—clinician repeatedly asked preferences, offered to rehearse with role options, and worked jointly to identify clinics and scripts.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Moderate efforts to reflect change-oriented language and offer harm-reduction options, but missed opportunities to strategically elicit and amplify Elena’s own motivations with targeted open questions or scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and respected reluctance by offering lower-commitment harm-reduction options, though could have used double-sided reflections and summaries more to shift sustain talk toward change.
