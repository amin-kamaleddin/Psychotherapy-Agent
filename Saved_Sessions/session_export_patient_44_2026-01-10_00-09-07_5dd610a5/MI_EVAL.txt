# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating progress and normalizing struggle throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows a good empathic stance with reflections and normalization, but could probe the emotional texture of discouragement and comparison more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on exercise, diet, and coping strategies, and tied interventions directly to actionable goals and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely co-created tools and plans with the patient, invited tweaks, and negotiated follow-up, reflecting a joint problem-solving approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and control by asking preferences, offering options, and honoring the patient’s decisions and flexible follow-up requests.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively amplified existing change talk and converted it into specific actions, but relied more on offering options than eliciting the patient's own ideas or values-based reasons.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance and offered choices, though at times provided many solutions rather than eliciting and building on the patient's own strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Reinforced and elicited specific commitments (e.g., 3×30 min, 10‑minute delay) but could more actively draw out additional change talk and use scaling questions to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing and reframing barriers and slips, though more frequent double-sided reflections could further resolve ambivalence.
