# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly communicated unconditional positive regard and normalized missed doses, validating Samir’s stance and reducing pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate paraphrase captured frustration and guardedness, though there were missed opportunities to probe deeper emotional nuance or past experiences with meds.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session stayed clearly focused on medication adherence and a single, behaviorally specific trial, with concrete details (timing, wording, discreetness) worked out.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The plan was co‑created from Samir’s preferences with repeated invitations for his input, demonstrating genuine teamwork and mutual decision‑making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently emphasized choice and no obligation, avoided pressure or directives, and respected Samir’s repeated refusals to promise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The psychiatrist elicited practical solutions and patient-generated options (reminders, wording, timing) but did not sufficiently draw out deeper personal motives or values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positioned Samir as the expert on his life, invited his ideas, and let him set the specific terms of the intervention, showing strong power‑sharing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Open, evocative questions prompted tentative change talk and a concrete trial, but the clinician did not substantially deepen intrinsic reasons or elicit stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician avoided arguing and used neutral reflections to reduce sustain talk, though more advanced techniques (double‑sided reflections, contrast with small steps) could further soften resistance.
