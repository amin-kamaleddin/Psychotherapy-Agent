# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician showed consistent unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental validation, framing difficulties as understandable barriers rather than personal failure. Language was respectful and supportive throughout with no blaming statements.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician used accurate perspective-taking and timely reflections that captured Marcus’s affect and priorities, which facilitated deeper disclosure. Empathic paraphrases and validation helped shift Marcus from discouraged to energized.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear focus on increasing physical activity with concrete, time-bound goals and successive goal-directed questions. The clinician moved Marcus from contemplation to a specific implementation plan and commitment.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The interaction was clearly co-constructed: Marcus proposed solutions and the clinician refined and supported them rather than imposing a plan. Tools and suggestions were offered when requested, preserving a team approach.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported Marcus’s choice and control by using invitational language and offering options rather than directives. Suggestions were framed as optional and Marcus was asked to choose timing and backups.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician effectively drew out Marcus’s own motivations, strengths, and solutions using open questions and scaling, leading Marcus to identify personally meaningful reasons and concrete plans. Elicitation focused on Marcus’s values and preferred strategies.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician positioned Marcus as the expert on his life and co-created the plan with him, sharing power in decision-making. Marcus owned the decisions while the clinician supported and refined them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician actively elicited, reinforced, and amplified change talk through open questions, scaling, and reiteration of Marcus’s language, culminating in specific commitments. Repeated reflections and summaries strengthened Marcus’s change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician acknowledged and reframed sustain talk into manageable steps and redirected toward change, but used few explicit double-sided reflections or deliberate discrepancy work. More targeted reflective strategies could further resolve ambivalence.
