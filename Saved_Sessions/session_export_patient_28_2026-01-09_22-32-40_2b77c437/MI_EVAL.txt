# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, praising past success and avoiding blame. Reinforces willingness and normalizes difficulty throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates solid reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately summarizing values and barriers. Could deepen empathy by reflecting underlying emotions (frustration/discouragement) more explicitly.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the session tightly focused on increasing activity with concrete, measurable plans and steps. Questions and interventions consistently drive toward small actionable commitments.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction reads as a team effort with the clinician offering tools and inviting Dante's input and preferences. Clinician adapts suggestions (text length, fridge note) to Dante's choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserves Dante's sense of choice by offering options and asking which is most doable. Avoids pressuring language and checks willingness before proposing steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation by linking activity to valued outcomes and past successes and using scaling questions. Could deepen evocation by inviting more prospective language about how change would feel and amplifying Dante's own motivational statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is co-constructed with frequent invitations for Dante's input and shared decision-making. Plans reflect Dante's choices while the clinician offers supportive tools and adaptations.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports change talk via importance/confidence questions, past-success recall, and scaling, which increases motivation. Could further strengthen change talk through more selective reflections and summaries that amplify Dante's change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges barriers and offers pragmatic contingencies (5-minute backup) that reduce sustain talk's power. However, the clinician often moves quickly into problem-solving without using double-sided or more empathic reflections to explicitly soften sustain talk.
