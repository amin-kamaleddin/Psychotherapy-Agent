# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, validating the patient's caution and normalizing concerns throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking with validating reflections and practical understanding of worry, though reflections stayed pragmatic and could deepen emotional nuance around anxiety and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on starting and safely taking the statin, repeatedly translating concerns into concrete safety and adherence action steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—invited patient input on scripts, rehearsed role-plays, accepted edits, and co-created the plan and logistics as a joint problem-solving process.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported patient choice by offering options, asking permission before interventions, and leaving the decision to start in the patient's hands while clarifying safety steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the patient's own reasons and readiness (small-step prompt, confidence rating) and built those motivations into concrete preparatory and commitment steps.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the patient as an equal partner, drawing on their expertise and preferences, and co-constructing the plan (labs, timing, stop/call rules) with mutual input.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk by inviting a small first step, supporting commitment language, rehearsing messages, and arranging concrete follow-up to convert intent into action.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and did not reinforce hesitation, redirecting to safety planning and feasible steps, but relied mainly on factual reframing rather than MI strategies like double-sided reflection to more fully soften sustain talk.
