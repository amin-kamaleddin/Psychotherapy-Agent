# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Samuel’s ambivalence, avoiding judgment or blame. Tone and validations supported the client and affirmed his experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with specific, timely reflections that mirrored Samuel’s language and feelings. Reflections deepened understanding and kept the client engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept the session tightly focused on smoking reduction and moved toward concrete behavioral tests and plans. Questions were purposefully sequenced to progress from review to specific implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as joint problem-solving, consistently inviting Samuel’s ideas and integrating them into the plan. Clinician positioned themselves alongside Samuel to tweak and review strategies together.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly offered options and asked what felt doable, respecting Samuel’s preferences and avoiding pressure. Moves reinforced the client’s sense of choice and control over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicited Samuel’s own motivations, reasons, and concrete plans through evocative questions and prompts. Drew motivation from the client, producing self-generated steps and expressions of commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated Samuel as the expert on his life and transferred ownership of solutions to him. Solicited his input at each step and co-created actionable strategies together.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk, calling out successes and inviting concrete, doable goals tied to Samuel’s aims. Reinforced client-generated steps (tally, walk, gum) to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and validated sustain talk without reinforcing it, but made fewer double-sided reflections or strategic reframes. Could more deliberately juxtapose change and sustain statements to reduce sustain talk's pull.
