# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating Sofia's feelings and affirming small efforts throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong empathic listening and accurate reflections with a prompt safety check, but missed some opportunities to probe deeper affect and the emotional meaning of being blamed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, task-focused direction on reducing avoidance, reconnecting with a friend, and building concrete coping micro-steps without drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with frequent invitations for Sofia to choose options, offers to co-write or role-play only if wanted, and transparent limits.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Sofia's sense of choice and control by offering multiple options, respecting refusals, normalizing any decision not to act, and avoiding pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and scaffolded small, practical motivation (timer, texts, tiny reward) but relied more on proposing scripts than eliciting Sofia's own reasons, values, or long-term motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a collaborative stance and power-sharing: clinician repeatedly asked Sofia to select options and co-created micro-plans while honoring her preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Encouraged small change talk through micro-steps that produced some client statements of willingness, but leaned toward offering ready-made solutions rather than eliciting reasons, importance, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently reframed setbacks while offering lower-risk alternatives, though could strengthen use of double-sided reflections to integrate sustain and change talk.
