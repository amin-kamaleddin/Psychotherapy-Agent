### Acceptance

### Empathy
- [x] Criteria met
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate empathic reflections and perspective-taking (“Feeling overwhelmed…”, “snoozing happens and your mistrust is valid”). Missed opportunities to probe the emotional subtext of repeated sarcasm and resistance.

### Direction
- [x] Criteria met
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on asthma self-management (meds, follow-ups) and steered toward a concrete, measurable plan (inhaler-by-sink + 8:00 AM alarm). Repeated, explicit safety instructions (use rescue inhaler/ER) and no drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
- [x] Criteria met
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used permission language and invited patient choice (e.g., “Can I ask…?”, “which feels doable right now?”). Co-constructed the plan rather than imposing it. At times offered multiple unsolicited practical tips without first eliciting the patient’s prioritization.

---

### Autonomy Support
- [x] Criteria met
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong emphasis on choice and agency (“If you want a check-in… say so then — I’ll respect your pace,” accepted “no promises”). Avoided pressuring language and reinforced ownership of decisions.

---

### Evocation
- [x] Criteria met
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited patient reasons/ideas for change (desire to stop breathlessness; agreed to a reminder) and turned them into a behavioral experiment. Could have probed values, benefits, or confidence (e.g., scaling) to deepen intrinsic motivation.

---

### Partnership
- [x] Criteria met
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Positioned clinician and patient as collaborators (“what feels doable,” “try it for two weeks”), invited choices and respected pace. Could more explicitly highlight the patient’s expertise and invite prior-success strategies.

---

### Cultivating Change Talk
- [x] Criteria met
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change talk and converted it into a concrete first step (reminder + sink placement). Did not sufficiently amplify or deepen change talk (no values probe, confidence scaling, or explicit commitment elicited).

---

### Softening Sustain Talk
- [x] Criteria met
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing with defensive/minimizing language (e.g., “Oscar hasn’t made me die yet”) and routinely redirected to small, manageable actions. Could strengthen use of double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge sustain talk while eliciting change.
