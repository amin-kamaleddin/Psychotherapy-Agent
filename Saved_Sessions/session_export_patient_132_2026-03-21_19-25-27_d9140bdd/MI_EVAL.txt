### Empathy

### Direction

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as cooperative and co-constructed tools with Yasmin, inviting her input and tailoring templates to her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering multiple options, seeking permission before plans, and emphasizing Yasmin’s control over which tools to use.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of priorities and use of the patient’s language, but the clinician largely provided information and structured plans and missed opportunities for open, evocative questions to draw out internal motivation and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely treated Yasmin as the expert and offered choices, though occasional detailed recommendations shifted the interaction toward an expert-delivery style rather than sustained co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively reinforced change-oriented language with process goals and non-scale benchmarks, but could use more direct evocative questions to deepen Yasmin’s own reasons and values for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently redirected weight-focus toward alternatives (e.g., RPE, energy), though more strategic double-sided reflections would further validate both sides while guiding toward change.
