# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows strong unconditional positive regard—validates priorities, respects privacy, and normalizes ambivalence without shaming or minimizing.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Displays accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, but at times is more pragmatic than deeply exploratory and misses opportunities for emotional reflections about stress or social meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on vaping and ties conversation to concrete behavioral goals and values, moving efficiently from problem identification to a specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner—asks open questions, invites the client's ideas, and scaffolds solutions so the client co-constructs a detailed, doable plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and explicitly respecting the client's decision to try a private approach.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies the client's intrinsic motives and confidence, drawing out reasons for change and specific commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly collaborative with repeated checks for the client's input and shared decision-making that centers the client's lived experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reinforces, and amplifies change talk through reflections, scaling questions, and requests for concrete first steps and implementation details.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects toward change effectively, but could more explicitly acknowledge sustain talk with double-sided reflections and probe the social/emotional value of vaping.
