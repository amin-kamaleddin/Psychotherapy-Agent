# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive throughout the session with frequent affirmations and normalization of setbacks. The clinician repeatedly acknowledged Samuel’s efforts and limits, conveying clear unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and perspective-taking, summarizing concerns and validating ambivalence. Missed a few opportunities for deeper affective reflections (worry, frustration) that would strengthen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction with specific, actionable steps tied to identified triggers. Sets a measurable one‑week trial and schedules a midweek check‑in to advance the agenda toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly collaborative with the clinician soliciting Samuel’s input at every step and integrating his choices into the plan. Suggestions were offered as options and the plan was co‑constructed and negotiated.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms Samuel’s freedom to choose, offering multiple options and deferring final decisions to him. Frames the trial as voluntary and checks confidence, supporting self‑directed change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits Samuel’s ideas and motivations and draws the plan from his statements. Could further evoke intrinsic motivation by exploring values, long‑term reasons for quitting, and using summary reflections to amplify change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys that Samuel is the expert on his experience and shares power in decision‑making, asking permission and offering to help. The clinician repeatedly asks which options feel most doable and defers to Samuel’s preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with affirmations of progress, specific goal‑setting, and eliciting a concrete one‑week commitment. Uses targeted questions to strengthen and focus Samuel’s desire and plans for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, normalizes slips, and redirects to practical strategies and backups. Could further soften sustain talk with more double‑sided reflections that explicitly acknowledge reasons for staying the same while highlighting reasons for change.
