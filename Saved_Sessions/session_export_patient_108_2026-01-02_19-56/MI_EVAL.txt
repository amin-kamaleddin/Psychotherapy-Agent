**Comments:** Partnership was evident in invitations and adaptations. Greater co-construction (having Oliver generate lines first, then refining together) would enhance partnership further.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4/5  
**Observed:**
- [x] Elicited concrete commitments (pick a date, send text, rehearse with friend)  
- [x] Patient produced change statements which clinician operationalized into steps  
- [ ] Missed opportunities to reflect/amplify change talk more aggressively (double-sided reflections, summaries)

**Comments:** Clinician successfully elicited and operationalized several change statements. More active amplification and summarizing of Oliver’s pro-change language would strengthen commitment.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3/5  
**Observed:**
- [x] Acknowledged sustain talk (guilt, fear of causing homelessness) and responded empathically  
- [ ] Consistent use of double-sided reflections or tying enforcement actions to values to soften sustain talk  
- [ ] Avoided procedural/consequence language that might reinforce sustain talk

**Comments:** Sustain talk was acknowledged, and clinician provided reframing and support. However, several procedural/consequence-focused statements (arranging removal, movers/police) were presented in ways that risked reinforcing Oliver’s worry about being a bully. More double-sided reflections and clearer linkage of enforcement to Oliver’s compassionate intentions/values would better soften sustain talk.
