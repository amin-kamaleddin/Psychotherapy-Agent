# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, using supportive language and repeated validation of the patient's strengths and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking that captured ambivalence, though reflections were often surface-level and could probe deeper emotions (fear, guilt, exhaustion).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking with concrete short-term goals and actionable steps, using scaling and minimal topic drift to keep the session on target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative problem-solving, offering options and inviting the patient's input so the plan was genuinely co-created and driven by her preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by asking permission before advice, offering options, and affirming the patient's ownership of decisions and the pace of change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and resources using scaling and concrete prompts, but could further deepen evocation by exploring values, past successes, and summarizing change talk to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively fostered power-sharing with frequent invites for preference and input, co-constructing the plan and supporting shared decision-making throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with scaling and prompts for doable steps, yet missed opportunities to amplify and summarize the patient's own reasons to move toward stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without reinforcing it and normalized slips while offering harm-reduction, but could more often use double-sided reflections and slower, reflective redirection to soften sustain talk and deepen discrepancy.
