**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflective listening that labels emotions and concerns (e.g., “getting bigger… scary… overwhelming,” worry about childcare); reflections capture emotional content and vulnerability.

**Comments:** Consistently invites patient preference, offers options, asks permission to practice scripts, frames actions as joint work — genuine shared decision‑making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 1 ☐ 2 ☐ 3 ☐ 4 ☐ **5 ☑**
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors patient choice and limits (“I’m with you wherever you are,” accepts “not promising”); offers scalable steps and avoids pressuring language.

### Evocation

**Comments:** Elicits patient ideas and strengths (asks what’s doable, what strengths used with family) and generates change talk, but could more systematically draw out and amplify the patient’s own motivational language (importance, reasons, confidence).

### Partnership

**Comments:** Successfully elicits initial change talk (phone triage, asking sister) and reduces barriers with concrete scripts; needs more amplification and elicitation of commitment/confidence to strengthen intention.

### Softening Sustain Talk

**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and validates sustain talk (fear, “not promising”); redirects to small steps. Could more actively use double‑sided reflections or summaries to help resolve ambivalence into a specific choice.
