# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating—normalizes ambivalence and conveys unconditional positive regard, which reduced shame and supported disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong empathic statements and validation of fear and guilt, but missed opportunities for deeper reflective listening (e.g., double-sided reflections) before moving to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in pregnancy; every intervention ties back to protecting the pregnancy and managing social pressure.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction is collaborative—asks preferences, rehearses wording, and tailors supports—but frequently provides ready-made scripts instead of first inviting co-generation of ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Routinely offers choices and asks for preferences, supporting agency; could strengthen ownership by asking Sofia to generate more solutions before offering options.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and leverages Sofia’s motivations and reasons for change, but often shifts quickly to solutions rather than drawing out more personal arguments, confidence, and importance.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Uses some collaborative language and rehearsals, but the session leans clinician-led with scripted options rather than fully privileging Sofia’s expertise and ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk through rehearsals and affirmations, though it could better elicit commitment and confidence with scaling and targeted questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and offers practical alternatives, but does limited explicit exploration or reframing of sustain talk (e.g., double-sided reflections or strategic summaries).
