# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing slips and using respectful language throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and validation with reflections and acknowledgement of frustration and courage, but did not consistently explore deeper emotions or shame before moving to practical solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on reducing alcohol use and moved the session toward concrete, actionable steps (AF nights, checklist, alarms, weekly recap).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked largely as an equal partner by soliciting preferences and co-creating plans, though offering many specific suggestions at once slightly reduced the sense of the patient driving the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy through choices, invitational language, and flexible options, reinforcing the patient's control over decisions and reducing pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some choices and change intentions, but did not sufficiently probe intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence to deepen internal motivation and resolve ambivalence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance and repeatedly invited input, but could improve by explicitly acknowledging the patient's expertise and eliciting more of their ideas before offering solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced some commitment language and planning, yet tended to offer solutions rather than strategically amplifying the patient's own statements of desire, ability, or reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and did not strengthen sustain talk, but missed opportunities to use double-sided reflections or summaries to explicitly soften ambivalence and reframe sustain talk.
