# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive—normalizes worry, affirms effort, and frames slips as information rather than failure, conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses empathic language and some reflections about practical concerns, but misses deeper emotional reflections (e.g., discouragement or fear of repeated failure) that would better validate underlying feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on evening snacking with interventions and commitments tied directly to that target behavior, keeping the session actionable.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—solicits input and co‑constructs plans—but frequent unsolicited, detailed directives without permission reduce evenness of power‑sharing in places.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice by offering options, asking which action the patient will commit to, and explicitly reinforcing that the patient is in charge of what to try and report back.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation through scaling and nightly wins and draws out self‑efficacy, but explores intrinsic motives and values behind change only minimally.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some partnership present (choices, invites input) but the clinician frequently adopts an expert role with unprompted, structured advice, which weakens the sense that expertise resides with the client.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk via confidence ratings, commitments, and nightly reporting, though could more often reflect and amplify the patient's own change statements and values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and redirects sustain talk to strategy without reinforcing it, but does not regularly use advanced softening techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to more fully explore ambivalence.
