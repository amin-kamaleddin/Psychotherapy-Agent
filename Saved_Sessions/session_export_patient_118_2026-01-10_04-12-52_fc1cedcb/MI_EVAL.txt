# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence, explicitly honoring the patient's wish to avoid smoking discussions and reducing coercive pressure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used multiple accurate reflections and perspective-taking that captured the patient's competing pulls and concerns about being lectured, demonstrating high-quality empathic listening.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, clinically useful focus on improving dental health and appropriately shifted to the patient's accepted goal, though the original smoking target was not pursued here.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the session as joint problem-solving, solicited preferences, offered options, and integrated the patient's directives into a concrete plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed the patient's right to choose, offered options rather than prescriptions, and explicitly honored the decision to defer smoking discussions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively evoked motivation for the immediate dental goal but gave minimal permission‑based exploration of any latent motivation or values related to smoking.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Shared power by offering choices and deferring to the patient's preferences; the plan was clearly co‑constructed with the patient's expertise guiding decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited and reinforced change talk about dental behavior and concrete steps, but missed opportunities to gently elicit or amplify any emerging change talk about smoking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and accommodated sustain talk by validating the patient's reasons and redirecting to mutually acceptable, practical goals, effectively reducing resistance.
