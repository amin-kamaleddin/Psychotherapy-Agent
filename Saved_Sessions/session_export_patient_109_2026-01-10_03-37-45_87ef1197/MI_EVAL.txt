### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently demonstrated unconditional positive regard, warmth, and validation with no judgmental statements; normalized Rafael’s discouragement repeatedly.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed solid empathic listening and perspective-taking with reflections and normalization of feelings, though opportunities to deepen emotional exploration were missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on returning to exercise throughout the session with interventions tightly linked to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered joint planning by asking preferences and co-creating steps, but often supplied concrete templates rather than prompting Rafael to generate more content himself.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control with permission-seeking language and low-pressure options, avoiding coercive phrasing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some barriers and small commitments but relied more on practical solutions than on drawing out Rafael’s intrinsic motivations, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance by inviting choices and preferences, though at times moved quickly into clinician-led solution offering rather than fully client-generated ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced existing change talk and asked for small commitments, but did not systematically elicit or amplify reasons, strengths, or commitment language to deepen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided arguing and used normalization and low-pressure framing to reduce resistance, though could use more double-sided reflections to explicitly integrate ambivalence.
