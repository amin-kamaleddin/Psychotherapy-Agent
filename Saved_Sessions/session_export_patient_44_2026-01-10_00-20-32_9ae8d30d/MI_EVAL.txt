# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently warm, nonjudgmental, and validating; normalizes progress and acknowledges difficulties so Erik feels accepted throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, capturing hope and wariness and asking about barriers and supports to understand Erik's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors (gym, diet, coping with comparisons) and ties each intervention to actionable steps aligned with Erik's goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as joint problem‑solving by offering options, drafts, and immediately inviting Erik to choose or modify plans, sharing power in decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors Erik's choices, offers realistic options and permission-based suggestions, and avoids pressuring language to support his sense of control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation through benefits and confidence scaling, but could more fully draw out deeper personal values and intrinsic reasons before moving to solutions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly collaborative: the clinician repeatedly asks Erik's preferences, invites tweaks, and co-creates the plan so Erik's expertise guides the approach.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently reinforces and elicits change talk and confidence ratings and translates intentions into concrete actions, but would benefit from more reflective amplification to deepen reasons.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects toward coping and planning rather than arguing, though more double-sided reflections and explicit summaries could better resolve ambivalence.
