# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician used a respectful, nonjudgmental tone, validated preferences, and offered choices consistently. To reach a top rating they could add more individualized affirmations and normalize ambivalence about alcohol and BP.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There were some empathic moves (open question, supportive statements), but reflective listening and exploration of the patient's feelings were limited. The session moved quickly into instructions without probing or reflecting the patient’s minimizations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a clear, consistent focus on blood pressure monitoring, logistics, and safety throughout the visit. Conversation stayed on-task with concrete next steps and no topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The interaction was collaborative in offering options, asking preferences, and inviting choices. Most content remained clinician-driven, however, and the plan could be more jointly generated using the patient's routines and input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently honored the patient's choices, presented multiple options, and avoided pressuring language. Recommendations were framed as choices to take to the PCP, reinforcing patient control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** There was little effort to elicit the patient's own motivations, values, or ambivalence; the session was primarily informational. Missed opportunities included asking importance/confidence or exploring pros and cons of change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician fostered a working partnership by asking preferences and offering collaborative next steps. The patient remained relatively passive, and stronger partnership would involve more explicit co-creation of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Efforts to evoke desire, reasons, or commitment to change were minimal and mostly absent. The clinician did not capitalize on the patient's agreement to monitoring to explore meaning, benefits, or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** The clinician avoided confrontation but did little to strategically soften sustain talk or invite ambivalence. There were no double-sided reflections or gentle reframes to acknowledge minimization while opening room for reconsideration.
