# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☒4 ☐5
**Comments:** Respectful, nonjudgmental tone and validation throughout ("that sounds like a lot"; accepts patient's limits and "not promising" statements). Would reach 5 with more explicit, individualized unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☒3 ☐4 ☐5
**Comments:** Uses brief acknowledgements and factual reflections (e.g., workload, plan). Missed opportunities for deeper emotional reflection about burnout, reluctance, and day-to-day feelings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☒5
**Comments:** Strong focus on a single, behavioral target (time management), clear, measurable plan (3 MITs, days, time, alarms) and specified follow-up (Friday check-in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☒3 ☐4 ☐5
**Comments:** Offers choices (3 MITs vs 45‑min block; which days) and asks about barriers, but clinician often supplies solutions early—patient involvement limited to selecting from clinician-defined options.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☒4 ☐5
**Comments:** Consistently honors patient's readiness and "not promising" language, normalizes ambivalence and encourages reaching out to supports. Could further invite patient-generated alternatives to reach top score.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☒4 ☐5
**Comments:** Elicits patient input (readiness ruler, choice of plan, if–then details, barriers) and draws tentative change talk; purposeful questioning supports evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☒4 ☐5
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language ("let’s make a test," offers to check in) and reviews plan collaboratively, though clinician often provides structure/content rather than co-creating fully.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☒3 ☐4 ☐5
**Comments:** Elicits tentative change talk (choice, readiness rating, commitments) but does not consistently amplify or deepen desire, reasons, ability, or commitment statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☒4 ☐5
**Comments:** Effectively normalizes ambivalence and reframes slips ("slips are information"), offers scaled-down alternatives (pause two minutes, pick one MIT), and avoids arguing. Could add more double-sided reflections to further resolve ambivalence.
