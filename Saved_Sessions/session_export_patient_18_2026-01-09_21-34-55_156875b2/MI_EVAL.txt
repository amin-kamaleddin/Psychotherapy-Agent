# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, validating Anya and asking permission before offering strategies.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking (e.g., ‘awkward but manageable’ and noting softened responses), but reflections stayed more behavioral than exploring deeper emotions or meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on handling alcohol offers with practical strategies, rehearsals, and actionable planning throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint effort by offering options, inviting practice, tailoring role-plays, and co-constructing plans with Anya.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported autonomy by following Anya’s lead, offering choices, and asking permission rather than using prescriptive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited preferences and confidence (choice of lines, rehearsal, confidence rating) but missed deeper probes into values or internal motivations to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned Anya as an active co-designer—repeatedly asked permission, offered choices, and deferred to her preferences in selecting and practicing strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created opportunities for change talk through options, rehearsal, and a confidence ruler, but did not consistently amplify emergent change language or follow up on the 7/10 rating.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and effectively pointed out softened refusals, though could more systematically use double-sided or strategic reflections to resolve ambivalence.
