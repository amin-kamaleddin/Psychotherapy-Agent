Normal post-exertion soreness
- Diffuse, achy, comes on gradually after activity, improves with rest/heat/OTC meds, usually mild–moderate (0–4/10).

Concerning signs (reduce activity, call rehab/doctor)
- New sharp or focal pain, worsening over 24–48 hrs, pain >6–7/10 not helped by meds, increasing swelling/warmth, pain with fever.

Urgent red flags — seek immediate care
- New numbness/weakness, saddle anesthesia, loss of bladder/bowel control, sudden severe uncontrollable pain, or signs of DVT (one-leg swelling/redness) or chest symptoms.
