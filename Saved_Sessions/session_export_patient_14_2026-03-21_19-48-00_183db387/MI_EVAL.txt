# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect—validates the patient's frustration about waiting and focuses on workable solutions without blaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening, mirroring the patient's time-pressure theme and priorities to keep them engaged.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, persistent focus on time-efficient physical activity and safety, repeatedly mapping specific, measurable plans to the patient's schedule.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and negotiates timing and tweaks, but relied on a fairly structured, clinician-driven menu with several technical prescriptions rather than eliciting the patient's own exercise structure first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors the patient's choices and capacity—offers options, accepts preferences (email check-in, shortened circuit), and avoids pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete commitment and follow-up plans, but did not systematically explore underlying reasons, values, or confidence to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters shared decision-making by offering options and adapting the plan, yet provided prescriptive technical guidance at times without explicit permission, leaving some clinician-driven power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change language (asked what felt doable, mapped plan), but missed opportunities to probe the patient's deeper reasons/values and to assess importance or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and did not argue with sustain talk about time constraints and normalized barriers, though could have used more strategic reflections to explore ambivalence and further reduce sustain talk.
