### Acceptance
- [x] Respectful, nonjudgmental tone
- [x] Neutral reframing of minimization (e.g., “you call them ‘mini, little’…”)

### Empathy
- [x] Reflective listening / paraphrase of patient content
- [x] Accurate summaries that track shifting priorities
- [ ] Deep emotional reflections (e.g., shame, frustration)
- [ ] Consistent empathic affirmations

#### Score (1–5): 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and accurate paraphrasing that track the patient’s ambivalence. Reflections largely stay at content level and could probe deeper emotional nuance (frustration about nagging, shame, fear of failure).

- [x] Logical progression from reasons → values → plan
- [x] Moves toward a concrete, testable step without coercion

#### Score (1–5): 5
**Comments:** Strong directional focus throughout; each question builds logically toward a specific, testable action while preserving patient choice.

### Collaboration
- [x] Invites patient input and priorities
- [x] Frames decisions as the patient’s to make
- [ ] Co‑creation of plan and follow‑up (limited)

#### Score (1–5): 4
**Comments:** Repeatedly asks for the patient’s views and choices, avoiding an authoritarian stance. Session is clinician‑structured; could increase co‑creation by soliciting alternative strategies or follow‑up preferences.

### Autonomy Support
- [x] Acknowledges patient agency
- [x] Frames options as experiments/tests rather than mandates
- [x] Avoids pressuring language

#### Score (1–5): 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforces autonomy (e.g., “what step could you try this week to test whether it works?”), supporting ownership of any change.

### Evocation

- [x] Asks the patient to prioritize reasons/values

#### Score (1–5): 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out the patient’s reasons (wife, kids, money, work), asks for prioritization, and elicits a past success used to inform a feasible plan.

### Partnership
- [x] Treats patient as expert on his life
- [x] Solicits patient’s views and feasible steps

- [ ] Explicitly invites co‑design of evaluation/follow‑up

#### Score (1–5): 4

### Cultivating Change Talk
- [x] Elicits desire/reasons/values for change
- [x] Amplifies and operationalizes change language (e.g., “trying,” “calmer husband”)
- [x] Secures a concrete commitment/testable step

#### Score (1–5): 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and builds change talk, moving the patient to articulate values and commit to a concrete, testable step (leaving cigars in locker / outside‑only on breaks).

---

### Softening Sustain Talk
- [x] Avoids reinforcing sustain talk/minimization
- [ ] Uses double‑sided reflections to bridge sustain and change talk
- [ ] Explicitly acknowledges sustain talk as a bridge to change

#### Score (1–5): 3
**Comments:** The clinician redirects away from sustain talk and does not reinforce it, but missed opportunities to use double‑sided reflections or explicitly acknowledge sustain talk to diffuse defensiveness.
