# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized the patient's stance, and explicitly validated their perspective throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, rapport-building reflections but many were surface-level and repetitive; the clinician did not probe deeper emotions or values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained focus on smoking and alcohol with targeted questions and practical strategies, but did not push to explore discrepancy or consolidate a negotiated next step when change language appeared.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative—solicited patient preferences, co-offered strategies, and honored the patient's choice to lead the agenda.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly and explicitly supported autonomy by emphasizing choice, lack of pressure, and offering help only as optional.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to draw out the patient's perspective and gather information, but questions were more informational than evocative and did not elicit personal reasons or values for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a strong partnership stance by inviting input, offering options, and following the patient's lead throughout the session.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Efforts to elicit change talk were minimal and passive; the clinician missed opportunities to amplify tentative change language or ask evocative DARN-C questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and validated sustain talk but did not use active strategies (e.g., double-sided reflections) to soften sustain talk or surface ambivalence.
