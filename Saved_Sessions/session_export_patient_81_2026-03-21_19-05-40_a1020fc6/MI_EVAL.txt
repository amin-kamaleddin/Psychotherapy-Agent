# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalized feelings, and affirmed the client's experience throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking with timely summaries and reflections that helped the client feel understood and open up.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on meth use with concrete behavioral goals and measurable next steps (sober audition days, cash envelope, Ben check‑in).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative: the clinician invited input, adjusted language to the client's voice, rehearsed responses, and co‑constructed plans.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirmed the client's right to choose, offered options rather than directives, and checked willingness before planning to reinforce ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons and some motivation but relied more on problem‑solving; could more fully draw out values and use scaling to strengthen intrinsic commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as a genuine collaborator by offering options, asking permission, and inviting the client to co‑create and tweak the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created opportunities and reinforced change talk and specific commitments, but missed chances to use more open questions and scaling to amplify importance and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and gently redirected toward harm reduction, though double‑sided reflections and reframing sustain statements were used less often than ideal.
