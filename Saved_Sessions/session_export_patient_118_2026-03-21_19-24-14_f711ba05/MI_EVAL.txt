# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and honored the patient's wish not to discuss smoking, avoiding blame or judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and acknowledged concerns about lectures and dental priorities, but missed opportunities to reflect his emotional fatigue to deepen connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on dental treatment logistics and concrete next steps while declining to press smoking, keeping the session productive and goal‑directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as joint problem‑solving with frequent invitations for input and adaptation to his preferences, co‑creating practical artifacts like scripts and an email.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported the patient's choice by offering options, asking permission, and explicitly honoring his request to leave smoking out unless he chose otherwise.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Did little to draw out internal motivations for smoking change and missed brief, permission‑based opportunities to elicit even minimal change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as an equal collaborator, offering choices and co‑constructing the plan (scripts, email, role‑play) rather than prescribing.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made almost no effort to elicit the patient's motivations for changing smoking behavior and did not reflect or amplify potential change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk by accepting the refusal and redirecting to useful, patient‑centered tasks, preventing escalation of resistance.
