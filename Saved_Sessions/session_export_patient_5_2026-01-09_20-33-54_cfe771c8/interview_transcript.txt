PSYCHIATRIST: Citalopram is an SSRI antidepressant. Common side effects: nausea, dry mouth, sleep changes, sweating, tremor, headache, and sexual problems. Less common but serious: low sodium (especially in older adults), bleeding risk, and at high doses possible heart rhythm changes.

It does interact dangerously with phenelzine (an MAOI): together they can cause serotonin syndrome (high fever, fast heart rate, severe stiffness, confusion) — potentially life‑threatening. Do NOT combine them; a prescriber-supervised washout (usually ~14 days) is required when switching. If you’re taking either now, contact your prescriber or ER for urgent advice.
