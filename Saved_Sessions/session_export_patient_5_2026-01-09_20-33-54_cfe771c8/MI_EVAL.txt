# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental from the opening through repeated reassurance; tone remains calm and validating even when giving urgent instructions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and normalizes the patient's worry with brief reflective statements, showing perspective-taking, but reflections are often informational rather than deeper emotional elaborations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused agenda on the citalopram–phenelzine interaction, immediate safety steps, and ED expectations with concise, practical guidance throughout.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Provides actionable advice and asks for specific information, but the exchange reads more expert-led than a co‑constructed, two‑way problem-solving process and rarely solicits feasibility or preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supports autonomy by discouraging unilateral stopping and outlining options and expectations, though it could more explicitly invite the patient's preferences and decision-making role.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Uses few open questions or reflective strategies to elicit the patient's intrinsic motivations or values; the session focuses on clinical directives rather than drawing out personal reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative elements (e.g., asking husband to bring bottles, recommending contacts) are present, but the clinician largely assumes the expert role and could ask more about the patient's perspective and experience.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces help-seeking and encourages immediate protective actions, but does not actively elicit the patient's own reasons, goals, or confidence for following through, missing opportunities to amplify change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and legitimizes the patient's reluctance to stop phenelzine and avoids confrontation while recommending supervised tapering, though uses few double-sided reflections to contrast concerns and safety needs.
