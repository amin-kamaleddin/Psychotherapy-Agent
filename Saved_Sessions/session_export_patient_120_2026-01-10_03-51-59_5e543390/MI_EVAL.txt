# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Sofia's reactions, reducing defensiveness and supporting disclosure throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used strong reflections and validations linking context and emotion, but often moved quickly to problem-solving instead of deepening affect-focused reflection on guilt and fear.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in pregnancy with concrete, actionable weekend and exit plans that kept the session on target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative stance—co-created plans, solicited Sofia's preferences, and adjusted interventions based on her choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by asking permission, offering choices, and affirming Sofia's control over decisions and pacing without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used the importance ruler and elicited reasons for change, but could further probe values, past successes, and elicit more client-generated strategies to build confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered partnership and power-sharing through offering options, asking which to start with, and inviting Sofia to direct role-play and agenda choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change-oriented statements (e.g., the 7 rating and planning), but missed opportunities to amplify commitment language and assess confidence for a specific commitment step.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and stayed nonjudgmental, but made limited use of double-sided or evocative reflections to strategically soften ambivalence before moving to solutions.
