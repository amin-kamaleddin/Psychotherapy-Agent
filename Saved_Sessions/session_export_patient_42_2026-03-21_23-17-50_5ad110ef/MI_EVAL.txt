# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence with frequent validating statements (e.g., “That pull sounds powerful and conflicting”). Tone is nonjudgmental and supportive throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate paraphrases and client language effectively, showing good perspective-taking (e.g., “instant‑friend vibe”). Some reflections are repetitive and could probe deeper into underlying feelings like loneliness or fear of rejection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on smoking reduction while preserving social connection, moving logically from values to a specific, time-limited plan (one cigarette per visit).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co‑constructed: the clinician invites Elise’s preferences, builds on her ideas, and offers to shape wording and supports together rather than prescribing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently presents options, emphasizes that Elise doesn’t have to do everything at once, and repeatedly asks which approach feels acceptable to her, reinforcing her sense of choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Elicits Elise’s own motivations and resources with evocative questions and leverages past successes (no‑weekday‑drinks) to build a change plan, drawing on intrinsic reasons and strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Uses inclusive language and shared decision-making (e.g., “We can figure this out together”), inviting Elise to choose priorities and shape the plan, which fosters a genuine partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk by asking for a small, doable step and past successes, but could more often amplify, summarize, and elicit confidence/commitment language to strengthen resolve.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Rolls with sustain talk and validates the social benefits, then redirects to partial-change options; however, several reflections echo sustain talk verbatim and would benefit from more double-sided reflections or strategic summaries.
