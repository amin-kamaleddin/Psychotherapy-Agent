# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and respect for the patient's wishes, avoiding judgment or pressure. Repeatedly honors privacy and offers support without coercion.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking but reflections are somewhat formulaic. Few attempts are made to reflect underlying emotions or deeper nuances.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on initial engagement and safety throughout the session. Provides crisis resources and keeps discussion tied to the session target without drifting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively solicits the patient's preferences and follows them, framing the encounter as client-directed. Decisions about contact and disclosure are left to the patient, demonstrating genuine power-sharing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports patient choice by repeatedly emphasizing control and offering options while avoiding directives. Honors the request for silence and frames offers as optional.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Makes limited efforts to draw out the patient's own motivations; an initial open question is present but no gentle probes are used after resistance. Missed opportunities for subtle invitations or scaling to test for openings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the clinician as a collaborative partner and defers to the patient's authority over contact and disclosure. Consistently asks permission and follows the patient's lead.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 1
**Comments:** Essentially no elicitation of change talk after the opening question; the clinician moved quickly to honor silence without offering low-risk evocation. No curiosity, values prompts, or scaling were attempted to invite change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively neutralizes sustain talk by avoiding arguing or persuasion and respecting the patient's statements. Reframes safety concerns to preserve the patient's control and de-escalate resistance.
