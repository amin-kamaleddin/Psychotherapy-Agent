**Comments:** Elicited factual information and heard some patient change talk, but did not systematically elicit or deepen the patient's intrinsic motivations, values, or discrepancies (few open questions aimed at eliciting why change matters to him).

### Partnership

**Comments:** Tone intermittently collaborative (scripts, offer to help message PCP), but clinician frequently adopted an expert stance with didactic information delivery and multiple abrupt session closings that reduced sense of ongoing partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Patient expressed clear change talk (desire/need for medication to quit). Clinician acknowledged it but rarely reflected or amplified those statements or probed for commitment/confidence language to strengthen motivation.

---

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):**  [ ] 1  [ ] 2  [x] 3  [ ] 4  [ ] 5  
**Comments:** Clinician recognized and normalized sustain talk (stress, triggers, side‑effect concerns) and offered pragmatic solutions.
