# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing the patient's worries and affirming practical coping efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-quality reflective listening and accurate perspective-taking, with timely reflections that capture and validate the patient's core concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, appropriate focus on arranging urgent tests and referrals, but misses opportunities to use that focus to scaffold movement toward later psychological or self-management steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative interaction with co-produced plans, repeated invitations for patient input, and concrete offers of help (scripts, written notes) that defer to patient priorities.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms the patient's right to choose, offers options without pressure, and supports the patient's timing and decisions about psychological or management steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some elicitation of priorities but limited effort to draw out internal motives, values, or confidence; few follow-ups to explore what change would mean for the patient.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a partnership stance and shares power, asking preferences and co-constructing concrete actions such as referral content and who should attend appointments.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits conditional change talk and encourages coping strategies, but does not consistently amplify or explore the patient's existing change language and motivations.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Validates and reflects sustain talk which preserves rapport, but does not actively soften it or use double-sided reflections to open space for alternative explanations or steps.
