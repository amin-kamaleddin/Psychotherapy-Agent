# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a consistently nonjudgmental, respectful stance with normalization and safety/confidentiality checks, and avoided shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid empathic listening, brief reflections, and permission‑seeking, but missed opportunities for deeper or more complex reflections to capture emotional nuance and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on cannabis use related to stress and school, producing a concrete, measurable 2‑week experiment, log format, and scheduled follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative—solicited priorities, negotiated logistics, and co‑created the plan—though parts of the session shifted toward a clinician‑driven plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by asking permission, offering multiple options, and framing the trial as a curiosity‑driven experiment to preserve client choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some interest in the patient's preferences, but relied more on clinician options and structure and missed chances to elicit personal values, reasons, or use evocative scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered partnership through shared decisions and permission‑seeking, yet occasionally led with a preconstructed plan instead of inviting the patient to design alternatives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited tentative change language and praised commitment but did not systematically probe, amplify, or elicit stronger reasons, confidence, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk which avoided confrontation, but did not consistently use double‑sided reflections or selectively reinforce small change talk to shift ambivalence.
