# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, normalizing the patient’s experience and avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic understanding with validating reflections and normalizing statements but stops short of deeper emotional exploration of loneliness and raw adolescent affect.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on weight-related habits and social support, with a concrete two-week plan tied directly to the patient's goals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a collaborative tone by offering choices and adapting suggestions, though the interaction leans toward clinician-driven solutions and specific prescriptive ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports autonomy by offering options, checking permission before giving advice, and inviting the patient to choose and tweak the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some commitment and choice but relies more on offering solutions than drawing out the patient's intrinsic reasons, values, or confidence for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership through shared decision-making and checking preferences, but occasionally adopts an expert stance with prescriptive meal plans that shifts some control away from the patient.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generates some change talk and commitments (e.g., breakfast/snack swaps) but does more telling than eliciting the patient's personal motivations, reasons, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes discouraging beliefs with normalizing, low-pressure language, though could use more double-sided reflections to acknowledge ambivalence.
