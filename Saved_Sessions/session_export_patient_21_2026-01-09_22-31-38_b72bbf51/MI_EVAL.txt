# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently displayed warmth, respect, and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing concerns and validating strengths. Praise for practical plans and unconditional positive regard made it safe for Camila to share ambivalence.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening with accurate paraphrases and validations (e.g., 'I hear you'), but some reflections were broad and could be deepened. More affect‑focused or complex reflections would better capture emotional nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained clear focus on increasing physical activity with specific, measurable options and a one‑week experiment. The clinician kept conversation goal-directed with concrete planning and follow‑up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as collaborative problem‑solving: plans were co‑constructed, messages co‑written, and follow‑up preferences honored. Camila was treated as an equal partner whose choices directed the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported choice and ownership, offering options and inviting Camila to pick or tweak plans. The one‑week experiment was framed as a trial and pressure was avoided.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited reasons, strengths, and confidence effectively (e.g., 0–10 scaling and what would raise it), but missed some opportunities to amplify and consolidate change talk into firmer commitment language. More targeted evocative questions linking behavior to values would strengthen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a genuine partnership stance by offering options, asking permission, and adapting language to Camila's voice. Power was shared throughout planning and follow‑up decisions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicited and reinforced Camila's change talk using open questions, summaries, doable options, and confidence scaling. The clinician amplified plans and set concrete steps that supported enactment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk without reinforcing it, then pivoted to contingency plans and small achievable steps. This reduced the influence of worries while validating concerns.
