### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard—normalized ambivalence and offered reassurance without blame. Statements validated Camila’s situation and choices throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated high empathic attunement with frequent accurate reflections, complex paraphrases, and summaries of emotional tone. The clinician repeatedly reflected specific concerns (time, energy, fear of failure).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing brief walks and actionable implementation details. The conversation stayed centered on concrete steps rather than drifting to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the session as joint problem‑solving—invited Camila’s input and integrated her past strategies into the plan. Power was shared and decisions were co‑created.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by offering multiple choices, normalizing any decision, and repeatedly asking which options felt realistic. No coercion; Camila’s preferences drove the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Camila’s own motives and values (energy, health, keeping up with partner) and used them to shape the plan. Open questions and requests for doable steps expanded her reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently co‑created the plan, invited her expertise, and framed suggestions as options rather than prescriptions. The clinician engaged Camila as an equal partner in decision‑making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
