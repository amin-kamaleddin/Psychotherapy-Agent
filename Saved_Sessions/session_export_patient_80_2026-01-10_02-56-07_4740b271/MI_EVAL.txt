# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validated Luka's efforts and strengths without judgment, creating a warm, safe tone for disclosure.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening and accurate reflections of progress and worries, though could have probed emotional content and underlying feelings more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on reducing drinking and preventing violence, offering a practical 3-step plan and sequencing strategies while checking readiness and safety.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative with co-created plans, invitations to choose steps, role‑play, and adaptations based on Luka's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported Luka's sense of choice and control by offering options, asking which step felt doable, and respecting his readiness rating.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivations and resources using open questions and a readiness ruler, but could have probed deeper into values and personal meanings to strengthen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently adopted a partnership stance, deferring to Luka's expertise and inviting his input on what felt most doable and realistic.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with questions, affirmations, and concrete next steps, though there were missed chances to amplify reasons and values for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected to change strategies, but did not use explicit double‑sided reflections or explicitly validate the pull of alcohol to resolve ambivalence more fully.
