# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed respect and nonjudgmental regard, normalizing difficulties and validating small successes (e.g., 22 days). Language avoided labeling or blame and honored the client's experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Multiple accurate reflections and perspective-taking demonstrated attentive listening, tying call anxiety to behavior and goals. Responses matched Timur's ambivalence and emotional state, showing clear emotional attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on the specific target behavior (contacting probation about courses/jobs) and every turn advanced a concrete implementation plan. The clinician moved smoothly from brainstorming to a clear, actionable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as a genuine two-way collaboration with frequent choice language and co-construction of the script and backup plan. The clinician repeatedly asked which options felt most doable and tailored the plan to Timur's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supported Timur's control by offering options and avoiding pressure, honoring limits like reluctance for check-ins. Choices about script, timing, and backup preserved the client's autonomy throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician successfully evoked practical motivation and commitment by generating a specific plan, but tended to emphasize logistics over deeper elicitation of core values. Brief pauses to explore what matters (e.g., Fiona, small joys) could have strengthened intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician maintained a partnership stance, inviting Timur to choose wording and process and sharing power in decision-making. Repeated offers to write or rehearse and reliance on Timur's judgment reinforced collaboration.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician actively elicited and amplified change talk, moving from general intent to concrete action steps (script, day/time, reminder, backup text). Questions about what felt doable reinforced Timur's expressed commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward practical steps, but missed chances to explicitly reflect or reframe sustain talk (e.g., beliefs about laziness or acceptance). Using double-sided or empathic reflections could have further softened resistance.
