# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed respect and nonjudgmental regard through validation and normalization of Dante’s experience, avoiding blame throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used frequent reflective, situational, and affective statements that showed understanding, but missed opportunities for deeper, more complex reflections on ambivalence and emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on restarting physical activity with concrete, behavior-linked actions and minimal topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session felt collaborative with invitations to choose barriers and experiments, though clinician-delivered solution lists sometimes dominated instead of eliciting the patient's ideas first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and reinforcing ownership without pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made some attempts to draw out motivation via choices and check-ins, but did not extensively elicit internal motives, values, or scaled readiness to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Invited input and negotiated plans, yet frequently shifted to providing expert suggestions which reduced opportunities for the client to co-create solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened space for and reinforced small change talk and wins, but did not systematically elicit or amplify desire/ability/reason/need or stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk and doubt without shaming, but did not use MI-specific reframing (e.g., double-sided reflections or ambivalence summaries) to weaken sustain language.
