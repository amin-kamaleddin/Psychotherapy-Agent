# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance (e.g., “I hear you,” “I won’t pry”), normalizing the patient's privacy and choice throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated a supportive empathic stance and attended to safety and tiredness, but reflections were somewhat formulaic and could deepen with more complex reflections of underlying emotion or mistrust.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on initial engagement, safety, and rapport-building, repeatedly returning to this goal without drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance by offering options, seeking the patient's preference, and deferring to the patient's pace, reinforcing a joint, non-expert-driven interaction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy with repeated affirmations of choice and control (e.g., “You’re in control,” “I won’t push”), consistently avoiding coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Limited effort to elicit the patient's internal motivation or values; interventions focused on presence and safety rather than open, curiosity-driven questions or scaling to draw out change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed that the patient is the expert on her needs and offered control, but could more explicitly invite agenda-setting or summarize and ask permission before offering suggestions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Made passive offers and choices but did not actively elicit desire, ability, reasons, need, or commitment language; more targeted open questions and invitations to explore change were needed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing or confronting resistance by honoring privacy and not arguing, though could enhance strategy with double-sided reflections to gently highlight discrepancies.
