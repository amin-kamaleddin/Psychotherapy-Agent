# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental tone, normalizing difficulty and avoiding blame. Offered support and warmth throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic listening with accurate reflections of triggers and relational worries, but some reflections stayed surface-level. Missed opportunities to deepen affective understanding of guilt and physiological anxiety.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use in pregnancy with each intervention tied to reducing drinking. Session was well-organized around concrete action planning and succinct information-giving.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a genuine partnership by following the patient’s preferences, offering role-play, and co-creating specific steps. Clinician positioned themselves as a partner rather than sole authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported autonomy by asking permission before offering information and presenting multiple strategy options. Emphasized the patient’s choices and invited her selection of approaches.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited intrinsic motivation using the importance scale and follow-up questions, then amplified the patient’s reasons and commitments. Converted motivations into concrete, actionable steps and rehearsals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited the patient’s input and framed the work as collaborative, offering choices and tailoring practice to her preferences. Shared power was evident in decision-making and planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently evoked, reflected, and amplified change talk using open questions, a 0–10 importance scale, and requests for specific steps and commitments. Reinforced concrete plans (NA drink, telling Camila, Mom check-in).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk by acknowledging concerns and offering practical strategies, but relied more on pragmatic redirection. Could use more double-sided reflections and values-based exploration to explicitly soften ambivalence.
