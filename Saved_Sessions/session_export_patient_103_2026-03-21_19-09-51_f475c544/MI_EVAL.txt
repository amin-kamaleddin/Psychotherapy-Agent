# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental respect, validating the patient's feelings and offering options rather than blaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, mirroring the patient's priorities and affect while checking safety and maintaining an empathic tone.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, appropriate focus on managing anger and obtaining documentation, repeatedly guiding the session toward concrete scripts and immediate actions.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was co-constructed: invited input, iteratively refined scripts, rehearsed language, and problem-solved together rather than lecturing.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported choice by offering explicit options, respecting the patient's decisions, and tailoring interventions to the patient's preferences and control over escalation.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited practical ideas and commitments from the patient but did not sufficiently draw out deeper motivations, values, or confidence to strengthen intrinsic commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance throughout, sharing power with the patient by co-creating scripts and centering the patient's expertise and goals.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully encouraged concrete change-oriented language and commitments (scripts, rehearsal), though missed opportunities to evoke deeper reasons and confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated frustration and redirected toward problem-solving to reduce sustain talk, but could increase use of double-sided reflections to further soften resistance.
