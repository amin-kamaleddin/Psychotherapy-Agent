# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing the client's doubt and fatigue and offering explicit affirmations of effort.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong, accurate reflective listening that captured both content and feeling, paraphrasing ambivalence and exhaustion to build rapport and understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear focus on self-harm safety, moving the session toward practical, concrete steps with immediate timelines and indicators of success.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative—asked for the client's ideas, checked which options felt doable, and co-constructed the safety plan rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the client's choice and control, validated limits and ambivalence, and invited the client to choose small, manageable steps without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited the client's own motives and problem-solving (e.g., ice, band‑aids, removing sharps, texting roommate), promoting self-generated strategies and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the interaction as an equal partnership, deferring to the client's expertise and jointly deciding which immediate actions to take.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced several change statements and small commitments, but could more systematically amplify and consolidate these into stronger commitment language or use scaling rulers.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided confrontation and reflected sustain talk while redirecting to safer alternatives, though more double-sided reflections and summary statements could further reduce sustain talk.
