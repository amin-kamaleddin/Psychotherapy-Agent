# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician establishes a clear nonjudgmental stance and consistently uses validating, normalizing language that conveys unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** There are multiple empathic moments and clarification of ambiguous language, but reflective listening is relatively brief and the clinician moves quickly to information-gathering and solutions rather than deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains a strong, consistent focus on reducing problematic drinking with concrete, behaviorally specific goals and strategies linked to the client's concerns.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is cooperative and tailored: the clinician repeatedly asks for the client's preferences, offers choices, and adapts scripts to Camila's voice while sharing expertise when requested.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently supports choice and control by offering multiple options, conditional phrasing, and honoring Camila's preference for moderation rather than pressuring for abstinence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some internal motivation is elicited (readiness rating, doable strategies), but the clinician largely supplies solutions and misses opportunities to explore values, personal meaning, and to amplify commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The stance is collaborative and invites client input, but offering a fairly detailed prescriptive plan early in the session reduces the sense of co-constructed expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Clinician elicits an important readiness rating and a brief change statement but does not follow up to deepen or amplify desire, ability, reasons, or commitment language before moving to solutions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician acknowledges sustain talk and avoids arguing, offering respectful social strategies and scripts, though stronger MI techniques (e.g., double-sided reflections or strategic summaries) could further neutralize sustain talk.
