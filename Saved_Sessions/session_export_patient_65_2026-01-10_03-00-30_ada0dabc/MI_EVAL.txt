# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician is consistently nonjudgmental and validating, normalizing the client's worries and conveying unconditional positive regard throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frequent, accurate reflections capture both factual and emotional content and are followed by invitations to choose next steps, demonstrating strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintains clear focus on completing court-ordered hours and repeatedly steers toward concrete, actionable tasks (confirm hours, identify sites, scripts, tracking).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician regularly asks for the client's preferences and tailors options, promoting teamwork, though they sometimes take the lead in problem-solving rather than co-generating solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently affirms choice and control by offering options and accepting the client's reservations, preserving the client's right to decide.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Interaction is solution-focused with little elicitation of the client's internal reasons, values, or motivations; evocative questions about importance or personal benefits are largely absent.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician frames the work as a joint process and invites input, but often supplies detailed plans instead of explicitly inviting the client to co-create them, limiting full parity.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are pragmatic prompts and choice questions that could invite change talk, but the clinician rarely deepens or amplifies tentative change statements and moves quickly into problem-solving.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician neutrally acknowledges and echoes reluctance without reinforcing sustain language, but relies more on logistical planning than strategic reflections or reframes to shift sustain talk toward change.
