# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveyed consistent unconditional positive regard and normalized Elena’s efforts in a warm, nonjudgmental way. Repeated affirmations and acceptance of limitations supported rapport throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking and reflective summaries that validated stressors and ambivalence. Could probe and reflect deeper emotional content (fear, guilt, exhaustion) to reach the highest level.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on parental smoking cessation with concrete, actionable next steps. A stronger push to elicit confidence/importance ratings or consolidate commitment earlier would improve direction.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was highly collaborative with shared problem-solving, checking preferences, and co-creating a small behavioral plan. The clinician avoided an expert-only stance and tailored suggestions to Elena’s constraints.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly offered options, asked permission, and respected Elena’s expressed hesitancy without pressure. Consistently reinforced her control over decisions and next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited personally meaningful motivators (child’s health) and a specific action (buy gum), emphasizing Elena’s own reasons for change. Could deepen evocation by exploring past successes and using scaling or amplifying reflections.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited Elena’s input and treated her as the expert on her life, co-creating the plan and offering to help in ways she preferred. The session felt like joint problem-solving throughout.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Successfully elicited change language and a realistic first step, asking what felt doable. Did not consistently amplify or build on client change statements to strengthen commitment and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk without confronting it but missed opportunities for double-sided reflections or reframing to reduce its stickiness. More strategic softening (e.g., juxtaposing reasons for/against) would help shift sustain statements.
