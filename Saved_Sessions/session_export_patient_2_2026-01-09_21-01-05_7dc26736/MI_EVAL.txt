# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence while avoiding judgmental language. Could add occasional explicit normalization of slip‑ups to further strengthen acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high‑quality reflective listening and perspective‑taking, accurately naming underlying feelings and constraints. Brief reflective summaries tying emotions to core values could deepen empathy further.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on changing the morning routine with questions and reflections tied to concrete behavioral steps and proximal outcomes. The session moves logically from values to a specific plan without drifting off topic.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co‑constructed, inviting patient input at each decision point and integrating her language into the plan. Continuing to check fit with the patient's priorities at follow‑ups will help sustain this strength.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supports choice by offering options, asking about feasibility, and validating realistic caveats, which preserves patient ownership. The non‑coercive approach reinforces control over decisions and the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation by linking change to personally meaningful values and anticipated benefits, producing concrete commitments. Asking the patient to state her top reasons for change in her own words could further solidify motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the work as collaborative and defers to the patient's expertise, co‑creating a realistic plan and inviting choice. Having the patient summarize the plan back would further cement ownership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk and confidence statements with evocative questions and reflections, leading to concrete commitments. Occasional use of a numerical confidence ruler might further strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and neutrally reframes ambivalence, shifting toward solutions and values. Could more explicitly use strategies like double‑sided reflections or selectively amplifying brief pro‑change statements to further soften sustain talk.
