# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician maintained a respectful, nonjudgmental tone throughout, validated Maria's concerns and time constraints, and treated her research and worries as legitimate.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking mirrored both affect and content, acknowledging frustration, time pressure, and specific side-effect worries.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed tightly focused on asthma management and the inhaled-steroid decision, producing clear, actionable outcomes like printable questions and follow-up plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly cooperative approach: the clinician repeatedly asked for Maria's preferences and co-created the exact wording to hand to clinic staff rather than dictating choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently reinforced Maria's control over decisions, offered options and documentation of her preferences, and honored her boundary about refusing steroids without conditions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some attempts to elicit Maria's reasons were made, but the interaction prioritized pragmatic scripting over exploring intrinsic motivation, importance, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician invited collaboration at each step, checked wording, and shared decision-making by offering choices about tone, firmness, and content to match Maria's needs.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There was limited elicitation of change talk and reinforcement of attendance, but the clinician did not sufficiently pursue or deepen the patient's implicit change statements (importance/confidence).

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician avoided arguing and reframed skepticism into practical alternatives and scripts, though more use of double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence would have strengthened the approach.
