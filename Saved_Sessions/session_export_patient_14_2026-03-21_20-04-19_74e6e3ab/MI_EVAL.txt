# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, sustained unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language throughout the session, with supportive statements and normalization of the patient's concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and concise reflective listening that validates time constraints and priorities; could deepen emotional reflection and explore ambivalence more fully.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a focused agenda on time-efficient behavior change and moves the conversation consistently toward a concrete, measurable implementation plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is clearly co-constructed with the patient choosing and modifying offered options, and language frames the work as a joint effort.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes patient choice, asks permission before offering options, and supports the patient's control over pacing and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits practical reasons and plans using scaling and questions, but could probe deeper into personally meaningful values and intrinsic motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clearly collaborative and respectful of the patient's preferences, though initial presentation of concrete options leaned slightly toward clinician expertise before eliciting more of the patient's own ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and planning, using confidence scaling and commitment language; could amplify desire/importance and use more evocative questions to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and works around sustain talk without arguing, offering pragmatic alternatives; could more actively soften sustain talk with double-sided or strategically tipping reflections.
