# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with frequent affirmations and normalization; clinician communicates warm acceptance and validates the client's priorities and efforts.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good perspective-taking and some reflective listening with a supportive tone; more extended reflections on emotional experience would deepen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on translating the client's value into a specific habit and actionable plan; keeps the conversation oriented toward implementation and problem-solving.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative with joint decision-making, co-created scripts, and frequent solicitation of the client's input; clinician adapts to the client's wording preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and framing plans as experiments; affirms the client's preferences and ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation by asking for concrete habits, start dates, and commitments, drawing on the client's values; however, frequent clinician prescriptions reduce opportunities to further evoke the client's own reasons and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positions the client as the agent of change and invites collaboration through options and permission; recommendations are offered as suggestions rather than directives.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces action-oriented language and converts values into specific change talk; could more deliberately reflect and amplify client change statements to strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Limits sustain talk by steering toward low-pressure, doable steps and optional strategies; explicitly acknowledging small hesitations with double-sided reflections could further reduce sustain talk.
