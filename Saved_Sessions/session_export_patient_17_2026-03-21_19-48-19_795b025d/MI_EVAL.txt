# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with early warm framing and multiple validations; avoided blaming and normalized common reactions to quitting.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and acknowledged ambivalence/emotions, but reflections tended to summarize facts rather than deeply explore shame and stigma.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation with concrete behavioral targets and minimal topic drift throughout the session.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Overall collaborative tone with offers of scripts and notes, but the clinician frequently provided ready-made plans instead of jointly constructing them with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed choice and presented options (quit date vs gradual reduction), respected caution about meds, and avoided pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some efforts to draw out motivation (open questions, goals), but the clinician moved quickly into technical planning rather than using more evocative questions to deepen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invited patient preferences at points, yet much of the interaction became clinician-driven with directive prescriptions rather than true co-creation of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supported and reinforced some change talk and clarified goals, but missed opportunities to systematically elicit and amplify the patient’s own reasons, confidence, and past successes.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk, normalized slips, and framed gradual reduction helpfully, though could use more strategic double-sided reflections or reframing to further blunt resistance.
