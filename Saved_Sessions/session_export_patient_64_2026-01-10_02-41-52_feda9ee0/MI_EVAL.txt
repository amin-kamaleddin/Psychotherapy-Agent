# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating the patient's concerns and motives while avoiding labels or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good understanding by reflecting core worries and normalizing concerns, but missed opportunities for deeper affect exploration and more complex reflections of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept clear focus on the vaccination decision throughout, consistently steering toward practical, low-burden steps and avoiding topic drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a true collaborative exchange—invited input, incorporated wording edits, and co-created scripts and a manager note to support shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, asking what the patient wanted, and avoiding pressure while leaving the decision to the patient.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some family-focused motivation but relied heavily on information and problem-solving rather than systematically drawing out the patient's intrinsic reasons or using scaling questions.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveyed a collaborative stance and invited agenda-setting, though at times shifted into expert information-giving without explicitly asking permission to share.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Affirmed change-relevant motivation and asked open logistical questions, but did not sufficiently amplify, follow up, or summarize the patient's own reasons to deepen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and avoided arguing, but primarily responded with factual information instead of using double-sided or complex reflections to soften resistance.
