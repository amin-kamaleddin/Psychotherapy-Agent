# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, used affirmations and normalization, and explicitly acknowledged Marcus' constraints, creating a warm, nonjudgmental stance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good perspective-taking and reflective listening but reflections were mainly pragmatic rather than exploring deeper emotions like frustration or hopes, leaving opportunities to deepen emotional understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity with concrete options, a specific weekly plan, and a follow-up request, avoiding drift or vagueness.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Co‑created the plan and solicited Marcus' preferences, but frequently led with long menus of prescriptive options rather than eliciting Marcus' solutions first.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, respected Marcus' decisions, and invited him to select what felt doable, reinforcing his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some intrinsic motivators (enjoyment of walking/dancing and family support) but relied heavily on clinician-provided solutions and missed opportunities to draw out deeper values and reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative relationship with shared decision-making on days/times and labels, yet often shifted agenda-setting by offering detailed prescriptive lists.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced change talk with targeted questions, praise, and summaries, but could have used more open, evocative questions to deepen desire, ability, and reasons language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Successfully validated barriers and immediately offered small, feasible alternatives and contingency plans, normalizing setbacks and reducing sustain talk and defensiveness.
