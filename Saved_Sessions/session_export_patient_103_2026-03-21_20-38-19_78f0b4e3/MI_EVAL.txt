# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Opened with an explicit nonjudgmental statement and consistently validated the client's feelings, demonstrating strong unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate, frequent reflections that captured emotion and practical impact, but missed a few opportunities for deeper exploratory reflections probing meaning and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on managing appointment delays and protecting work time, consistently steering the conversation toward concrete, actionable solutions and a testable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Structured the interaction as joint problem-solving by offering options, co-writing and refining scripts, roleplaying, and inviting the client's edits and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported the client's choice and control by offering options, asking which felt doable, and avoiding pressure while honoring the client's caution and skepticism.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Drew out the client's own solutions and concrete commitments using client language and roleplay, but could have more deeply elicited values and used scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the client as the expert, invited choice and wording edits, and genuinely shared power in creating and rehearsing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the client's ideas and converted them into specific, testable steps and scripts, though could have more consistently amplified and summarized change language and probed confidence/commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and reframed skepticism without arguing and offered low-effort experiments, but could use more strategic double-sided reflections to shift momentum away from sustain talk.
