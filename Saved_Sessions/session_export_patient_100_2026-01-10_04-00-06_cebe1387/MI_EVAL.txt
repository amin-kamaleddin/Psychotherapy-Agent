### Acceptance

### Empathy

**Comments:** Strong reflective listening and naming of feelings capture Maya's ambivalence, but several reflections are repetitive rather than deepening emotional understanding.

### Direction

**Comments:** Session stays focused on avoiding drinking at parties and protecting goals, moving clearly toward a concrete, time‑bound behavioral plan.

### Collaboration

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership

**Comments:** The clinician invites Maya's input and co-constructs decisions, yet could further foreground her expertise by having her summarize next steps or generate contingency plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk and specifics for action, but would benefit from more explicit amplification of commitment and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sustain talk is acknowledged and normalized without confrontation, but there is limited strategic work (e.g., double-sided reflections or reframing) to shift sustain language toward change.
