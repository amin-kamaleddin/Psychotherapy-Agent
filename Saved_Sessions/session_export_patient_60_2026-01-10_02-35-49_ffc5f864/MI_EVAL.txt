# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalizing Jason's feelings and creating safety and trust throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong, accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking captured both content and feelings repeatedly, checking understanding before moving forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on resolving the conduct referral with actionable planning and rehearsal, avoiding drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely collaborative interaction with frequent invitations to co-create wording and strategy, privileging Jason's ideas and preferences in planning.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirmed Jason's choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and letting him decide how to present and rehearse his statement.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited goals and pragmatic preferences about outcomes but did not consistently evoke intrinsic motivation or reasons to change drinking behavior or accept responsibility.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently fostered partnership and shared control with collaborative language and choices, positioning the clinician as a teammate rather than an authority.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal effort to elicit change talk about alcohol use or responsibility; conversation focused on procedural outcomes rather than desire, ability, reasons, or commitment to change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided confrontation and rolled with resistance using reflections and redirection, though could have used more double-sided or strategic reflections to shift sustain talk toward change.
