# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Conveys consistent nonjudgmental support and validates Sofia's distress, making her feel heard. Occasionally shifts quickly into practical suggestions in ways that feel slightly prescriptive rather than deeply affirming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate empathic reflections and normalizing statements that prompted disclosures and engagement. Reflections are often concise and corrective, with missed opportunities for more complex emotion-focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on coping with rumors, reducing checking, and cautious re-engagement with the friend. Repeatedly returns to concrete, observable goals and balances safety with a practical action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative: decisions and plans are co-constructed, and the clinician invites Sofia's input and offers role-play and drafting support. Rarely, language becomes more directive which slightly reduces client-led problem solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, respects Sofia's choices (e.g., not involving adults), and avoids pressuring her, supporting her sense of control. Framing actions as Sofia's decision is evident throughout the session.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivation and immediate commitments (breathing, drafting, checks) but does not sufficiently probe values, goals, or discrepancy to deepen intrinsic reasons for change. Missed opportunities to ask open questions about what reconnecting would mean for Sofia.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership by inviting choices and offering to help draft or rehearse messages, showing that Sofia's judgment matters. At times the clinician slips into more prescriptive directions that lessen shared problem-solving.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk and concrete commitments but often supplies solutions rather than drawing out Sofia's own reasons for change. Could more actively reflect and amplify statements like 'I miss her' to strengthen desire, reason, and ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing, validates reluctance, and offers small acceptable steps that minimize strengthening sustain talk. Could increase use of double-sided reflections to explicitly name ambivalence and gently shift weight toward change.
