# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with a warm, nonjudgmental tone and frequent validating statements that normalize Hana’s experience.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong empathic listening with accurate reflections of Hana’s emotional and somatic experience and uses tailored grounding strategies that track her state.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on preparing for and practicing the certification exam, tying nearly every intervention to concrete behavioral steps and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Works largely as a joint enterprise by soliciting preferences and wording tweaks, but leans toward directive, step-by-step instruction instead of co-creating strategies with Hana.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering options, checking timing preferences, and respecting Hana’s decisions without coercive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits Hana’s reasons and past successes and invites doable steps, but misses opportunities to deepen values-based motivation and use scaling to build commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters collaboration by asking for preferences and inviting choices, though specific scripts and instructions shift some power toward the clinician rather than jointly constructing the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and amplifies change talk and reinforces commitment for small steps, but could more systematically draw out importance/confidence and deepen emotional reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoids amplifying sustain talk by normalizing anxiety and acknowledging fears, yet rarely uses double-sided reflections or reframing to explicitly resolve ambivalence.
