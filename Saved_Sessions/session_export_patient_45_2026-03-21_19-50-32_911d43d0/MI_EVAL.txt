# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating, avoided blame and normalized the patient's reactions while offering options rather than criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear perspective-taking with accurate reflections and summaries that validated the patient's feelings and stance throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a focused agenda on medication and lifestyle goals, offering concrete first steps, scripts, and measurable follow‑up plans to move toward change.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction read as joint problem-solving with tailored recommendations, though the clinician sometimes led with ready-made scripts instead of co-constructing each element.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by presenting options, asking permission, and avoiding pressuring language, reinforcing the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some successful evocation (e.g., asking what bothers the patient) but session relied heavily on providing information and scripts rather than persistently drawing out motivations, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fostered a collaborative tone and invited input repeatedly, but at times provided prescriptive phrasing without fully checking or co-creating the patient's preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change language, but often moved quickly to advice and solutions instead of amplifying, sequencing, and deepening the patient's own change statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk and steered toward safety and alternatives, though could more actively resolve ambivalence using MI tactics like double-sided reflections.
