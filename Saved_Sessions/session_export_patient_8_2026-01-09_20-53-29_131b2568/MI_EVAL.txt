# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently uses warm, nonjudgmental, and validating language (e.g., normalizing ambivalence and avoiding blame). Acceptance is steady throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, frequent reflective listening and mirrors the client's language (e.g., 'just five minutes' → 'two hours'). Empathic attunement clearly captures feelings and consequences.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on excessive phone/social media use and ties discussion to concrete outcomes (sleep, lectures, deadlines). Progressively narrows toward actionable steps and a specific plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is highly collaborative—elicits the client's ideas, integrates past successes, and asks the client to choose and refine supports. The clinician avoids a didactic stance and co-creates the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly honors client choice and voluntariness by offering options and asking which feels most doable. Clinician acknowledges hesitancy without pressuring, supporting client ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively draws out concrete motivations and desired outcomes (less groggy mornings, pride in past success). Could deepen by probing intrinsic values and using scaling/confidence questions to consolidate commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently frames the work as joint problem‑solving and invites the client's expertise (e.g., 'let's build on what helped'). Demonstrates genuine power‑sharing and mutual refinement of the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk and links supports to goals, including a concrete weekly plan. Missed opportunities include amplifying pride, asking scaling questions, and eliciting stronger commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully acknowledges sustain talk with double‑sided reflections that avoid argumentation. Could more actively shift sustain talk toward discrepancy by selectively reflecting change‑favoring statements and exploring longer‑term costs in the client's words.
