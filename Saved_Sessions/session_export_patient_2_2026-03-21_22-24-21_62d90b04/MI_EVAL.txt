# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance through empathic normalization and affirmations that validate the patient's situation.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and reflective listening, capturing both content and affect (stressors, ambivalence, and motivating values) which facilitates elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking reduction, redirects from alcohol when needed, and moves the conversation toward a concrete, actionable plan without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-constructs the plan with the patient by soliciting ideas and preferences (alarm, tea, sister check-in) and avoids an expert-imperative stance.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently preserves the patient's sense of choice and control by offering pacing, asking preferences, and respecting limits while ensuring the plan fits her life.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's own reasons and strategies for change by asking about values, past successes, and concrete steps, then amplifies and links them to action.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as collaborative, repeatedly inviting the patient's input and co-creating the plan while checking preferences and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits, reinforces, and labels change talk, links values to specific steps, and invites small first steps that deepen and expand motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and does not strengthen sustain talk, but misses some opportunities for more active softening (e.g., double-sided reflections to gently reframe minimization).
