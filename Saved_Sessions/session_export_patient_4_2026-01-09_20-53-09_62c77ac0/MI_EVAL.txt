# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and respect, normalizing feelings and honoring the patient's boundary. The clinician avoided minimizing or labeling and affirmed the patient's experience throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated highly accurate understanding with complex reflections and summaries that captured both content and emotion. These moves clarified values and continuously checked understanding.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on symptom management and a concrete target behavior (short tea breaks) throughout the session. Questions and reflections repeatedly returned to a feasible, value-linked plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was a genuine joint exploration using inclusive language and shared problem-solving to co-design supports and the plan. The clinician built on the patient's strengths and invited her contributions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly supported the patient's choice and avoided pressure, affirming limits and framing the plan as the patient's decision. The clinician respected the patient's wishes regarding further testing and boundaries.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out intrinsic motivations and linked change to valued outcomes, eliciting concrete reasons and markers for change. To improve, the clinician could probe confidence, prioritize goals, and elicit a firmer commitment statement.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session was clearly co-constructed with repeated solicitations of the patient's ideas and preferences, positioning her as the expert on her life. These moves demonstrated strong power-sharing and joint planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk, produced a specific doable step and linked it to values. Missed opportunities included more amplification of commitment language and asking for confidence/readiness ratings to consolidate motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged and normalized sustain talk without arguing and balanced desire for tests with support for small steps. Could strengthen by using more double-sided reflections and selectively highlighting discrepancy to reduce sustain talk.
