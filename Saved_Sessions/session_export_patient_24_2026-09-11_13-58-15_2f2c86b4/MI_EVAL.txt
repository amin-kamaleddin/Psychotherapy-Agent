# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized ambivalence (e.g., validating training and reassuring 'you’re worthy'), though the repeated phrasing came across as slightly scripted.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, responsive reflections and mirrored the client's language ('don’t like rules', 'casual'), which built rapport and captured the client's experience.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on alcohol use linked to marathon training and consistently moved the conversation toward specific, measurable steps (e.g., not drinking before long runs, earlier dinners, drafting a text).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the session as a shared project by offering options and explicitly asking the client to choose or refine them, allowing the client to drive the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice and ownership by offering options, checking preferences, and avoiding coercion while reaffirming the client's agency.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Drew motivation from the client by eliciting values (training), prompting client-generated steps, and amplifying change language into a concrete, time-limited experiment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative and deferred to the client's expertise, inviting the patient to select phrasing and approaches that felt natural.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Systematically elicited and reinforced change talk with forward‑oriented questions and reflections, which produced concrete client commitments and an actionable plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected sustain talk toward pragmatic compromises, but missed opportunities for more explicit double‑sided reflections or summaries to strategically surface ambivalence.
