# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, validating honesty and limits and respecting the patient’s boundary about not discussing alcohol today.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking with reflective listening and summaries that capture feelings, workload, and parenting values, tracking shifts from guarded to cooperative.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear behavioral focus on reducing evening smoking and steadily moves the conversation toward a specific, measurable, time-limited plan and signs of success.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Works as an equal partner throughout, soliciting the patient’s ideas, privileging her choices, and co-constructing a doable implementation plan with backups.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently fosters choice and avoids pressure by framing the plan as a tweakable experiment, honoring the patient’s selections and boundaries.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation by linking change to valued outcomes (mothering, energy, sleep) and drawing on past successes to boost self-efficacy.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently positions the work as collaborative with inclusive language and frequent invitations for the patient to choose supports and shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk with evocative follow-ups and reinforcement of specific benefits and concrete steps toward reducing evening smoking.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change, though could more often use explicit double-sided reflections to name ambivalence and deepen discrepancy.
