# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard, normalized feelings, and affirmed efforts while avoiding blame; honored the patient's request to omit med-change instructions.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used accurate reflections and safety-oriented empathy, but did not consistently probe deeper emotions or offer more complex reflections of ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on glucose monitoring and guided the session toward a specific, measurable, time-bound plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functioned as a genuine team approach by inviting patient choices, adjusting the plan to preferences, and planning joint follow-up to refine the strategy.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported patient choice by offering options, asking permission before advising, and reinforcing ownership of the plan while respecting boundaries.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some commitment to try the plan but relied more on supplying solutions than drawing out the patient's internal motivations, values, or past successes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated shared decision-making and power-sharing through invitations and honoring preferences, though the clinician sometimes moved into advising rather than eliciting the patient's ideas first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Made deliberate attempts to elicit change talk and secured verbal commitments, but did not explore personal reasons, values, or confidence to deepen and amplify change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively normalized worries and gently redirected toward manageable steps and coping scripts, though could use more double-sided reflections to strategically pivot ambivalence toward change.
