# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and validation with normalizing statements and affirmation of priorities, avoiding blame or minimization throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurately took the patient's perspective and used reflective listening to capture both emotional and pragmatic concerns, then tailored options to those needs.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on the statin decision and concrete next steps (fact sheet, scripts, monitoring/contingency plan) without drifting into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner by inviting choices about materials, rehearsal, and next steps, consistently checking and incorporating the patient's preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Supported patient choice by offering options, asking what felt doable, affirming the right to delay or decline, and honoring the request for a written plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some preparatory motivation (doable steps, values) but relied heavily on providing solutions rather than probing deeper for intrinsic reasons, importance/confidence, or amplifying change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently adopted a collaborative stance, co-creating materials and involving the patient in shaping the plan and how to proceed.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced small-step change language and offered supports to translate intent into action, but could have amplified reasons for change and drawn out stronger commitment or confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and acknowledged concerns while redirecting to manageable steps, though missed opportunities for double-sided reflections to explicitly juxtapose sustain and change talk.
