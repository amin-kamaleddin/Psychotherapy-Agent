# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician repeatedly communicated unconditional positive regard with nonjudgmental language and normalizing reflections, consistently conveying warmth and respect.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** High-quality reflective listening accurately captured both content and feeling (e.g., tracking the client's 'noise' metaphor), helping the client feel understood and elaborated.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The session maintained a clear focus on reducing reliance on cutting and moved efficiently from exploration to a concrete, time-limited experiment without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician used inclusive language and solicited the client's input to co-construct a practical experiment, honoring the client's lived experience and preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Choice was emphasized throughout (e.g., asking what feels doable and framing the trial as the client's test) while also including appropriate safety instructions without undermining autonomy.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician elicited internal motivations and concrete desired outcomes but could have probed confidence, linked values more explicitly, and asked for commitment language to deepen intrinsic motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently fostered the client's expertise and shared decision-making, avoiding an authoritarian stance and inviting the client to co-design the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Change talk was effectively elicited and reinforced leading to a small experiment, but the clinician could more actively amplify and summarize emerging change statements and explore confidence/ability.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician used double-sided reflections and validated the function of cutting without confronting sustain talk, though some sustain content was repeated and could have been followed more often by strategic pivots toward change.
