# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveyed respect and non‑judgmental regard (repeated validations, praise, normalization of worries). Recommendation in eval: add occasional strength‑and‑values statements to deepen individualized warmth.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good reflective listening and invitations to explore (e.g., asking how Narcan conversation should go). Missed opportunities for deeper affect‑focused reflections about the emotional drivers of use ("escape," underlying feelings).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, appropriate focus on harm reduction and concrete next steps (Narcan, SSP, job resources, crisis steps). Balanced guidance with patient autonomy; recommended to check permission before offering many resources.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative (offered role‑play and choices). At times resources/advice were provided without explicit permission — small improvement would be more frequent brief permission checks.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Respected patient choices (acknowledged not ready for MAT), reinforced self‑efficacy and decision‑making (supported rehearsals, safety options), preserved patient control over next steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited and amplified change talk (specific planning, confidence scaling, rehearsal). Multiple prompts produced commitment/preparatory language and concrete commitments.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Worked alongside patient with summaries and collaborative planning; maintained supportive stance. Could enhance shared decision‑making by explicitly asking permission before offering multiple suggestions/resources.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively drew out and consolidated change talk (role‑play offer, 0–10 confidence question, summaries of commitments). Reinforced readiness and concrete steps throughout the session.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Validated sustain talk and avoided arguing; pivoted to harm reduction. Missed opportunities to use MI‑specific moves (double‑sided reflections, highlighting discrepancy) to further soften sustain talk.
