# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating the patient's fears and offering choices without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated good empathic understanding and some reflections but relied at times on informational delivery rather than deeper, complex reflections of emotion and ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on asthma medication adherence and safety, moving logically from problem identification to concrete planning (scripts, reminders, action steps).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in shared problem-solving by co-designing doctor questions, role-playing, and tailoring strategies to the patient's preferences, supporting teamwork rather than unilateral directives.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported the patient's sense of choice and control by offering options, acknowledging individualized timing, and avoiding pressuring language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some change-related intentions via practical scaffolding but seldom used evocative MI questions to probe the patient's deeper motives, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and inviting of preferences, though the clinician occasionally shifted into expert/educator mode without prefacing or eliciting the patient's interpretation first.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Created many opportunities for change talk through offers of help and concrete planning, but could more often reflect, amplify the patient's change statements, and elicit importance/confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk by normalizing concerns and providing balanced options, though double-sided reflections were used infrequently to more gently pivot ambivalence.
