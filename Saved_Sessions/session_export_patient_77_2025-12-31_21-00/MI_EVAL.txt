- [x] Communicated respect / nonjudgmental tone (e.g., "I hear frustration and loneliness")

### Empathy

**Comments:** Reflections were appropriate and helped the patient feel heard, but were mostly surface-level. Missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections about loneliness and parental impact.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4

**Comments:** Strong support for autonomy via framing and reassurance. Could strengthen by explicitly offering choices and eliciting which options the patient prefers.

- [ ] Used scaling or questions to draw out desire/importance/confidence

**Comments:** Some elicitation occurred, but the clinician did not sufficiently draw out intrinsic motivation or reasons for change; more open questions to evoke change talk are needed.

**Comments:** Change talk occurred but was not consistently amplified or explored; missed chances to deepen commitment and confidence about change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
- [x] Acknowledged sustain talk and fears (shame, fear of failing, parental reactions)
- [x] Offered reframing strategies (tracking as information, experiment framing)
