# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, validating competing priorities and offering pragmatic options without shaming; tone remained supportive throughout.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good perspective-taking with accurate reflections and summaries of priorities and barriers, but missed deeper emotional reflections about social connection and the soothing function of vaping.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on reducing vaping with concrete steps, barriers, supports, and contingencies, moving efficiently from values to a feasible action plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction was strongly collaborative with open questions and co-constructed planning, though fairly structured option menus slightly limited equal exploration at times.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly framed choices and asked the client to choose what felt doable, affirming control and avoiding pressure or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively drew motivation from the client using scaling, eliciting doable steps, and building on Anika’s own reasons and strategies for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Treated the client as an active agent and invited her input on the plan, though occasional structured prompts subtly steered the agenda rather than fully privileging client-originated ideas.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk with concrete planning and scaling, but could have more selectively amplified spontaneous client statements (e.g., 'I play better when I'm not high').

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and redirected to goals effectively, but did not explicitly use double-sided reflections or name the pros of vaping to more strategically soften sustain talk.
