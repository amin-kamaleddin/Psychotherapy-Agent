# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with frequent affirmations and validation; could occasionally add explicit normalization of difficulty to deepen unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate perspective-taking and timely reflections that capture values and ambivalence; could further deepen by naming specific affect (guilt, pride, relief) at times.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with logical progression from values to a concrete experiment; balances direction with client input to keep momentum.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session reads as joint problem-solving with co‑constructed plans and clinician deferring to client preferences; clinician positions themself as a supportive partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Regularly affirms the client's choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and presenting optional check‑ins; reduces pressure while supporting ownership.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws motivation from the client by linking change to core values, using evocative questions and scaling, and eliciting personally meaningful success indicators.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative, asking for client preferences and co‑creating the plan which demonstrates genuine power‑sharing and respect for client expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Skillfully elicits and strengthens desire, reasons, and commitment through evocative questions and scaling; could further amplify existing change talk more explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and uses neutral reflections to explore barriers, but would benefit from more strategic double‑sided reflections to shift resistance when it appears.
