# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and validating—normalizes doubt and counters self-criticism effectively. Could add brief empathic summaries naming the struggle more fully to deepen unconditional regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good empathic listening, reflections, and attunement (e.g., rehearsing self-compassion, suggesting breathing). Missed opportunities for deeper, complex reflections of sighs and self-doubt before shifting to problem-solving.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing brief exercise and moves logically from assessment to concrete implementation and troubleshooting.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functions as genuine collaboration with the patient’s preferences shaping the plan and language, rather than top-down instruction.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly respects autonomy by offering options, framing experiments, and inviting the patient to choose days, phrasing, and strategies rather than prescribing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some effort to draw out motivations (asking why exercise matters), but the session largely focused on logistics and problem-solving rather than eliciting personal values, reasons, or confidence. Could strengthen evocation with more evocative questions and reflections to amplify client-generated motives.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative stance and shared decision-making, co-creating the plan with clear incorporation of the patient’s input and choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits concrete change talk and converts interest into specific intentions and commitments (days/times, alarms). Could more consistently amplify and summarize initial change statements to elicit stronger desire, reasons, and confidence.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Skillfully avoids reinforcing sustain talk through normalization, reframing, and offering small alternatives (2‑minute rule, backups). Could more often use double-sided or amplified reflections to explicitly acknowledge sustain talk while moving toward change.
