# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently expressed unconditional positive regard through normalizing statements and validation of small steps, creating a warm nonjudgmental tone throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening with concise summaries that captured the client's ambivalence and emotional context.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, pragmatic focus on a specific target behavior (a one-day nicotine patch trial) and repeatedly guided the conversation toward concrete next steps and supports.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked jointly with the patient to tailor plans and materials, soliciting preferences and co-constructing decisions (day, scripts, backups) rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by asking permission before offering options and framing interventions as optional, respecting the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used evocative questions and scaling to draw out the patient's own motivations and resources, which produced concrete reasons and a time-bound plan she endorsed.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treated the patient as the expert in her life, inviting input on texts, timing, and contingency plans and sharing power in creating the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicited and amplified change talk through open questions, readiness scales, and follow-ups, which yielded specific supports and a clear trial plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected toward change, but missed opportunities for stronger strategic softening such as explicit double-sided reflections to reduce sustain-talk pull.
