# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, honoring Amira’s preferences (e.g., ‘Don’t change diet yet’) and offering practical, validating options. No signs of labeling, criticism, or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid, pragmatic empathy—checked mood/readiness and normalized low-energy days. Could deepen emotional reflection with more complex reflections of hope and discouragement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused target on increasing physical activity and created a concrete one-week micro-plan and tracking system. Resisted drifting to unrelated topics and kept the session goal-directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-created the plan, repeatedly asked for preferences (timing, format, wording) and iteratively adjusted materials. Decisions were constructed with Amira rather than imposed.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently reinforced Amira’s control over choices (plan selection, PDF options, honoring no diet change) and framed low-energy options as legitimate. Language supported self-direction rather than mandates.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicited some motivation via readiness scaling and plan preference but relied largely on practical planning. Missed opportunities to draw out deeper values, reasons, and importance/confidence talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clear partnership in soliciting many preferences and co-designing the checklist, but the clinician occasionally shifted to a more prescriptive expert role with a detailed program. More elicitation of Amira’s own structure ideas would strengthen partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Supported conditions for change talk (readiness question, micro-plan) and affirmed benefits, but did not systematically elicit or amplify Amira’s own reasons and values. The clinician moved quickly to solutions instead of deepening change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively avoided reinforcing sustain talk and honored sustain-language about diet while normalizing low-energy days. Reframed short sessions as valid progress and included explicit low-energy options on the printable.
