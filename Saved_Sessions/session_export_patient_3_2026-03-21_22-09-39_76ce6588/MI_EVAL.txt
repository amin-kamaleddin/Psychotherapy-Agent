# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard through validation, praise, and normalizing language. No blaming, lecturing, or minimizing was observed.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strong perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening that captured both emotion and practical context. Validated ambivalence and matched Aisha’s affect throughout the session.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on restarting exercise and moving from motivation to a specific, doable plan. Every intervention tied directly to the target behavior and session goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created materials and rehearsed language with Aisha, centering decisions on her preferences. The clinician positioned themselves as a partner helping implement the plan rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Offered options and framed suggestions so Aisha retained ownership of the choices. Avoided pressuring language and elicited her commitment level before advancing plans.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out reasons and self-generated strategies (scaling, concrete plans), but could deepen intrinsic motivation by exploring values and amplifying spontaneous change statements. More targeted elicitation of personal meaning would strengthen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly invited Aisha’s preferences, asked permission, and shared power in planning and scripting. The interaction felt collaborative and client-driven.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk with scaling questions and concrete planning, producing specific commitments. Could more often amplify and summarize her change statements and probe the personal significance behind them.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected focus to practical solutions and contingencies. A brief area for improvement is using double‑sided reflections to acknowledge skepticism before immediately offering solutions.
