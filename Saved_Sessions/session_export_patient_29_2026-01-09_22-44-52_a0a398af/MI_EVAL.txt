# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with normalizing, non‑shaming language and explicit acceptance statements that reflect ambivalence without judgment.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates high-level reflective listening and perspective-taking, accurately capturing the patient's motivations and barriers through repeated paraphrases.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking cessation and medication/NRT options, tying questions and reflections to concrete first steps and an actionable plan while staying patient-centered.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Functions as an equal partner—offering options, soliciting the patient's priorities and preferences, and co-constructing the plan (notes, follow-up, and choices).

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by offering multiple options, asking which steps feel doable, and emphasizing the patient's control over decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits internal motivation and past strengths (values, benefits) but could more systematically amplify and consolidate motivational statements and elicit confidence/importance ratings.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Displays strong partnership by deferring to the patient's preferences, asking which options feel realistic, and jointly prioritizing items for the PCP note and plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits clear change talk and practical steps (gum, patch, routines) but missed opportunities to amplify change language and secure explicit commitment or scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and redirects toward solutions, but uses few double-sided reflections or explicit reframes to more actively diffuse ambivalence.
