### Acceptance

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate understanding through reflective listening and complex summaries that were well attuned to the patient's guarded affect and concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on cholesterol management and the time-limited lifestyle trial, steering toward concrete behaviours and measurable follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5

### Partnership

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited specific change language and small steps (walks, reminders, labs) but the patient's change talk remained tentative and could be amplified to strengthen commitment.
