# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Opens with a supportive, nonjudgmental stance and clear safety messaging; tone is respectful but pragmatic with few individualized validating or normalizing remarks.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Shows some understanding and offers helpful summaries, but tends to shift quickly to factual information and directives rather than using reflective listening to deepen empathic connection.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on safe medication switching, washout timelines, warning signs, and practical dosing/taper details with no drift from the target.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Frames the encounter as helpful and recommends follow-up with the prescriber, but the interaction is mostly clinician-led and does not consistently co‑create a plan with the patient.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Affirms patient choice and repeatedly advises confirming plans with prescribers/pharmacists, though some firm medical phrasing reduces the sense of open choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Minimal elicitation of the patient's values, reasons, or confidence; few open questions were used to draw out or build on the patient's change talk.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes some partnership elements (offering brief summaries, deferring to prescriber), but largely directs care without eliciting the patient's expertise about how the washout would affect them.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforces the patient's stated intentions and prompts next steps, but makes limited use of open questions to elicit deeper reasons, importance, or confidence for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by acknowledging risk, offering bridging options, and reassuring about tapering effects, though it could better validate concerns and use double‑sided reflections.
