# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently showed unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental, validating tone throughout the session, with affirmations and reflections that avoided shaming or minimization.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and acknowledged ambivalence, but missed chances to probe deeper into emotional or identity‑based meanings of drinking for fuller empathic depth.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, steady focus on reducing weekend/social drinking and efficiently guided the conversation toward concrete harm‑reduction strategies and a measurable plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint effort, invited patient preferences, co‑built the plan, and explicitly offered help rather than imposing solutions, with the patient’s ideas shaping the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirmed the patient's choice and control using noncoercive language, offering options and respecting the decision not to quit while supporting smaller steps.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited practical motivation and intentions (confidence scale, plan), but could have probed more for intrinsic motivators, values, and personalized discrepancy to deepen evocation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Positioned the patient as an active decision maker and co‑creator of the plan, offering options and asking the patient to choose tactics and follow‑up preferences.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and strengthened change talk through planning and confidence scaling, though could have more frequently amplified and summarized the patient's own reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and did not reinforce sustain talk, redirecting to reduction strategies, but used few explicit softening techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections) to artfully reframe ambivalence.
