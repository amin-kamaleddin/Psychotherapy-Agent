# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with explicit validation and affirmation of the patient's feelings and values. The clinician normalizes ambivalence and supports the patient's identity as a good father who made mistakes.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate perspective-taking and timely reflective listening, capturing both content and emotion. Reflections deepen rapport and convey genuine understanding and belief in the patient.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on target behaviors (reducing alcohol, attending treatment, safer living) and links conversation to concrete planning. Elicits a specific short-term commitment and moves from values to actionable steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Treats the patient as an equal partner and repeatedly solicits his ideas to shape the plan. Summaries use the patient's language and proposed next steps are co-created with family involvement.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently endorses the patient's right to choose and offers options rather than directives. Elicits what protections and supports would feel self-directed to preserve independence.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively draws out intrinsic motivators (children, past successes) and elicits self-generated strategies. Converts evoked reasons into specific, achievable steps and plans.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Genuinely shares power by asking for priorities, non-negotiables, and collaborating on a written plan with whānau. Defers to the patient's expertise about his life and uses his language to build the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk across reasons, ability, and commitment, and consolidates it into concrete commitments. Reinforces and builds on statements of intent to create a practical plan.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoids reinforcing sustain talk and redirects toward change, but underuses targeted MI techniques to soften sustain-oriented concerns. Could more often use double-sided reflections and explore the emotional pull of independence to reduce sustain talk's influence.
