# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental, with affirmations and neutral language that normalized ambivalence. The clinician conveyed steady unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong reflective listening, paraphrases, and perspective-taking that showed understanding of Jamal's situation. Could probe or reflect more deeply on emotions and underlying ambivalence (e.g., explore what 'for me' really means).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, sustained focus on getting to school on time with concrete barriers, strategies, scaling, and a one-week test. The session stayed goal-oriented and appropriate for the preparation stage.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co-created the plan by soliciting Jamal's ideas, incorporating them, and negotiating next steps rather than imposing solutions. Help and guidance were offered after Jamal requested it, supporting shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported Jamal's sense of choice by asking permission, offering multiple options, and asking which felt doable. Reinforced ownership of the plan without pressure or coercion.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively used past successes, scaling questions, and problem-solving to elicit change talk and concrete commitments. Would be stronger by more directly eliciting intrinsic motives and linking punctuality to Jamal's personal values or goals.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated a highly collaborative stance with repeated permission-seeking and invitation to choose options. The clinician avoided an expert-only posture and allowed Jamal to shape the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and strengthened desire, ability, and commitment through questions about past successes, scaling, and concrete planning. Could amplify and deepen Jamal's intrinsic reasons by asking for more elaborated 'for me' statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk but often reflected Jamal's extrinsic motives (e.g., 'stop the nagging') rather than using double-sided reflections or strategic reframing. Missed opportunities to normalize ambivalence and pivot toward Jamal's brief intrinsic statements.
