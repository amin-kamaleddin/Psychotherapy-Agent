# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The clinician uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes lapses, which reduces shame; some affirmations feel formulaic and lack individualized naming of Laura’s unique strengths.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic attunement with reflective statements and normalization, but reflections are often brief and followed by action, missing chances for deeper emotional exploration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session maintained a clear, consistent focus on the target behavior with concrete, goal-oriented strategies and a specific follow-up plan.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Interaction reads as collaborative problem-solving with adaptation to client preferences, though the clinician frequently supplied ready-made scripts rather than eliciting Laura’s own formulations.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician offered choices, asked permission, and validated testing the plan, but could more explicitly emphasize Laura’s ownership and invite her to generate more details herself.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Initial elicitation of values and intentions was effective, but the clinician shifted into advising and structuring instead of continuing to draw out and deepen Laura’s own motivations and strengths.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are collaborative moments and acceptance of client preferences, yet the interaction remains fairly clinician-led with many prescriptive recommendations that reduce client co-creation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The session elicited statements of values and intentions, but the clinician often moved quickly to solutions instead of amplifying, reflecting, or asking follow-ups to strengthen change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Sustain talk was consistently acknowledged and reframed (lapses as data) with practical backups and cues, though more use of double-sided or rolling-with-resistance reflections could enhance this work.
