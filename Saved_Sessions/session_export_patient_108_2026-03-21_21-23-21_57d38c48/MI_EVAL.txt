# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, repeatedly validating Oliver’s feelings and intent. Warm, nonjudgmental stance is maintained throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows strong empathic listening with accurate reflections and mirroring of language, helping Oliver feel heard. Could deepen emotional nuance with more complex reflections (e.g., exploring guilt/shame and the meaning of 'weak').

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, focused trajectory from problem definition to concrete actions (scripts, date, role‑play, follow‑up text). The session is purposefully goal-directed and practical.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative with co-creation of scripts, role selection, and ongoing adjustments based on Oliver’s preferences. Positions the client as an active decision-maker throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports choice by offering options (draft/role‑play, script choices, dates) and avoiding pressure. Leaves final decisions to Oliver, reinforcing his ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits motivation (importance/confidence scaling, commitments, concrete steps) and records change statements. Could further deepen evocation by asking more open, values-focused questions and eliciting stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong partnership by inviting preferences, co‑creating language, and tweaking suggestions collaboratively. The clinician and client work as equal partners in planning and rehearsal.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and supports change talk with scaling, specific commitments, and action planning, leading to clear change statements and a set date. Would benefit from more frequent amplification and reflective magnification of the client's own change language before moving to scripts.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk (guilt, attachment) but tends to pivot quickly to action rather than using strategic techniques to diminish it. Could use double-sided reflections and selectively follow sustain reflections with change-oriented language to reduce resistance.
