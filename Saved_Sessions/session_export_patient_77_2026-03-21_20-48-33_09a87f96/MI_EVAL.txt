# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with normalizing, nonjudgmental language and explicit affirmations of strengths throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate, timely reflections that mirror both affect and content, helping the client feel understood and encouraging elaboration.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, focused progression from problem identification to specific, doable steps (plan, timeline, follow-up) that stay on the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates genuine teamwork by soliciting the client's expertise, co-drafting texts, offering options with permission, and sharing decision-making power.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently honors choice by offering multiple options, asking permission before suggesting, and validating the client's preferences without pressure.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Draws motivation from the client by eliciting desired outcomes, past successes, importance/confidence ratings, and using the client's responses to shape the plan.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative relationship by asking permission, inviting the client's selection of options, and involving her in follow-up planning.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and amplifies change talk through open questions, scaling, reinforcement of motivation, and building on client-selected actions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and reframes sustain talk into solvable barriers, though could more actively use double-sided reflections and explore underlying emotions to further reduce sustain language.
