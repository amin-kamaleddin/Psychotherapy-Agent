# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes the patient's position with affirmations and validating language throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate, empathic reflections that make the patient feel heard, though could use more complex reflections to probe deeper emotions like guilt or fear about parenting.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on smoking/exposure reduction and moves from exploration to a concrete, implementable plan with follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively co-creates the plan with the patient, solicits preferences, and offers supports contingent on patient choices, showing genuine joint problem-solving.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, frames recommendations as suggestions, and explicitly preserves the patient's right to choose her path forward.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits the patient's own motivations and solutions and draws on values about protecting her child, but could more systematically probe reasons and use scaling or 'what would make you say yes' prompts to deepen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters a collaborative partnership by asking choice questions, checking preferences, and ensuring the patient's ideas drive the plan and supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk and asks which strategies feel doable, though missed opportunities to further probe desire/reason/need and to elicit explicit commitment language or confidence/importance scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and redirects minimizations to harm-reduction options, but could more explicitly use double-sided reflections to surface ambivalence and soften sustain talk further.
