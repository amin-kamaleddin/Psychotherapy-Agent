# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through normalizing statements, affirmations, and explicit validation of constraints and priorities, creating a warm, nonjudgmental stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking and reflective summaries (e.g., noting the move and linking goals to function), but missed some opportunities to name deeper emotions and explore functional examples more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity and moved logically from goal identification to specific planning, scheduling, and implementation steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session felt like a team effort with frequent invitations for client input and co-construction of the plan, though the clinician occasionally offered ready-made options before eliciting more of Amira’s own ideas.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported choice and control by asking preferences, offering fallback options, and avoiding pressuring language, which reinforced Amira’s ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out change talk and reasons for change and reinforced commitment, but could use more open questions to deepen intrinsic motivation and confidence statements.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Encouraged shared decision-making and checked preferences frequently, yet sometimes tilted toward an expert role by providing detailed plans instead of prompting client-generated options.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk and shaped implementation intentions, but would benefit from more selective reflections and open questions to amplify desire, ability, and reasons for change.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and offered compassionate fallback plans, though could more often use double-sided reflections to explicitly acknowledge ambivalence and contrast change talk.
