# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through normalization, reassurance, and repeated affirmations of strengths, reducing shame and promoting safety.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong perspective-taking with accurate, timely reflections that mirror the client's language and emotional tone, showing clear attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear, consistent focus on increasing physical activity and moves logically from values to a specific, actionable plan with markers of progress.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly co-constructed—offers options, asks the client to choose and refines the plan based on her responses, positioning the clinician as a supportive partner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice by inviting preferences and framing steps as optional and adaptable, avoiding pressuring language and affirming the client's control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motivation by drawing out values, practical benefits, and envisioned outcomes, prompting the client to articulate reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally collaborative and invites input, but occasionally offers concrete suggestions without explicit permission, which slightly reduces shared decision-making.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and reinforces change talk through values questions, small-step invitations, amplification of intentions, and asking about signs of progress.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and neutralizes sustain talk without arguing, but uses fewer double-sided reflections or strategic summaries that explicitly contrast sustain and change sides.
