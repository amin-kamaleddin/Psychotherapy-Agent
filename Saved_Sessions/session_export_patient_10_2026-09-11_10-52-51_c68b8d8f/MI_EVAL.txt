# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support through affirmations and strengths-based language, validating the client's experience and choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated clear perspective-taking and accurate reflective listening that acknowledged frustration, though deeper, more textured reflections of emotion and mixed pride/frustration were sometimes missed.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a focused, purposeful direction toward the target behavior with concrete, time-limited steps (pick two jobs, request phone screens, rehearse an intro) and a clear next action.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Worked as an equal partner throughout—offering options, soliciting preferences, co-writing drafts, and deferring to the client's judgment to co-construct decisions and next steps.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported client choice by offering multiple options, asking which felt most doable, and only offering drafting/rehearsal when invited, keeping control with the client.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out the client's own ideas and used her language to shape concrete strategies, but missed opportunities to probe deeper into intrinsic motives and the basis of her 10/10 confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the interaction as collaborative and shared power, co-creating the plan and repeatedly asking how the clinician should partner to support the client's choices.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced commitment language and translated it into actionable steps (drafting messages, rehearsing), though could have amplified and explored the client's strong confidence more explicitly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledged sustain talk about childcare without judgment and redirected to workable alternatives, but could have used more double-sided reflections and explicit validation of ambivalence.
