# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician shows clear unconditional positive regard, repeatedly normalizing David's stance and avoiding judgment. The clinician consistently validates David's preferences and limits, including his 'not quitting' statements.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Accurate, consistent understanding of David's perspective through reflections and summaries that capture stress, practicality, and guarded openness. The clinician also accurately reflects discordant statements, demonstrating strong perspective-taking.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on reducing cigarette use at work and advances toward a concrete, time-limited nicotine replacement trial. Every turn moves the plan forward to a specific behavioral goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treats the interaction as joint problem-solving, soliciting David's input at every decision point. The clinician incorporates David's choices into the plan, showing his ideas shape the session outcomes.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly affirms David's control and frames the trial as optional and time-limited, preserving his sense of choice. The clinician honors David's stance of 'I'm not quitting' while still exploring an experiment.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits internal motivation by asking about signs of success and past short trials, generating client-driven reasons to test change. Could strengthen evocation by drawing out intrinsic motives, values, and amplifying spontaneous change talk toward commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative: the clinician frames the plan as David's choice, repeatedly invites preference, and shares decision-making. David's expertise about what will work meaningfully shapes the plan.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk by asking about logistics and signs of success and labeling the plan as a controllable experiment. Could deepen change talk by probing personal reasons, importance/confidence, and selectively amplifying benefits toward commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids confrontation and reframes the encounter as an experiment, reducing pressure and forward movement. At times the clinician repeats 'not quitting' phrasing rather than using double-sided reflections to highlight discrepancy and further soften sustain talk.
