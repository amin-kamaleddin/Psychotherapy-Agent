# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, honored the patient’s priorities and constraints, and normalized slips without shaming.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Provided accurate, validating reflections and perspective-taking about triggers and goals, but could probe emotional nuance (e.g., worries about motherhood, stress/tiredness) more deeply.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on evening smoking reduction with specific action steps, a monitoring plan, and a concrete follow‑up time.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative—tactics and scheduling were co‑constructed and the plan incorporated the patient’s real‑world constraints throughout.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported choice by asking permission, offering options, respecting the patient’s decision not to discuss drinking, and emphasizing the patient’s control over the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited the patient’s own motivations and resources (health for child, triggers, past attempts), but could more systematically probe importance, confidence, and deepen commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fostered a partnership stance by asking permission, negotiating goals and follow‑up, and treating the patient as the expert on what will work for her.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced specific change talk and doable tactics and set accountability, though could have amplified motivations and drawn out stronger commitment and confidence statements.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and respected the patient’s focus, but sustain‑talk was largely left unaddressed rather than gently reframed or balanced with double‑sided reflections.
