# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard, normalizes difficulty, and avoids blame while respecting Lukas's boundaries. Occasional explicit normalization of adolescent experimentation could further reduce shame, but overall acceptance is strong.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Provides accurate, timely reflections that capture both content and feeling, opening space for disclosure and building rapport. Could slightly strengthen by explicitly naming underlying emotions (e.g., worry or guilt).

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on early alcohol use and concrete avoidance strategies, progressing from assessment to skills rehearsal and planning without drifting. The clinician consistently guides the session toward practical next steps.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engages in genuine joint problem-solving, solicits feedback, and adapts interventions to Lukas's comfort, co-constructing a realistic plan. The clinician treats Lukas as an equal partner whose context informs decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently offers options, checks preferences, and avoids pressuring, reinforcing Lukas's control over choices. Suggestions are presented as choices that support ownership and likely follow-through.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits Lukas's motives and practical strategies, linking them to his goals, but stops short of deeper value exploration. Could strengthen by using scaling and eliciting a time-linked commitment to consolidate motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates an explicitly collaborative stance by asking which activities to do, inviting tweaks, and honoring limits about naming teammates. Power-sharing is evident throughout the interaction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces change talk (reasons and plans) and affirms self-management, but does not fully develop commitment language. Missed opportunities include scaling importance/confidence and asking for a specific, timed commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk, neutrally reframes ambivalence, and redirects toward strategies rather than debating drinking. Could further weaken sustain talk by using double-sided reflections and strategic summaries of both sides.
