# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** ☑ Clinician consistently nonjudgmental and accepting; normalizes skepticism ("That skepticism makes total sense"), repeated reflections demonstrate unconditional positive regard.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** ☑ Strong reflective listening and perspective-taking (accurate complex reflections about protecting IB/band and not wanting people "breathing down my neck"). Reflections became somewhat formulaic and clinician seldom probed deeper affect or meaning behind the client's sustained skepticism.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** ☑ Clear, appropriate focus on a concrete behavioral target (weekly neutral reminder). Questions moved toward a specific action and plan. Missed opportunity to explicitly link plan to the originally flagged alcohol risk.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** ☑ Collaborative stance: solicits client preferences, co-constructs plan (sender, wording, time), offers options (draft vs. role‑play). Could invite more client-led agenda-setting and ask client to summarize to enhance shared ownership.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** ☑ Strong emphasis on choice and control: multiple options, clear opt-out permission, frames the trial as voluntary and low-pressure, honors boundaries (no follow-ups, neutral wording).

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** ☑ Effectively elicits client's own ideas and reasons (choice of weekly text, sender, wording, indicators of success). Could deepen by exploring values/meaning that connect protecting IB/band to broader motivations.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** ☑ Treats client as expert on what will work (asks who to trust to send text, preferred phrasing). Partnership could be strengthened by delegating specific tasks to the client and prompting a client-led summary/decision.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** ☑ Elicits change- and commitment-oriented statements ("I'll try it," choice of least annoying option). Could more actively amplify and reflect change talk to strengthen commitment and explore confidence/barriers.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** ☑ Acknowledges and normalizes sustain/ambivalence (validates skepticism). Limited use of active softening strategies (few double-sided reflections, reframes, or explicit discrepancy work) to reduce sustain talk and reorient toward change.
