# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence with warm, nonjudgmental language, reducing shame and supporting openness.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates strong, accurate reflective listening and validation, but reflections are somewhat repetitive and could deepen emotional nuance with more complex/affect‑focused reflections.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol and a concrete, achievable target (one alcohol‑free night), repeatedly guiding toward concrete planning without coercion.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Operates as an equal partner throughout—asks permission, offers choices, and co‑creates plans based on the patient's preferences in a reciprocal manner.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently affirms the patient's freedom to choose, resists labeling or pressure, and offers options that reinforce ownership of decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits the patient's intrinsic motivations and reasons for the experiment and draws out implementation intentions that link change to personally meaningful outcomes.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clearly frames the interaction as a joint exploration, inviting input and power‑sharing while co‑creating practical, patient‑driven plans.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively and skillfully elicits and amplifies change talk through targeted questions and reflections that build desire, reasons, and commitment toward a small experiment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Acknowledges and contains sustain talk using double‑sided reflections, but at times allows sustain talk to stand without more strategic moves to diminish its influence or decisively amplify change talk.
