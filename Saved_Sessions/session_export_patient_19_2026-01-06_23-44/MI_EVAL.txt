### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed respect and normalized Anya's struggles with warm, supportive language and affirmations. Could further strengthen by more explicitly separating the person from the behavior in a few moments.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong perspective-taking with accurate reflections of triggers, goals, and reactions. Occasional deeper affect-level reflections (shame/hope) could have further deepened emotional resonance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Kept a clear focus on reducing gambling and improving finances, sequencing assessment to concrete, time-limited actions. Consistently moved the session toward doable steps and implementation planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Engaged in mutual problem-solving with frequent invitations for Anya's input and co-construction of supports like scripts and role-plays. Did not impose a single solution and shared power effectively.

### Autonomy Support

### Evocation

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently framed the work as a partnership, deferring to Anya's expertise and offering supports only with her agreement. Demonstrated strong power-sharing through collaborative planning and offers to stay on the line.

### Cultivating Change Talk

**Comments:** Effectively elicited and reinforced specific change talk and implementation intentions (freezing card, auto-transfer). Missed opportunities to amplify values-based reasons and to deepen desire/commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
