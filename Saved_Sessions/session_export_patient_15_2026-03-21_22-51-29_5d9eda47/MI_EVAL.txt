# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard with validating language and appreciation, avoiding judgment and normalizing feasible steps.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses frequent, accurate reflections and summaries that make the patient feel heard; could deepen emotional understanding with more complex reflections probing underlying feelings or minimizations.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on blood pressure–related behaviors and moves the session toward a concrete, measurable action plan with contingencies.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Frames the interaction as joint problem-solving, invites input at every turn, and co-constructs the plan using the patient's own ideas and solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly reinforces patient choice and control by repeatedly asking preferences, offering options, and avoiding prescriptive language.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicits intrinsic motivations and links values to concrete actions, also prompting the patient to identify meaningful indicators of success.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conveys shared responsibility and positions the patient as the primary decision-maker through open invitations and use of patient-generated strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively elicits and amplifies change talk with value-evoking and commitment-oriented questions, leading to concrete intentions and plans.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and skillfully redirects to planning, but could better soften sustain talk using double-sided reflections or linking minimizations to the patient's goals.
