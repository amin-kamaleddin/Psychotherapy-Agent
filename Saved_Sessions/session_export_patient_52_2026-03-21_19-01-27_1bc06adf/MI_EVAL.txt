# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician used consistently nonjudgmental, validating language and normalized Irina’s experience throughout, conveying unconditional positive regard and respect for her choices.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Multiple accurate reflections and perspective-taking named exhaustion, guilt, and need for 'me time,' demonstrating strong, consistent empathic listening responsive to Irina's context.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session stayed clearly focused on alcohol as a stress-coping behavior with a practical agenda (safety, harm-reduction options, concrete nights, skills, follow-up) and no drift to unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was clearly collaborative: the clinician checked in, offered choices, and tailored strategies around Irina’s schedule and preferences, incorporating her ideas into the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician consistently framed options, asked permission before offering advice, and invited Irina to choose nights and wording, honoring her freedom to decide.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Clinician elicited intrinsic motivations and commitment language (parenting concerns, willingness to experiment) but could have more systematically probed values, importance, and confidence to deepen motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** The clinician positioned Irina as the primary decision-maker, repeatedly inviting her input and co-creating the plan rather than taking an expert-only stance.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited concrete change talk and commitments (specific nights, reminders) and prompted planning, but missed opportunities to amplify and deepen change statements and link them to personal values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk skillfully by avoiding argument, normalizing alcohol's function, and offering pragmatic substitutions and harm-reduction strategies that steered the conversation toward manageable change.
