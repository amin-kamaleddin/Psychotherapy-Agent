# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and supportive, normalizing the patient's anxiety and validating concerns throughout the session without blaming or labeling.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Displays good empathic listening and brief reflections but often moves quickly to information and planning instead of deepening emotional reflection or ambivalence.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps the conversation tightly focused on cholesterol management, medication adherence, and safety with clear, sequential action steps and follow-up plans.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively invites the patient's preferences, implements requested modifications, and maintains a team-oriented tone that co-produces the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly upholds patient choice by offering options, asking permission before interventions, and framing decisions as the patient's to make.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some motivational material and choices but does not consistently draw out intrinsic reasons, confidence, or commitment language, favoring information and problem-solving.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Maintains a collaborative stance and checks preferences, but much of the session is clinician-driven with recommendations rather than eliciting the patient's own solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces some change talk and links actions to values, but misses opportunities to follow up, amplify, and deepen desire/reason/need and commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Sometimes redirects sustain talk with normalization and planning but rarely uses double-sided reflections, evocations, or summaries to gently resolve ambivalence.
