# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses a respectful, nonjudgmental tone and normalizes Sofia's feelings, reducing shame (e.g., safety check, reassuring statements). Some responses move quickly into problem-solving and corrective reassurance, slightly reducing wholly unconditional acceptance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Accurately names and reflects Sofia's emotions and ambivalence, which facilitates continued sharing. Missed opportunities for deeper affect-focused reflections and the clinician often shifts quickly to advice.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on stopping alcohol in pregnancy and repeatedly brings the conversation back to concrete steps. Direction is strong and appropriate to the session goal.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Includes some collaborative elements (choices, tailored scripts) but is largely clinician-driven with many ready-made scripts and directives. Would benefit from more co-creation, eliciting Sofia's language, and negotiating acceptable strategies.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Offers options and asks about making a plan, supporting choice to an extent. However, prescriptive language and firm recommendations undercut autonomy; more permission-seeking and explicit emphasis on Sofia's control are needed.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits information and some planning that produces change talk, but relies more on giving solutions than drawing out Sofia's own motivations, values, and confidence. Lacks scaling questions, reflective summaries, and prompts to explore 'what would be different' if sober.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Invites input at times and offers options, but much of the session centers clinician expertise via detailed to-do lists and scripts. Greater partnership would include eliciting Sofia's phrasing, earlier role-play, and joint development of strategies.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Affirms motivation and provides concrete tools that elicit some change talk, yet rarely uses open questions, reflections, or summaries to deepen and amplify Sofia's reasons and commitment. More strategic evocation and reinforcement of change language would strengthen commitment.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk and offers practical strategies to manage social pressure, but sometimes counters sustain talk with reassurance or corrective statements instead of rolling with it. Missed MI-consistent moves include double-sided reflections and eliciting Sofia's own plans to manage peer pull.
