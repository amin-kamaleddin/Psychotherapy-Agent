# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently showed unconditional positive regard, warmth, and normalization (multiple affirmations and nonjudgmental language). This approach supported the patient and avoided pressure or criticism.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Good empathic listening with accurate reflections of practical barriers and context. Could deepen empathy by reflecting more on emotions and the personal meaning behind the patient's concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on smoking and specific small behavioral changes throughout the session. The clinician kept dialogue targeted on actionable steps and avoided drift.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction functioned as joint problem-solving with frequent solicitation of the patient's input and integration of his choices into the plan. The clinician positioned themselves as a supportive partner rather than an authority.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician repeatedly offered options, asked permission before offering ideas, and respected the patient's estimates and choices. Statements emphasized the patient's control and decision-making throughout.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used open questions, scaling, and plan-elicitation to draw out some motivation and concrete steps. Evocation leaned toward pragmatic planning rather than exploring deeper values or intrinsic reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently collaborative and delegative, asking for preferences and co-creating plans with the patient. Power was effectively shared, reinforcing the patient's role as the decision-maker.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk using specific invitations, confidence rulers, and if-then plans, producing concrete commitments. Missed opportunities to elicit and amplify intrinsic reasons or stronger desire/ability language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Did not reinforce sustain talk and showed respect, but used problem-solving more than strategic softening techniques. Few double-sided reflections or explicit explorations of ambivalence allowed sustain talk to remain unaddressed.
