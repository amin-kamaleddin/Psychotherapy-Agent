# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and a nonjudgmental stance, normalizing and validating Sofia's experience while inviting her preferred approach.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate empathic reflections and perspective-taking to acknowledge emotions and context, but could use deeper or more complex emotional reflections to capture sadness, disappointment, or fear of failure.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on increasing brief movement with concrete implementation steps (10 min/day, anchors, backups) and sets a measurable one-week test to advance planning.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Conducts joint problem-solving by soliciting input at key decisions, integrating the patient's wording, and co-creating the plan rather than imposing a unilateral solution.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supports choice by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and framing skips as data, which reinforces Sofia's control over pace and content.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits logistical ideas, preferences, and plans from the patient, but could deepen evocation by exploring underlying values, personal motivations, and confidence about change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Largely collaborative—invites preferences and celebrates the patient's contributions—but occasionally shifts toward expert problem-solving instead of explicitly privileging the patient's expertise.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces patient-generated change language and invites commitment to a one-week test, yet could more actively amplify desire/importance/confidence and summarize change talk to strengthen motivation.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk by reframing doubts, offering contingency plans, and promoting self-compassion, though more double-sided reflections could better acknowledge ambivalence while guiding toward change.
