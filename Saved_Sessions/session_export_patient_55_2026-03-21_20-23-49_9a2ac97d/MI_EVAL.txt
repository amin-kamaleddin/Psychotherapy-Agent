# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Clinician consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizing statements with a warm, nonjudgmental tone; multiple validations reinforced acceptance throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates accurate understanding through reflections and summaries that match the client's language and emotion, effectively moving the conversation forward.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Keeps a clear, consistent focus on reducing late‑night workload and guides the session toward concrete change steps (25‑minute blocks, specific times, and check‑ins).

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction is clearly collaborative with inclusive language and co‑creation of the plan; client input shaped decisions about tasks, timing, and follow‑ups.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports the client's sense of choice and control by offering options, asking preferences, and avoiding pressuring language while framing the plan as the client's decision.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Frequently elicits motivations, confidence, and intentions using scaling and 'what would be different' questions, but often supplies strategies rather than drawing more client‑generated values and ideas.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally treats the client as a collaborator and elicits preferences, but occasionally adopts a somewhat directive/problem‑solving stance by offering menus of options before inviting the client's own solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits desire, ability, and commitment (scaling, specific plan) and increases expressed confidence, but could further amplify client‑generated reasons and invite more elaboration on change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Acknowledges sustain talk without punishing it, but tends to move quickly into offering solutions rather than using double‑sided reflections or exploring ambivalence to soften sustain talk.
