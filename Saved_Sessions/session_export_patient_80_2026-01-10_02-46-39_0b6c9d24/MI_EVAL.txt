# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard with warm, validating language and strengths-based affirmations throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrates good reflective listening and accurate summaries of triggers and supports, though could deepen affect-focused reflections (shame/weakness) before moving to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on reducing alcohol use and managing aggression, guiding the session toward concrete, actionable relapse-prevention steps and rehearsals.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Highly collaborative—co-creates the lock-screen note and partner script, role-plays on request, and consistently invites the patient’s preferences to shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supports choice and control by offering options, asking permission, and respecting the patient's readiness without pressuring or directing.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits motivation and builds on expressed successes using the patient’s language, but could more explicitly explore intrinsic values and use scaling to strengthen commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Fosters genuine power-sharing through joint decision-making, invitations rather than prescriptions, and positioning the patient as the expert on what will work.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces change talk, praises progress and rehearses plans, but missed opportunities to amplify commitment language and use scaling questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and gently pivots to change with relapse-prevention options, though could more often use double-sided reflections to resolve ambivalence.
