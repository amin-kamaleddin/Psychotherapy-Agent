# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard and normalizes setbacks, validating effort and avoiding judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening and accurate reflections that capture facts and feelings, though some reflections are repetitive and could deepen emotional meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear focus on smoking reduction and moves to a concrete, time‑bound test (Friday shift) without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session is co‑constructed: clinician offers options, solicits preferences, and lets the patient's choices shape the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly emphasizes choice and the patient's control, avoids pressure, and invites the patient to design and commit to his own plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits self‑generated solutions and commitment language but could further probe personal values and use scaling to strengthen internal motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopts a collaborative stance throughout, asking the patient to choose doable strategies and sharing decision‑making power.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicits and reinforces concrete change talk and produced a specific commitment (bringing a travel mug), but could amplify reasons for change and assess confidence/commitment more directly.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids reinforcing sustain talk and reframes slips as learning, though more double‑sided and strategically tipping reflections would further blunt sustain talk.
