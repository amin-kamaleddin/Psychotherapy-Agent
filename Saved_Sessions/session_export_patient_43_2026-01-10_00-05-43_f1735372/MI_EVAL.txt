# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicates unconditional positive regard and normalizes ambivalence, avoiding labeling or shaming throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Shows good empathic listening with accurate brief reflections and validations, but reflections are often short and could probe deeper into underlying feelings and meanings.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains a clear, consistent focus on smoking, gathers key behavior details, and anchors the session to specific, measurable, actionable steps and follow-up.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Creates a conversational, co-created plan and invites input, but frequently offers numerous specific tactics which can make parts of the interaction more clinician-driven than fully mutual.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supports patient choice by asking permission, offering options, and validating limited readiness, reinforcing the patient's control over change.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits a readiness rating and small commitments but relies heavily on providing solutions rather than drawing out the patient's intrinsic reasons, values, and motivations for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Fosters partnership by asking permission and letting the patient choose strategies and follow-up, though the heavy provision of recommendations reduces sense of equal power-sharing at times.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits some change talk and small commitments (delay, lines, walks) but does not consistently reflect, deepen, or selectively amplify the patient's own change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and normalizes ambivalence which helps, but misses opportunities for double-sided reflections or summaries to strategically soften sustain talk before moving to advice.
