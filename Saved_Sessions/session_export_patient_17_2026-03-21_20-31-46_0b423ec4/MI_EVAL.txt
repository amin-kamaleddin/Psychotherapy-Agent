# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and warmth, validated the client's experience, and honored her preferences (e.g., excluding her daughter), demonstrating a reliably accepting stance.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, affect-focused reflections and perspective-taking throughout the session, deepening the dialogue and guiding questions rather than just repeating content.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained clear, consistent focus on smoking cessation and moved logically from exploration to concrete planning (triggers, NRT, quit date, relapse plan) without being prescriptive.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the work as a partnership by soliciting preferences, co-constructing the plan, checking before offering tools, and positioning the client as an active contributor to decisions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly supported choice and control by offering options, asking permission before suggestions, and honoring the client's decisions (e.g., who to involve), reinforcing her ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited motivation using scaling and past-success questioning, but could further strengthen evocation by prompting more client-generated coping ideas and eliciting clearer commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the client as an equal collaborator, offering options, asking which next step felt most helpful, and tailoring plans together to share power over session direction.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk (importance/confidence, reasons, prior successes) but missed some opportunities to amplify and summarize change statements and to elicit specific commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and normalized ambivalence while redirecting toward planning, yet could have used more double-sided reflections and strategic pivots to further reduce sustain talk.
