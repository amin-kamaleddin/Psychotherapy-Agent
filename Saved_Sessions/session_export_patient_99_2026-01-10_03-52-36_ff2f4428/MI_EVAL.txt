# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, normalizing ambivalence and validating Matteo's life context. Avoided blame and treated his answers as reasonable throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Used solid reflective listening and perspective-taking that matched Matteo's disclosures and helped him reflect. Could deepen empathy by probing emotional reactions to the injury/screening and using more complex reflections to draw out values.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on alcohol use and its relation to the sprain/screening, moving from ambivalence to concrete planning. Resisted drifting and repeatedly tied discussion back to the target behavior.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was a genuine back-and-forth with shared decision-making; the clinician offered suggestions only after soliciting Matteo's preferences. The plan was co‑constructed and shaped by Matteo's choices.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently emphasized choice, offered options rather than directives, and validated Matteo's authority in selecting what fits his life. Even suggested wording was adapted and left for Matteo to choose.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out Matteo's motivations and self‑authored strategies, producing concrete change talk and a plan. Could be strengthened by amplifying expressed motivations, linking changes to personal values, and eliciting a clear commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a collaborative stance with shared power and frequent invitations for Matteo to choose and rehearse options. Language and structure signaled equal partners working together rather than an expert-driven directive.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Actively elicited and reinforced change talk using importance/confidence scales and planning, which led to specific behavior commitments. Could further increase strength and frequency by selectively reflecting and summarizing change statements to amplify commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and neutrally acknowledged sustain talk (social benefits), but made limited active efforts to soften it. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections, reframing of social pressures, and explicit pivots toward change.
