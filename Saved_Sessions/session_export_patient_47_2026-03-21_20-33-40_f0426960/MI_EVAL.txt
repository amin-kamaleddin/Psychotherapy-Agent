# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental language, normalizing ambivalence and affirming the patient's planning and insight throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Demonstrated solid reflective listening and perspective-taking with pragmatic summaries, but reflections tended to focus on behavior rather than deeper emotions or affective meaning.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on alcohol use and behavior change, eliciting a concrete first-step goal, operationalizing strategies, and attending to related medical safety.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as a joint effort by soliciting preferences for strategies and follow-up, mirroring plans to confirm shared understanding, and negotiating the check-in content.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Strongly supported patient choice by explicitly empowering decisions, asking permission before offering ideas, and presenting multiple options rather than directives.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively drew out change ideas, readiness, and personal motivators (son's concern, fall) but could further probe values and long‑term goals to deepen commitment and confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Adopted a collaborative stance throughout, repeatedly deferring to the patient, co-creating the plan, and ensuring the patient's choices shaped the session and follow-up.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced concrete change talk and used a readiness scale, yet missed some opportunities to more frequently reflect and amplify the patient's own reasons for change and link them to values.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and gently reframed ambivalence with normalization and manageable strategies, though could use more double-sided reflections to strategically highlight discrepancies.
