# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard through validation and permissive language, avoiding blame or judgment and preserving the client's dignity.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, sustained reflective listening that mirrored feelings and physical sensations, validating the client and encouraging disclosure; occasional deeper emotional naming could add nuance.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear focus on overcoming avoidance and building confidence, tying interventions to the target behavior and using scaling and follow-up to keep the session goal‑directed.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction read as genuine collaboration with repeated invitations for client input and co‑creation of concrete tools, and the clinician followed the client's pace rather than imposing solutions.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Actively supported choice using permission language, offering options framed as experiments and emphasizing the client's control over the plan and decisions.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicited proximal motivation and translated it into concrete experiments, but could deepen evocation by exploring broader values and eliciting stronger commitment language.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Session was largely collaborative and power‑sharing, though the clinician offered multiple concrete techniques in sequence rather than eliciting more of the client's own solutions.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk and helped convert it into a plan, but missed some opportunities to amplify spontaneous change statements and deepen commitment with follow‑up reflections or questions.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Handled sustain talk well by normalizing skepticism and reframing techniques as experiments, though more frequent double‑sided reflections could further validate ambivalence while highlighting discrepancy.
