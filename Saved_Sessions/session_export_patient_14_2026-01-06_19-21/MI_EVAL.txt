# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently nonjudgmental and respectful, validating frustrations and practical constraints which kept the patient engaged. Demonstrated strong unconditional positive regard throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Showed good perspective-taking and supportive statements, acknowledging irritation and time pressure. Could have used deeper, more elaborative reflections of emotions and ambivalence before moving quickly to solutions.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused agenda on weight-related behaviors with concrete, measurable goals and appropriate safety advice. Progressed logically from problem identification to a specific one-week plan and check-in.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Interaction was genuinely collaborative, repeatedly soliciting the patient's input and integrating his preferences into templates and the plan. Clinician alternated offering options and following the patient's lead.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently supported choice by offering options, adapting to refusals, and presenting the plan as a proposal the patient could modify. Reinforced patient ownership by using his wording for reminders and logs.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively translated brief change statements into actionable steps and elicited a real-world commitment. Missed opportunities to draw out deeper internal reasons, values, and confidence that would strengthen sustained motivation.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated strong partnership by co-constructing goals, asking permission, and using the patient's exact language in plans and logs. Power-sharing was evident in how decisions and wording were decided jointly.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced concrete change talk with a feasible, tailored plan and praise for actions. Could have more systematically probed desire, reasons, need, and confidence to deepen and extend change talk.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided reinforcing sustain talk and redirected barriers into time-efficient options that minimized resistance. Would benefit from more double-sided reflections and strategic rolling with resistance to further integrate sustain arguments.
