# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and normalized Seamus's position with frequent, genuine‑seeming acceptance statements and no judgment. Validated his priorities and avoided pressure throughout the encounter.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Used accurate, timely, and specific reflections that summarized ambivalence and mirrored Seamus's feelings, calming resistance. Reflections advanced understanding rather than parroting, supporting strong empathic attunement.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Kept a clear, clinically appropriate focus on making dental care doable rather than pressing for smoking cessation, maintaining useful behavioral goals. This approach reduced pressure but limited direct movement toward quitting from an MI target‑specific view.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Co‑constructed a practical plan with Seamus, solicited his exact wording for the record, and turned his preferences into actionable steps for the team. Demonstrated genuine teamwork by honoring and implementing the patient's input.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly reinforced Seamus's right to choose, permitted deferring discussion of quitting, and offered passive resources rather than pressuring for change. Asked for preferred phrasing and respected his decisions, supporting autonomy strongly.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Effectively evoked Seamus's solutions for appointment logistics and use of supports but did not substantially elicit his internal motives or reasons for changing smoking behavior. Missed opportunities to draw out desire, ability, or values related to quitting.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Framed the interaction as collaborative, deferred to Seamus's expertise, and invited his solutions, thereby sharing power in decision making. Co‑created the appointment plan with multiple invitations for his input and confirmation.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Focused primarily on logistics and practical steps rather than eliciting or strengthening language in favor of quitting; did not pursue Seamus's statements about past quitting experiences. Several opportunities to ask focused evocation questions and amplify pro‑change statements were missed.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoided arguing and skillfully downgraded pressure with reflections and neutral acceptance, which reduced defensiveness. Could have used more advanced techniques (e.g., double‑sided reflections or selective amplification) to more artfully redirect sustain talk toward change.
