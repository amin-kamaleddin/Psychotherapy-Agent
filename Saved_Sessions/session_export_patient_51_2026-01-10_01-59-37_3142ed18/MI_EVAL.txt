# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently respectful and nonjudgmental with repeated validation and supportive reassurance; no blaming or minimising of the patient's concerns.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Displayed basic empathy by acknowledging safety and the patient's time constraint and affirming her plan, but offered limited reflective exploration of her feelings or broader concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, focused emphasis on medication safety and logistics, providing concrete washout, taper, and emergency action steps throughout the encounter.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some collaborative moves (opening question, invitations to follow up) were present, but the exchange was mainly clinician-directed with limited co‑construction of the plan.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Supported patient autonomy by advising consultation with the prescriber, offering options for when to call, and inviting follow‑up, though it could have more explicitly affirmed patient choice or asked permission before advice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 2
**Comments:** Elicited very little internal motivation and relied on the patient's spontaneous commitments without using open questions or reflections to draw out reasons, values, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Opened collaboratively but shifted to expert-led guidance; limited elicitation of the patient's expertise or shared decision-making reduced consistent partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Reinforced the patient's stated intentions with brief affirmations, but did not systematically elicit or deepen desire, ability, reasons, or commitment language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Avoided amplifying sustain talk by being concise and pragmatic around time constraints, but did not use strategic reflections or double-sided statements to explore or soften ambivalence.
