# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician opens respectfully and validates Malik, but acceptance is undermined by intermittent lecturing and moralizing demands (e.g., asking for a promise), making the stance inconsistent.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are accurate reflections that build rapport and capture Malik’s perspective, but empathic listening is interrupted by confrontational, nonreflective statements, so empathy is uneven.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** The session stays focused on cannabis use, safety, and school impact and moves to a concrete harm-reduction agreement, though confrontational moments slightly risked engagement.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician invites input, role-play, and co-creation of an agreement, but repeatedly shifts into an expert/parental voice that reduces true shared decision-making.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Options and harm-reduction alternatives are offered and the clinician asks what Malik wants, yet pressuring language (demanding promises) undermines perceived choice.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Some client-generated solutions and open questions appear, but much of the work relies on advice/scripts rather than systematically eliciting Malik’s own values, reasons, or confidence.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** There are collaborative offers (scripts, drafting an agreement) that treat Malik as a partner, but authority-driven lecturing and role-playing the parent weaken consistent partnership.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Open questions and harm-reduction prompts produced limited change talk and a negotiated plan, but the clinician missed multiple opportunities to elicit and amplify Malik’s intrinsic reasons or use importance/confidence scaling.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** The clinician sometimes reframes sustain talk and offers compromise, yet direct challenges and demands at moments reinforced defensiveness instead of consistently softening sustain talk.
