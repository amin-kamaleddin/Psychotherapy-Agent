# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently conveys unconditional positive regard through validating language and honoring boundaries; normalizes feelings and avoids judgment throughout the session.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Uses accurate, empathic reflections that lower resistance, but reflections are sometimes brief or formulaic and miss opportunities to explore deeper affect or underlying concerns.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintains clear focus on coping and safety planning, repeatedly narrowing to actionable implementation details and avoiding drift into unrelated topics.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Truly co-constructs the plan by offering options, checking preferences, and following the client’s lead on how much technical help and support she wants.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Explicitly and repeatedly affirms the client's control (e.g., 'You’re in charge here') and honors refusals, which fosters strong client ownership of the plan.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 3
**Comments:** Elicits concrete behavioral commitments and practical reasons for safety but does not sufficiently draw out intrinsic motivation, values, or confidence about change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrates a collaborative stance by asking permission, offering choices, and sharing power so the client drives decisions and priorities.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Effectively elicits and reinforces concrete change talk around specific strategies and commitments, but could more directly elicit desire, reasons, and confidence language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Avoids arguing and reframes resistance to minimize sustain talk, though could further use double-sided reflections or summaries to acknowledge ambivalence more strategically.
