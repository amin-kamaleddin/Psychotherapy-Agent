# Motivational Interviewing Clinical Evaluation – MI

---

### Acceptance
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently communicated unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental support, using affirmations and normalization throughout the interaction.

### Empathy
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate, timely reflections and perspective-taking, matching Elias’s emotions and asking compassionate safety checks without judgment.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, appropriate focus on safety and relapse prevention, prioritizing immediate risk and concrete short-term goals like 'get through tonight.'

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Consistently treated the interaction as joint problem-solving—co-created the safety plan and checklist, offered options, and incorporated Elias’s preferences.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly offered choices, asked permission before interventions, and deliberately postponed pressure for large decisions to respect Elias’s control.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited some internal motivation (kids reminder, progress, tiny goals) but at times relied on offering strategies rather than systematically drawing out Elias’s own reasons, values, and commitment.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Acted as a collaborative partner by inviting preferences, letting Elias tweak plans and scripts, and staying engaged during outreach to supports.

### Cultivating Change Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Elicited and reinforced change talk (highlighting progress, setting tiny goals) but missed opportunities to use more open questions and amplifications to lengthen and intensify change language.

### Softening Sustain Talk
#### **Score (1–5):** 4
**Comments:** Generally avoided reinforcing sustain talk and neutrally acknowledged ambivalence, though could have used more double-sided reflections to capture both sustain and change before pivoting.
