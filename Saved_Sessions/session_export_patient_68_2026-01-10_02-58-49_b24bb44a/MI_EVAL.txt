### Acceptance

**Comments:** Consistently conveyed unconditional positive regard and nonjudgmental acceptance, validating Kevin's embarrassment and choices throughout; no shaming or punitive remarks.

### Empathy

**Comments:** Demonstrated accurate reflective listening and perspective-taking, picking up on embarrassment and coping functions, but could deepen emotional reflection beyond surface-level paraphrase to explore affective texture.

### Direction
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Maintained a clear, consistent focus on drinking and risk reduction, progressing logically from scaling to a concrete, time-bound harm-reduction plan while avoiding tangents.

### Collaboration
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Session functioned as joint problem-solving with inclusive language and co-creation of the plan; the clinician consistently sought Kevin's input at decision points.

### Autonomy Support
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Repeatedly affirmed Kevin's choice and offered options rather than directives, supporting smaller self-chosen changes and avoiding pressure to quit entirely.

### Evocation
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
**Comments:** Effectively elicited Kevin's own motivations and solutions using scaling and open prompts, building on his desire, reasons, and plans rather than supplying reasons for change.

### Partnership
#### **Score (1–5):** 5
